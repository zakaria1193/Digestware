The `DEBUG_SOLVER` macro used for debugging the path solver in GCC has been moved to a header file and renamed to `DEBUG_PATH_RANGER`. This change prevents potential naming conflicts with other debugging macros and sets the stage for a future patch that will introduce a new use for `DEBUG_PATH_RANGER`.

This improves the internal organization and clarity of debugging infrastructure within the compiler.
