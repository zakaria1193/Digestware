This commit strengthens the RISC-V bit manipulation instruction selection by replacing overly permissive operand matchers with stricter ones. It changes `immediate_operand` and `arith_operand` to `const_int_operand` and `reg_operand` respectively, preventing the compiler from incorrectly attempting to use immediate values or registers in contexts where only constant integers are valid. This resolves potential ICEs and unexpected behavior.
