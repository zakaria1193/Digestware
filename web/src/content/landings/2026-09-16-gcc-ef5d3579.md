This change prevents `VREL_UNDEFINED` from being recorded in relation chains within GCC's value analysis. Recording an undefined relation typically indicates an unreachable block, and including it could negatively impact subsequent searches and analyses. The modification ensures that only valid, defined relations are tracked, improving the robustness of optimizations.
