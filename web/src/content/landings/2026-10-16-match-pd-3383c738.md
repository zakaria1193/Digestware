The GCC optimization pattern for folding shift-and-mask operations like `(X >> C1) & C2` was previously limited to types with precision up to `HOST_BITS_PER_WIDE_INT`. This prevented the optimization from being applied to wider types such as `__int128`. This commit updates the pattern to use `wide_int` for its internal calculations, removing the precision limit and allowing the folding to occur on larger integer types. New tests are included to verify the fix.
