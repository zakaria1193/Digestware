This commit removes an optimization pass on RISC-V RV64 that aimed to replace sequences like `addi+and` with `ctz+bclr` for bit clearing operations. The optimization, intended to reduce instruction count, incorrectly handled 32-bit operations on 64-bit registers and produced incorrect sign extensions in some cases. By removing the problematic part of the pass, the compiler now correctly rejects this optimization when it cannot be safely applied, improving correctness for 32-bit operations on RV64.
