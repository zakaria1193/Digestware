The HPPA backend has introduced an alternative instruction sequence (`ldil`/`addl`) to load the high part of symbolic operands, reducing pressure on the `r1` register. This change aims to prevent compilation failures caused by the lack of available registers for spilling, offering a less costly alternative than spilling `r1` itself. A note is included regarding a limitation with PIC code linking into non-PIC executables.
