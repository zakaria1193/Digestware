This commit addresses an issue in Rust-GCC where the parser would silently drop input following an incomplete `pub` declaration, such as `pub <`. Previously, this could lead to unexpected compilation behavior. The fix ensures that the parser now emits an "expected item" error in such cases, improving compiler robustness.
