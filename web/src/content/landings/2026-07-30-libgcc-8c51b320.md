On aarch64, the `.aeabi_subsection` directive was incorrectly used, causing the subsequent `.previous` directive to switch to the wrong ELF section. This commit removes `.previous` from the build-attribute marking path, ensuring it only applies where intended in the `GNU_PROPERTY` path, thus fixing a potential assembly issue.
