This commit refactors the `ix86_expand_ace_builtin` function on the i386 architecture to unify the predicate check for the `tmm` register. It revises the logic to avoid an unnecessary `INTVAL` check when operands are not constant, resolving a bug reported in PR127264. A new test case is included to verify the fix.
