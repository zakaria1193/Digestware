The `vect_maybe_permute_loop_masks` function in GCC's vectorizer has been refactored to be optional and retargetable. This change allows it to determine the feasibility of a permutation based solely on loop masks without generating code, and permits overriding controls for source and target operands. This enhances flexibility for target-specific vectorization adaptations.
