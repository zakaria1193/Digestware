This commit corrects a bug in the microblaze linker's section relaxation process that caused an out-of-bounds read from the local symbol cache. The issue stemmed from a copied function missing a bounds check present in the original. This error could silently corrupt relocation addends, leading to miscompilations, particularly affecting structure member accesses after optimization. The fix restores the missing bounds check and adds new test cases to verify relocation addend integrity during relaxation.
