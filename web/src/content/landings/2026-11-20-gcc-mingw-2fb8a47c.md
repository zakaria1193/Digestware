This change clarifies the handling of the SPEC_PTHREAD2 macro within the mingw compiler driver's specifications. An empty spec expansion is generally not useful, but GCC requires certain specs to be referenced to avoid driver rejections. The update adds a note explaining why SPEC_PTHREAD2 is kept in LIB_SPEC and removes it from CPP_SPEC, streamlining the configuration.
