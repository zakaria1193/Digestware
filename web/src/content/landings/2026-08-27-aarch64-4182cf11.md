This commit introduces an optimization for the AArch64 architecture that recognizes specific vector permute patterns, such as those generated by `__builtin_shuffle`, and transforms them into a sequence of `REV64` and `EXT` instructions. This new optimization can significantly improve performance by replacing a less efficient table-lookup (`tbl`) instruction with a more streamlined two-instruction sequence. The optimization is intelligently applied only when the pattern does not occur within a loop, preventing potential performance degradation in loop-heavy code.
