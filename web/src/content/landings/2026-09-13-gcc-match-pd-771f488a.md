This commit introduces new patterns to GCC's `match.pd` that optimize expressions of the form `A - MIN (B, C)` and `A - MAX (B, C)` into additions with negated max/min respectively. This transformation, `A - MIN(B, C) -> A + MAX(-B, -C)`, requires that B and C are negatable and accounts for potential overflow in wrapping types, leading to more efficient code generation.
