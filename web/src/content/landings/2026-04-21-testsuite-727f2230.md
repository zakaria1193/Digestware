The Multiple Precision Complex (MPC) library version 1.4.0 changed how signed zeros are handled in the `cpow` function. This change caused failures in the `builtin-math-6.c` test within GCC's testsuite. This commit updates the test to reflect the new behavior of MPC 1.4.0, specifically regarding the sign of the imaginary zero part of the result.
