This commit updates the `builtin-math-6.c` test case to account for changes in how MPC (Multiple Precision Complex library) 1.4 handles signed zeros in the results of complex power (`cpow`) calculations. The changes in MPC 1.4.0 broke the test. The commit adjusts the expected results in the test to align with the new MPC behavior.
