The cost calculation for multiplication operations on the i386 architecture has been corrected to properly distinguish between `MULT_EXPR` and `MULT_HIGHPART_EXPR`. Previously, a change intended for high-part multiplication erroneously affected the cost of regular `MULT_EXPR`, hindering vectorization. This fix ensures accurate cost modeling, allowing the compiler to make better decisions about vectorizing multiplication operations.
