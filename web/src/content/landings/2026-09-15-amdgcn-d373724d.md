The AMD GCN backend in GCC has been updated to allow scalars residing in Vector General Purpose Registers (VGPRs) to be spilled into either Scalar General Purpose Registers (SGPRs) or Additional VGPRs (AVGPRs). This change addresses performance regressions observed when scalars were exclusively spilled to SGPRs, by providing flexibility in register allocation. The new approach improves performance across a range of benchmarks by better managing register pressure.
