An Integer Compiler Error (ICE) occurring when optimizing expressions like `(T)0.0 - x` with the `-ffinite-math-only` flag has been resolved. The issue stemmed from an incorrect assumption about how GCC handles negative zero in floating-point constants. A new predicate, `real_negzerop`, has been introduced to robustly identify negative zero across various constant types (real, vector, complex) and prevent the ICE.
