This change modifies internal-fn.cc to prevent an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) during the expansion of CLMUL intrinsics. Previously, when the left-hand side of these intrinsics was unused and not optimized out (e.g., at -O0), the compiler would attempt to expand a NULL_TREE, leading to a crash. The fix ensures that if the left-hand side is NULL_TREE, the expansion is skipped, aligning with the handling of other ECF_CONST intrinsics.
