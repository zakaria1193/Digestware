The riscv linker can now correctly relocate IFUNC (indirect function) symbol references originating from non-allocatable sections, such as .debug_info. Previously, the linker skipped these sections, causing relocation errors and toolchain aborts when only such sections referred to an IFUNC symbol. This change ensures these references are treated as STT_FUNC, allowing the linker to resolve them to the IFUNC's resolver function.
