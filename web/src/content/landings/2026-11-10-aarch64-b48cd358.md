This patch enables the AArch64 vectorizer to recognize and utilize floating-point dot product operations. It connects existing AdvSIMD and SVE FDOT instruction patterns to the `sdot_prod` optab, allowing loops that perform dot products to be compiled more efficiently. For example, a loop calculating `sum += a[i] * b[i]` can now use single FDOT instructions to multiply and accumulate pairs of half-precision inputs into single-precision elements, avoiding intermediate widening and separate multiply-accumulate steps.
