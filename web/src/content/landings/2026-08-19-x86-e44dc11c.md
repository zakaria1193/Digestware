The GCC compiler's expansion for the `truncsfbf2` intrinsic on x86 has been updated to mirror the behavior of the `vcvtneps2bf16` instruction. This change ensures that input denormal floating-point numbers are flushed to zero, SNANs are quietly converted to QNaNs, and rounding consistently uses the 'nearest even' mode. This improves consistency across floating-point conversion operations.
