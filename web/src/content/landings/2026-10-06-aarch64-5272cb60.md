Floating-point pairwise addition (FADDP) on AArch64 previously used a custom UNSPEC_FADDV, preventing RTL optimizations from recognizing its behavior. This commit modifies FADDP to use standard RTL patterns, similar to integer ADDP, allowing optimizations like combine to recognize and improve FADDP usage. This leads to more efficient code, for example, by packing two scalar FADDP reductions into a single vector FADDP instruction.
