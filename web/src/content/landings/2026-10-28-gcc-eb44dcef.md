This commit updates the `redundant_insn` function in `reorg.cc` to accept an `rtx_insn *` argument. Additionally, `relax_delay_slots` is modified to maintain the next real instruction in an `rtx_insn *`. This change enhances the precision with which redundant instructions are identified and handled, ensuring that context is maintained correctly during instruction reordering and optimization passes.
