This commit ports several AArch64 NEON bit manipulation and logical operation intrinsics, including `vand`, `vbic`, `vbsl`, `veor`, `vmvn`, `vorn`, `vorr`, `vrbit`, and others, to GCC's pragma-based framework. This transition simplifies the internal handling of these SIMD operations, consolidating their implementation and improving maintainability. Obsolete unspec definitions and builtin functions related to these intrinsics have been removed.
