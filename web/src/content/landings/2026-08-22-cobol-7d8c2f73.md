The Cobol compiler now supports expressions in BY CONTENT parameters, controlled by the new `-Wcontent-expr` option. This option, added to the IBM dialect, acts as a superset of IBM syntax, allowing constructs like ADDRESS OF and LENGTH OF expressions. This change addresses RT 3611 by relaxing ISO prohibitions on expressions in BY CONTENT parameters when a prototype is not present.
