A bug in GCC's Fortran front-end could lead to a double-free error when using `ASSOCIATE` or `SELECT TYPE` with polymorphic array selectors. Upon exiting these blocks, the compiler incorrectly copied back the original array descriptor, even after the selector had been deallocated. This commit corrects the behavior by ensuring this copy-back only occurs for `SELECT RANK` constructs, which can have dynamically allocated or pointer-assigned selectors. Other `ASSOCIATE` and `SELECT TYPE` constructs, where the selector is not `POINTER` or `ALLOCATABLE`, do not require this copy-back.
