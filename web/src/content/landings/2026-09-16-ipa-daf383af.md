This patch modifies IPA passes to accommodate internal `assume` bodies, which are purely for optimization and do not appear in the final binary. Heuristics in IPA passes often exclude these bodies, hindering optimization. This commit introduces bypasses for checks that would prevent optimizations like cloning or parameter splitting, ensuring that these optimization-specific constructs are processed more effectively. This aims to maximize the benefit of these internal optimization tools.
