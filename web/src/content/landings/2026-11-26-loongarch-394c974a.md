This commit fixes a bug in the LoongArch assembler (GAS) concerning Thread-Local Storage (TLS) LE (Local Exec) relaxation. When the '-mno-relax' option was used, the assembler incorrectly emitted relaxation markers for parts of the TLS LE sequence. This could lead the linker to optimize incorrectly by removing instructions, resulting in a wrong TLS address. The fix ensures that the relaxation marker for the `le_add_r` instruction is only emitted when linker relaxation is actually enabled.
