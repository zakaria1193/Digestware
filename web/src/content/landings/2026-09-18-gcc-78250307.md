This commit adds an assertion in `vect_build_slp_tree` to verify that nodes discovered by SLP (Simultaneous Loop Parallelization) are not marked as needing to be split. This change ensures that the SLP discovery cache remains consistent and prevents issues arising from nodes that were not properly soft-failed during discovery.
