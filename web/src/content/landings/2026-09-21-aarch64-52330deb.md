The AArch64 backend now supports `__builtin_isinf`, `__builtin_isfinite`, and `__builtin_isnan` for the 16-bit floating-point formats `HFmode` (_Float16) and `BFmode` (__bf16). Previously, these built-ins were limited to single (SFmode) and double (DFmode) precision, forcing classification through less accurate floating-point comparisons for smaller types. The new implementation uses integer arithmetic on a zero-extended representation of the floating-point values.
