A selftest for the `test_output_operand_modifier_operand` in GCC's middle-end was found to cause an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) during bootstrap on targets where QI and HI types are not suitable for subregister operations. This change removes the subregister from the selftest, resolving the bootstrap issue and allowing the test to pass on all targets.
