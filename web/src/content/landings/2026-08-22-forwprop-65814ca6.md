In `tree-ssa-forwprop.cc`, the `match_long_mul_phi` function has been updated to reorder its conditional checks. The check for the shift amount equality (`shift_amt == halfwidth`) is now performed before the more computationally expensive `gimple_mul_low_sum` and `gimple_mul_cross_sum` calls. This optimization can lead to faster compilation by avoiding unnecessary function calls when the shift amount condition is not met.
