Documentation for s390 instruction formats has been enhanced with improved ASCII-art formatting for better readability and scalability. Additionally, specific details regarding operand descriptions, instruction lengths, and field bit positions have been corrected for RIS and RSI instruction formats. The changes ensure consistent width for instructions of the same byte size and accurately reflect the specifications in the Principles of Operation.
