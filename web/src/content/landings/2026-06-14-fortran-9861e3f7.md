This GCC Fortran commit ensures that variables appearing in the references of a variable definition context, such as the `i` in `a(i) = 42`, are correctly marked as used. Previously, these variables were not marked, leading to false-positive `-Wunused-but-set-variable` warnings. The patch also refactors `gfc_value_set_at` by removing a default argument and introduces `gfc_expr_set_at` to provide more precise information to callers, enhancing warning accuracy.
