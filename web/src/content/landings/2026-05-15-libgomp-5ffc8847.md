This patch introduces two new scalable barrier implementations, 'flat' and 'tree', optimized for the `linux/futex_waitv` target, leveraging the futex_waitv syscall from Linux 5.16. These barriers offer improved performance, with flat barriers typically better for active waiting and tree barriers for passive waiting. The choice of barrier can be influenced by wait policy (`OMP_WAIT_POLICY`) and new environment variables (`GOMP_BARRIER_TYPE`, `GOMP_FORKJOIN_BARRIER_TYPE`), allowing users to tune synchronization behavior. A centralized barrier is also retained for specific use cases.
