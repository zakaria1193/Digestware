This commit addresses a bootstrap failure encountered during the RISC-V native build process. An unannotated fallthrough in the `shuffle_bswap_pattern` function, introduced by a previous commit, caused the compiler to issue warnings that were treated as errors. The fix annotates the intentional fallthrough to prevent this issue.
