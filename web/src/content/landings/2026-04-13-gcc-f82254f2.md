The vectorizer now creates points-to information from non-pointer accesses, similar to how `copy_ref_info` works. This improves the accuracy of points-to analysis after vectorization.  The changes mainly involve modifications to `tree-vect-data-refs.cc` to handle the creation and adjustment of points-to information for decl-based accesses during vectorization. This enhancement helps the compiler better understand memory dependencies, potentially leading to further optimization opportunities.
