This commit extends the vectorizer's capability to support an additional form of complex fused multiply-subtract (FMS) operations. Specifically, it enables matching FMS where reassocations result in the top-level operation being a PLUS_MINUS, occurring when the accumulator is different from the destination. This support is crucial for optimizing code that performs complex arithmetic, such as `c.real + (a.imag * b.imag - a.real * b.real)`, especially when the accumulator is not the same as the destination register.
