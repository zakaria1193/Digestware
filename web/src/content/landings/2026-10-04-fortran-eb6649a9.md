This commit addresses an issue in GFortran where pointer function results with spans larger than element sizes were incorrectly handled. Callers often ignored this span, leading to incorrect indexing of temporary results. The patch ensures that temporary results are marked with `GFC_DECL_PTR_ARRAY_P`, associate names and pointers are flagged as subreference arrays, and actual arguments are passed correctly, including making packed copies when the span differs from the element size. This resolves PR127731.
