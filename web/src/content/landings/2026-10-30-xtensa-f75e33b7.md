The Xtensa target's TARGET_SECONDARY_RELOAD hook previously had inaccurate register class checks. This patch replaces the direct comparison of register classes with GR_REGS or RL_REGS with a call to reg_class_subset_p(). This ensures the hook correctly evaluates subclasses of GR_REGS, improving accuracy for the Xtensa architecture.
