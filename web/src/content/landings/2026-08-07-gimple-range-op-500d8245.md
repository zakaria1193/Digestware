The `gimple-range-op` pass has been updated to correctly handle the precision of square root operations, particularly for glibc's `sqrt` precision hook. Previously, the widening step (adding or subtracting half an ulp) was not applied consistently for reverse operations, leading to inaccurate ranges. This change ensures that the `widen` method is used, providing a more robust and precise range analysis for square roots.
