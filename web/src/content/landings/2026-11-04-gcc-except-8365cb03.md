The `for_each_eh_label` function in `gcc/except.h` and `gcc/except.cc` now accepts an `rtx_code_label *` parameter for its callback. This change improves type safety by ensuring that the callback receives a specific label type. Additionally, `set_initial_eh_label_offset` in `reload1.cc` is updated to also take an `rtx_code_label *`.
