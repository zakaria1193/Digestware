Support for the Toshiba TX49 processor has been added to Binutils. This includes handling its MIPS III ISA with TX39 extensions, specific three-operand DMULT/DMULTU instructions, and the MIPS IV PREF instruction. The changes also address pipeline interlocks for division instructions and multiply-add instructions, ensuring correct operation with HI/LO registers.
