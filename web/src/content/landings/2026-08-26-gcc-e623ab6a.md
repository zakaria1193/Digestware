Test cases gcc.target/i386/pr89523-1a.c and gcc.target/i386/pr89523-1b.c are adjusted by removing scans for addr32 vgather instructions. Due to invariant load hoisting from unswitched loop bodies, the use of a gather is no longer necessary, and the tests are modified to reflect this.
