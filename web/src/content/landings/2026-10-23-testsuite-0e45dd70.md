A test case in the GCC test suite (`forwprop-vec-cond-float.c`) was failing on ARM with Neon. The issue stemmed from incorrectly checking the sinking of a floating-point conversion (`FLOAT_EXPR`). The fix restricts this check to targets that strictly support floating-point vector operations (`vect_float_strict`), preventing the compiler from sinking conversions that would prevent the expansion of vector conditional selects.
