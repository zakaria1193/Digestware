This change extends the support for register overlap to the RISC-V vwmaccsu.vx instruction. By leveraging the `Wvr` constraint in the vector-masking table, this modification aligns the behavior of `vwmaccsu.vx` with similar instructions like `vwmacc[u].vx`, allowing for more flexible and potentially optimized register usage.
