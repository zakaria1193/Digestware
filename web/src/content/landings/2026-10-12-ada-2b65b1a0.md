This commit addresses an issue with generating DWARF debug information for Ada derived types that have variant parts. It works around a DWARF limitation where the discriminant attribute must reference a member DIE of the same record type. The fix ensures that even when a discriminant is a sub-component of a parent type, its information is correctly captured in the DWARF output.
