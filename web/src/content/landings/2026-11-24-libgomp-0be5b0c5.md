This commit fixes how libgomp handles `GOMP_RUNTIME_USM=auto` for nvptx devices. Previously, an incorrect 'is-integrated' check prevented USM auto-detection. The logic has been updated to correctly identify integrated devices and to check USM accessibility on a per-device basis within `GOMP_OFFLOAD_is_accessible_ptr`, ensuring USM works as expected.
