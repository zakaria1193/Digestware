This change fixes two issues in the HPPA ELF linker's handling of relocations for `ldil` and `addl` instruction sequences. It corrects the bit manipulation used to extract the target address from the `ldil` instruction and ensures that the relocation conversion from `R_PARISC_DLTIND21L` to `R_PARISC_DPREL21L` correctly accepts any base register, addressing cases where GCC emits different base registers under LRA.
