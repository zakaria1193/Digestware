The libbacktrace library now reports the declaration line number for inlined functions, enhancing debugging information. This change adds a new field `backtrace_decl_lineno` to the `backtrace_moredata` structure and updates DWARF parsing logic to extract and provide this information. The corresponding test cases in `mdtest.c` are adjusted to verify the correctness of this new data, making it easier to pinpoint the source of inlined code.
