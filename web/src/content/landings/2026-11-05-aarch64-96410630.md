A test case for ARM64 floating-point multiplication intrinsics (`fmul_intrinsic_1.c`) was failing due to an incorrect pointer offset calculation. This commit corrects the offset for the `in2` pointer when `in2_lanes` is less than 1, aligning the generated assembly with the test's expectations. The fix ensures the test accurately reflects the behavior of the intrinsic function.
