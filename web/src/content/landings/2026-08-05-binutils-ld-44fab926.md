The GNU linker's `fatal` function has been corrected to ensure that fatal errors are always treated as errors by the `vfinfo` reporting mechanism. Previously, a bug caused these critical messages to be misinterpreted as warnings, potentially obscuring their severity to the user. This change ensures fatal errors are properly flagged and exit with status 1.
