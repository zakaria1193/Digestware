This commit corrects instruction selection for SSE vector concatenation on x86. It fixes an incorrect condition in `vec_concatv2sf` and `vec_concatv2si` patterns, preventing them from being selected when SSE4.1 is available and the operands are not compatible with the SSE4.1 specific instructions. Additionally, the generic SSE patterns are now disabled when SSE4.1 is enabled to ensure the more appropriate SSE4.1 patterns are used.
