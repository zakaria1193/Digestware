This commit corrects the behavior of reversing comparison codes in GCC's middle-end, specifically for `LTGT` (less than or greater than) and `UNEQ` (not equal) operations when floating-point traps are enabled. `LTGT` is a signaling comparison that can raise `FE_INVALID` on NaN operands, while `UNEQ` is quiet. Unconditionally reversing `LTGT` into `UNEQ` (or vice versa) could incorrectly raise an exception. The fix ensures these reversals are avoided when floating-point exceptions are honored, and refactors the handling of `NE` comparisons to use `!UNEQ` instead of `LTGT` to avoid triggering signaling behavior for quiet comparisons.
