This commit removes obsolete autoconf macros AC_C_CONST and AC_C_INLINE from libiberty's build configuration. These checks are no longer necessary as modern C compilers, especially those capable of building GCC, are expected to fully support these fundamental language features. Binutils already requires C99, further diminishing the need for these checks.
