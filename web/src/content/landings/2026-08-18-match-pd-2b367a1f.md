This optimization simplifies pairs of single-bit tests on the same operand into a single masked comparison, reducing instruction count. For example, it transforms conditions like `(X & C1) != 0 & (X & C2) != 0` into `(X & M) == M`, where M is the combined mask. This is particularly effective on architectures like ARM, reducing sequences from 4-8 instructions to a single compare-and-branch instruction.
