A recent change that removed `AUTODETECT_VALUE` broke the interaction of the `-fvar-tracking-uninit` option. Previously, `-fvar-tracking-uninit` explicitly implied `-fvar-tracking`, and the option defaulted to enabled for optimization levels of 1 and above. The change restored the implication that `-fvar-tracking-uninit` enables `-fvar-tracking`, ensuring correct behavior for tracking uninitialized variables. It also re-establishes default behaviors that were inadvertently lost.
