This patch makes necessary adjustments to the IPA-SRA (Inter-Procedural Scalar Replacement) pass to correctly handle outlined `assume` bodies and callback edges. It modifies checks within functions that process internal calls and argument flows to accommodate `IFN_ASSUME` calls, ensuring correct index remapping. Additionally, `assume` bodies are special-cased regarding their return values, which should not be removed. The patch also includes checks to prevent optimization of `assume` bodies lacking callback edges and adjusts remapping for parameter descriptors and flows across callback edges.
