This commit refactors the s390 target's instruction selection for bitwise OR operations on word and SImode values. Previously, the generic optab expander favored PLUS over IOR, leading to less efficient code. By explicitly choosing IOR, the compiler can now generate more compact and faster instructions, such as `stc` for storing, instead of a sequence involving load, logical AND, addition, and store when PLUS was used.
