This commit addresses five distinct problem reports concerning the COBOL compiler. It enforces the requirement for a PICTURE clause with COMP-3 data types, rejects arithmetic operations on INDEX operands, and corrects syntax errors where numeric literals were treated as floating-point instead of integers. Additionally, it improves handling of MOVE statements involving alphanumeric data and allows programs and paragraphs to share the same name, enhancing compiler robustness and adherence to the COBOL standard.
