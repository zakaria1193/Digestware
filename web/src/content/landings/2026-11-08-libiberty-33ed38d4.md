The libiberty configure script has been simplified by removing logic that relied on `srcdir` being `.` (the current directory). Since `srcdir` is always passed suffixed with `/libiberty` by the top-level Makefile, this change streamlines the detection of `libiberty_topdir` and removes the need for `AC_CONFIG_AUX_DIR`.
