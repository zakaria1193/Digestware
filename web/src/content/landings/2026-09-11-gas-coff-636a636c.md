- term: .popsection
  definition: An assembler pseudo-op that restores the most recently saved section

The GNU Assembler (gas) now supports `.previous`, `.pushsection`, and `.popsection` pseudo-operations for COFF targets. This enables finer control over section placement and manipulation, crucial for tasks like compiling the Linux kernel for Windows-based systems (ReactOS).
