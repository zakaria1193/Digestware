This commit corrects a performance regression on Clearwater Forest processors by accurately costing the emulation of `umul_highpart` for `VnDI` (vector doubleword integer) operations. The previous cost estimation was too low, leading the vectorizer to incorrectly choose `V4DI` (vector quadword integer) which was slower than scalar code for the NTT loop in `750.sealcrypto_r`. The fix uses `mulss` for widening multiplies and applies `ix86_vec_cost` to the entire cost calculation, respecting target tuning flags like `X86_TUNE_AVX256_SPLIT_REGS` and improving vectorization efficiency.
