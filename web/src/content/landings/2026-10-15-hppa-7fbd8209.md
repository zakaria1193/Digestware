On hppa targets using TARGET_SOFT_FLOAT, floating-point registers are not used. This commit allows mode changes across different classes, which was previously restricted, as the absence of hardware floating-point support removes the previous constraints. This change is a minor adjustment to the scheduler's mode-switching logic.
