This change improves the GCC static analyzer's output by ensuring that redundant casts are removed from the representative tree used in diagnostic messages. Previously, after a fixup process, the analyzer could reintroduce casts if the defining statement was a conversion, leading to messages like `(unsigned int)(num * 16)` instead of the clearer `num * 16`. This commit strips casts again after the fixup, resolving this issue and making analyzer warnings more precise.
