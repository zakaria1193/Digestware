This commit enhances the RISC-V Vector extension test suite with new cases for the `vfwmacc.vv` instruction. The tests are designed to validate the instruction's behavior when register groups overlap, meaning destination and source registers may be shared. Scenarios for half-precision (f16) and single-precision (f32) floating-point data types are covered, with various vector configurations (m1, m2, m4, mf2, mf4) to ensure robustness.
