The IPA-ICF pass's `redirect_all_callers` function previously failed to redirect callers that appeared after a thunk in the call list. This was because it advanced to the thunk's *callee* instead of the *next caller* in the list. This change corrects the logic to ensure all callers are processed, preventing some function calls from being incorrectly left unredirected.
