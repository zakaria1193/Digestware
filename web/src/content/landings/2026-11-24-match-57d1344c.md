This commit enhances the GCC matching patterns to optimize expressions involving bitwise NOT and negation. It folds `~X op -Y` into `(Y-1) op X` by leveraging the identity `~(-Y) == Y - 1`. This transformation allows GCC to simplify various comparisons, such as turning unsigned `~a < -a` into `a != 0`, signed `~a < -a` into `true`, and `~a == -a` into `false`. The optimization is safely gated to avoid undefined behavior with signed integer overflow.
