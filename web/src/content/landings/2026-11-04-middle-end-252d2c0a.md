This patch addresses a corner case in constant folding related to floating-point arithmetic, specifically for the expression `x - x`. When using `-frounding-math` with `FE_DOWNWARD` rounding, the result of `x - x` (where `x` is finite and not NaN) should be `-0.0` to honor signed zeros, not `+0.0`. The compiler's constant folding in `fold-const.cc` and RTL optimizers in `simplify-rtx.cc` is updated to reflect this, preventing incorrect simplification to `+0.0`.
