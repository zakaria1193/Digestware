This commit introduces a suite of new test cases specifically designed to validate the handling of register group overlap for the RISC-V vector `vfwadd.wv` instruction. These tests ensure that narrow operands (`vs1`) do not incorrectly fall within the `vs2` register group, covering various floating-point formats and vector lengths.
