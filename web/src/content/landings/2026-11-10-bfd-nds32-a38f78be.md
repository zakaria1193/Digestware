This commit removes the unused `got_size` global variable from BFD's ELF handling for the NDS32 architecture. The variable has been obsolete since 2013. Its removal cleans up the code, resolves a compiler warning, and merges handling for `.got` and `.got.plt` sections into the `.plt` section's logic.
