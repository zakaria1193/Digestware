GCC re-enables several tests for the `__gnu_pbds` (Policy-Based Data Structures) library that were previously disabled for C++17 and later standards. These tests were disabled due to issues with ambiguous string conversions, believed to be related to LWG 2946 and fixed in subsequent compiler revisions. The tests now compile successfully with C++17, allowing for their regression testing.
