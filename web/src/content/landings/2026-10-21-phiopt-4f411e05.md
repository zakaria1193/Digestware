The `spaceship_replacement` function in `phiopt` incorrectly checked the number of successors for `cond2_bb` instead of `cond3_bb`. This could lead to an out-of-bounds read and an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) when `cond3_bb` had only one successor. This change corrects the check to use `cond3_bb`, resolving the ICE and passing a new test case.
