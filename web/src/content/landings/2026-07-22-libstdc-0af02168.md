The uniform_int_distribution in libstdc++ could enter an infinite loop when paired with certain random number engines that consistently produce zero. This was due to an algorithm change that rejected low outputs. This patch modifies the rejection path to alternate between the front and back of the output range, ensuring uniformity without causing hangs.
