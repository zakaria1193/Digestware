This commit addresses issues in the AArch64 backend where Intelligent Code Generation (ICE) errors occurred during the expansion of TImode patterns when subregisters were involved. Following a previous fix, additional cases were identified and resolved by ensuring that `gen_highpart` is not called on subregisters and by enforcing `pure_register_operand` constraints on destinations and operands in relevant `TI` mode operations.
