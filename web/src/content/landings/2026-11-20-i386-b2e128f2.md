On i386, the `-mrecord-mcount` option previously emitted `__mcount_loc` entries without alignment, leading to less efficient RELA relocations in Position Independent Executables (PIE). This change aligns these entries to their size, mirroring existing practices for `__patchable_function_entries`. This optimization significantly reduces the number of relocations required for PIE builds of the x86_64 Linux kernel.
