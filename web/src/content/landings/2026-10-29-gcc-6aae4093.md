This commit introduces support for the PowerPC XXEVAL instruction to optimize SHA3 calculations, addressing a performance regression observed in newer GCC versions compared to older ones. The optimization involves fusing specific vector `ANDC` and `XOR` operations when they are adjacent and feed into each other. This fusion is enabled by modifications to generated instruction patterns, particularly in `fusion.md`, allowing the compiler to leverage the specialized VANDC/VXOR adjacent to VXOR fusion mode available on Power10 processors, thereby reducing instruction spilling and improving performance.
