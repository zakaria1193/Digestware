A regression in the Ada compiler's analysis of reduction expressions, triggered by the -gnatVa flag, caused an infinite loop due to invalid tree sharing. This commit prevents the `Relocate_Node` function from being called on the original, unanalyzed tree, thus fixing the loop and the underlying invalid sharing.
