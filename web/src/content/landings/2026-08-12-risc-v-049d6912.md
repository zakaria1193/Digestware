GCC has gained the ability to recognize and fuse specific pairs of SLLI (shift left logical) and SRLI (shift right logical) instructions on RISC-V architectures. This optimization aims to combine these operations when they have the necessary register dependencies, potentially reducing instruction count and improving execution speed. The new fusion logic is disabled by default and includes new test cases.
