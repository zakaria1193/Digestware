The ARM backend's SIMD and FP16 builtin types were not being properly registered as GGC roots, leading to incorrect restoration when loading Precompiled Header (PCH) files. This caused inconsistencies and an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) in `arm_mve_type_for_scalar_type`. The fix adds necessary GTY annotations and registers relevant headers with `gengtype` to ensure these types are properly handled during PCH loading.
