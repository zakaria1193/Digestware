This commit restructures the `adjust_paths_after_duplication` function in the backthreader to separate the process of finding candidate paths from the process of adjusting them. The per-candidate work is moved into a new `adjust_one_path` function, simplifying the control flow, removing the use of `goto`, and making the logic for rewiring candidates clearer.
