This commit fixes a typo in the C preprocessor directives within the `match.pd` file. An `#ifdef GIMPLE` was used where `#if GIMPLE` was intended, which could lead to incorrect conditional compilation of GIMPLE-related code.
The issue was found and fixed, and the change was verified through a bootstrapped build and test on an x86_64 Linux system.
