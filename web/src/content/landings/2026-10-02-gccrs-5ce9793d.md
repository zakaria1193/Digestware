The gccrs compiler now supports a new intrinsic function, `float_to_int_unchecked`, for converting floating-point numbers to integers without performing additional checks. This intrinsic mirrors a feature found in standard Rust casts, which also lack certain safety checks. Its addition is motivated by the need to handle potentially invalid casts and aligns the compiler's intrinsic support with Rust's casting behavior.
