An automaton has been introduced to parse COBOL PICTURE strings, enforcing ISO Table 10 rules for PICTURE sequences. This change enhances the accuracy of PICTURE clause evaluation, correctly identifying data item types or invalid characters, and has revealed errors in the test suite.
