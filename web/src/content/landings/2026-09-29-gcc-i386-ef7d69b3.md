On i386 architectures, this commit corrects how `va_arg` accesses arguments on the stack when those arguments are over-aligned. Previously, GCC could generate code that read from misaligned addresses, leading to incorrect behavior or crashes, especially with optimizations. The fix ensures that the alignment claimed for accesses does not exceed the actual alignment of the stack slot.
