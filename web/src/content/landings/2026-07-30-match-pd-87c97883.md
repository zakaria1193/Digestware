This change simplifies specific patterns of promoted saturating addition and subtraction operations in the match.pd system. It rewrites operations involving intermediate wide types and clamping into more direct, narrower forms. This reduces the number of instructions generated, especially for vector types, leading to improved performance on targets like AArch64.
