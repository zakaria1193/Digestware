The gccrs compiler now supports the `assert_inhabited<T>` intrinsic. This intrinsic is used to assert that a type is inhabited, meaning it can hold a value. The implementation involves adding a new handler in the backend, along with necessary declarations and type-checking information. New test cases are included to verify its functionality.
