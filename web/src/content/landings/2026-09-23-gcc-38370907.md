This commit corrects the cost estimation for vectorizable loads in GCC's tree-vectorizer. Previously, non-uniform invariant loads were costed incorrectly, and `scalar_to_vec` was inappropriately used for non-splat loads. The fix ensures more accurate cost analysis and utilizes `vec_construct` for non-splat loads, improving vectorization efficiency.
