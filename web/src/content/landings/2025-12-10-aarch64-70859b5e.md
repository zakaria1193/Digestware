This commit improves the representation of unspec codes in the aarch64 backend by changing their storage type from `int` to `unspec`. This change ensures that GDB will now print the symbolic names of these unspec codes instead of their raw integer values, aiding debugging. It also enhances type safety by using a dedicated type for unspec codes.
