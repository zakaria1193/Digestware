This commit implements the <bit> header functionalities for C++29's P3793R2 proposal, commonly known as 'Better shifting.' It introduces new concepts like `std::__signed_or_unsigned_integer` and function templates `std::shl` and `std::shr` for more robust and expressive bitwise left and right shifts.
