The exception handling subsystem now correctly accounts for non-call exceptions that can occur on the left-hand side (LHS) of assignments, particularly when the `this` pointer is involved. Previously, a fix for PR127133 incorrectly marked the `this` argument as non-trapping, leading to an oversight in exception analysis. This commit replaces checks for `tree_could_throw_p` with `lhs_could_trap_p` and `tree_could_throw_1` in `lower_eh_constructs_2` and related functions, ensuring that potential trapping on the LHS is properly identified, fixing PR127183.
