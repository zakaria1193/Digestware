Libstdc++ has updated its build configuration by replacing all instances of the deprecated `AC_TRY_LINK` macro with `AC_LINK_IFELSE` in its `acinclude.m4` file. This change modernizes the way `libstdc++` performs link-time checks for various features and capabilities, aligning with current Autoconf best practices. The update affects several `GLIBCXX_CHECK` and `GLIBCXX_ENABLE` macros related to linker features and atomic builtins.
