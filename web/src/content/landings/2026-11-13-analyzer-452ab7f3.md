This commit renames internal components within the GCC Analyzer that were previously referred to as 'symbol' to 'symbolic::node'. This change is primarily for improved clarity and consistency in the codebase, with no functional modifications intended. The renaming affects object files, header files, and various source files, including updates to manager functions like `alloc_symbol_id` to `alloc_node_id` and `m_next_symbol_id` to `m_next_node_id`.
