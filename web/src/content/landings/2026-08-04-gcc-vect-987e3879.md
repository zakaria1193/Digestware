This commit addresses an issue in the vectorizer where the `compatible_complex_nodes_p` check was too permissive, incorrectly assuming that real and imaginary parts of complex numbers always originated from the same defining statement. The change explicitly checks the defining statements for both real and imaginary parts, ensuring more accurate vectorization of complex arithmetic. A new test case for AArch64 is included to verify the fix.
