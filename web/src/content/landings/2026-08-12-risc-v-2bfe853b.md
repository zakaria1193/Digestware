GCC's RISC-V backend now includes support for the Ascalon XG CPU. This commit adds the necessary definitions to `riscv-cores.def`, `riscv-opts.h`, and `riscv.md` to recognize the `tt-ascalon-xg` microarchitecture. The Ascalon XG differs from the Ascalon X by lacking the `zvkng` extension and featuring reduced vector floating-point double-precision throughput. New defines for CPU units and reservations in `tt-ascalon-d8.md` capture these microarchitectural details for instruction scheduling and optimization.
