The s390 assembler now supports the `.insn` pseudo-mnemonics rxy_a, rxy_b, and rxy_c, corresponding to different generations of the RXY instruction format. The change adds these mnemonics to the opcode definitions and includes new test cases for z/Architecture. The assembler itself does not enforce architecture or CPU level requirements for these.
