This change optimizes the conversion of integer constants to poly_int coefficients by returning a cached integer_zero_node instead of repeatedly building a new zero tree. On targets like AArch64 and RISC-V, these conversions are frequent within alias analysis, and this optimization reduces compiler instruction count by about 1-2% on C++ codebases, leading to faster compile times.
