The AArch64 architecture port of GAS has been updated to explicitly reject section-relative symbols when generating GOT relocations. Previously, GAS would incorrectly convert GOT relocations of local symbols into section-relative ones without considering that GOT relocations cannot properly handle arbitrary offsets. This fix, addressing PR30788, ensures that the assembler generates correct relocation entries, preventing potential runtime issues and misbehavior for AArch64 binaries.
