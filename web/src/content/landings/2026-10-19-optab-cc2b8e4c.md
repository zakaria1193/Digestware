The sum of absolute differences (SAD) operation has been reclassified as a convert optab. This change affects how SAD is handled in various configurations, including aarch64, SVE, NEON, i386, loongarch, and RS6000. Documentation and internal pattern matching have been updated to reflect this new classification, impacting vectorization and cost evaluation.
