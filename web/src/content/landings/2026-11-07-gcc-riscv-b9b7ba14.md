GCC introduces a new splitter for RISC-V to optimize sequences involving a wide load, a shift, and a narrow store. This pattern is reduced to a simple narrow load and narrow store, effectively eliminating the shift operation and saving dynamic instructions. The change is critical for performance and has been tested on multiple RISC-V targets.
