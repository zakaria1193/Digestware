Alignment tests for the Darwin target have been adjusted to accurately reflect the platform's natural alignment rules. Previously, special casing for Darwin was present in `natural_alignment_32` and `natural_alignment_64`. These special cases have been removed, aligning the tests with the behavior where Darwin naturally aligns 32-bit and smaller objects, and 64-bit and smaller objects on LP64 systems.
