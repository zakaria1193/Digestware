This commit rectifies an issue with the `__get_cpuid()` and `__get_cpuid_count()` functions on i386 architectures. Previously, a simple bitmask incorrectly categorized the Centaur/Zhaoxin CPUID range (0xC0000000) as the Extended range, leading to incorrect results. The fix replaces the bitmask with explicit range checks, accurately identifying all four CPUID ranges (Basic, Hypervisor, Extended, and Centaur/Zhaoxin) and improving feature detection for these processors.
