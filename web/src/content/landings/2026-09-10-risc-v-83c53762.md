This commit introduces a suite of test cases for the RISC-V Vector (RVV) extension, specifically targeting an internal compiler error (ICE) that occurred with widening instructions. The ICE was triggered when the destination and source registers of a widening instruction had the same number of registers, leading to incorrect assertion failures in the compiler's reload pass. These new tests ensure that such scenarios are correctly handled and prevent regressions.
