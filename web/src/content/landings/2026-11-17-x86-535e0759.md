This change refines common subexpression elimination (CSE) on x86 by removing redundant REG_EQUAL and REG_EQUIV notes. Previously, when a vector constant load was converted to a scalar integer load, the notes were left in place, causing issues with register allocation and constant pool handling. The fix ensures these notes are dropped when the source is replaced by a CONST_INT, preventing incorrect register equivalences and potential ICEs.
