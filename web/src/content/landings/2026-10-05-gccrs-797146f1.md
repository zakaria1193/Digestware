gccrs has corrected the evaluation order of function call arguments to match Rust's specification. The compiler's gimplifier previously evaluated arguments in the order they were pushed onto the stack (right-to-left on x86), affecting method calls and overloaded operators. The fix ensures that arguments, including the callee, are saved and evaluated strictly from left to right, addressing issues with side effects in argument evaluation. This change resolves Rust-GCC/gccrs#4956.
