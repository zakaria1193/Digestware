This commit refactors the Cobol compiler's error reporting for 'BY CONTENT expression' issues, ensuring it displays the specific argument's location rather than the location of the parsing rule. This provides more precise feedback to developers when a BY CONTENT expression is used incorrectly, such as when the '-dialect ibm' option is not enabled.
