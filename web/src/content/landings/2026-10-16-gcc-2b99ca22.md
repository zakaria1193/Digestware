The `reg_set_data` structure in GCC's `sched-int.h` now stores an `rtx_insn *` directly in its `insn` field. Since `create_insn_reg_set` is the sole writer of this field and already provides an `rtx_insn *`, this change simplifies the data structure and removes potential indirection.
