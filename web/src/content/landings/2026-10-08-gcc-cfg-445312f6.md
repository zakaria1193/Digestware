This commit enhances AutoFDO by automatically assigning hierarchical copy identifiers to duplicated Control Flow Graph (CFG) regions and statements. Previously, manual updates were required, which was error-prone. The new system assigns a unique identifier based on the location of the first block or statement in the duplicated region. This allows sample profiles to more accurately distinguish between copies of the same source line, leading to better profile-guided optimizations.
