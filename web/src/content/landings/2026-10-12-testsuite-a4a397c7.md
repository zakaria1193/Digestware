This commit enhances test coverage for floating-point trapping comparisons, specifically addressing issues on Cortex-M targets that disable non-short-circuit operations. The original test `fp-trapping-cmp-7.c` was split into two, with explicit settings for the `logical-op-non-short-circuit` parameter. This ensures tests cover both scenarios: when comparisons are kept separate (parameter 0) and when they are combined (parameter 1), making the tests independent of target tuning.
