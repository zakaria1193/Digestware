This patch refines the profitability analysis for SLP (Superword Level Parallelism) vectorization in GCC. It now utilizes the computed schedule location for SLP nodes to determine the containing loop more accurately. This addresses issues where vector constants and externals, scheduled at the region entry, introduced 'outside' costs not present in scalar costing. By delaying the assessment of profitability for the entry region and considering total costs, the analysis becomes more reliable.
