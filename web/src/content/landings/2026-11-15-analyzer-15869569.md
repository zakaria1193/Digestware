This commit introduces an assertion in the constructor for `svalue_from_bit_vector` within the GCC analyzer. The assertion verifies that all-zeros or all-ones bit patterns for integral types have been correctly folded into a constant value. This strengthens the internal consistency checks of the symbolic value representation used by the analyzer.
