This commit corrects the advertisement of vector operation optabs for the ARC architecture. Previously, operations like multiply and divide were advertised for V2SImode even when not supported by TARGET_PLUS_QMACW. This led to middle-end ICEs when operands degraded to DImode. The change restricts these optabs to be advertised only when TARGET_PLUS_QMACW is enabled, ensuring that vector modes are correctly supported.
