primer_expert: This commit enhances the `gimple-range-fold` pass by allowing it to
  narrow loads from constant aggregates based on the known range of the array index.
  Previously, loads from constant tables always considered the entire table's data.
  Now, if the index SSA_NAME has a known range, the pass restricts the loaded values
  to only those elements within that range. This improvement, motivated by DOM parity

  comparisons involving such loads, and removes the need for special handling of INTEGER_CSTs.

The GCC compiler now optimizes loads from constant aggregates more effectively by considering the known range of the index. Instead of assuming the entire aggregate might be accessed, it now narrows the loaded values to only those elements that could possibly be reached by the index's known range. This enables further optimizations, such as folding comparisons involving these narrowed loads.
