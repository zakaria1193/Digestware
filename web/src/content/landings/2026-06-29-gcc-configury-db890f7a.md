This commit updates GCC's build configuration scripts to replace several obsolete autoconf macros. The changes are made to address warnings generated by `autoreconf -Wall` and primarily involve substituting macros like `AC_TRY_COMPILE` with `AC_COMPILE_IFELSE` and `AC_TRY_LINK` with `AC_LINK_IFELSE`, as recommended by autoconf documentation.
