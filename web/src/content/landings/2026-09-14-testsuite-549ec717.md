The GCC testsuite on Darwin now correctly checks for AVX512 availability by querying the kernel directly. This is because Darwin enables AVX512 state lazily, making a direct read of XCR0 unreliable during constructor calls. This change ensures the testsuite accurately reflects hardware capabilities.
