A constraint issue in the SPARC64 `sub_ddmmss` macro within `longlong.h` has been fixed by removing unnecessary '%' characters from the `ah` and `al` constraints. These characters incorrectly allowed the compiler to reorder operands, potentially altering the subtraction. The fix ensures the macro performs the subtraction as intended, with no observed impact on generated code across various optimization levels and microarchitectures.
