This commit enables register overlap for the RISC-V Vector extension instructions `vfwmacc.vv` and `vfwmsac.vv`. Similar to how `vwmacc.vv` handles overlapping source and destination registers, this change allows these dual-width floating-point ternary instructions to also utilize register overlap. This potentially improves instruction scheduling and register pressure management.
