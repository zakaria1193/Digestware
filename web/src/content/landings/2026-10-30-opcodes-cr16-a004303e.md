Two unused variables, `processing_argument_number` and `imm4flag`, have been removed from the CR16 disassembler code in `opcodes/cr16-dis.c`. These variables have been present but unused since the CR16 port was initially added in 2007. Their removal cleans up the codebase and resolves compiler warnings related to unused variables.
