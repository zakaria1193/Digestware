The linker's PDB (Program Database) generation could produce non-deterministic ordering for global symbols that had hash collisions. This was because the `global_compare_hash` function treated symbols with identical hashes as equal, relying on the host system's `qsort` implementation for their relative order. This commit modifies `global_compare_hash` to further sort colliding symbols by their record offset, ensuring that the PDB output is reproducible across different build environments.
