A recent change in control flow graph (CFG) representation was found to break the build bootstrap process for x86_64-linux and i686-linux targets. This commit reverts that problematic change by correctly handling `LABEL_NUSES` decrements and increments when redirecting control flow edges. The fix ensures that the RTL checking mechanism functions as expected during compilation.
