The performance of range inference `clear` operations has been improved by replacing a full control flow graph walk with a traversal of a linked list. This optimization focuses the clearing process on relevant data structures, reducing overhead and speeding up the range analysis phase of the compiler, addressing PR tree-optimization/126856.
