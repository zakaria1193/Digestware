The GCC analyzer incorrectly copied values into by-value parameters and rvalue-reference constructor parameters where a move was intended. This commit adds `std::move` in three specific instances within the analyzer's infinite loop detection and HTML sink reporting, ensuring that resources are moved efficiently instead of being copied.
