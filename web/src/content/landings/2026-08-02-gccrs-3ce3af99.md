GCC's Rust front-end (`gccrs`) has refactored its Abstract Syntax Tree (AST) handling for `SlicePattern` and `RestPattern`. This change allows `SlicePattern` instances to represent multiple `RestPattern` children, simplifying the handling of potentially invalid patterns that are guarded by `cfg` attributes. The implications of `RestPattern` are now deferred until the HIR lowering stage, reducing complexity earlier in the compilation pipeline.
