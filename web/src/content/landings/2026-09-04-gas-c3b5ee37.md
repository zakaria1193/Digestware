The `obj-fdpicelf.c` and `obj-fdpicelf.h` files, which were used to inject a single `#define` into the assembler configuration, have been removed. These were not appropriate and have been replaced by the standard mechanism of using target-environment (`te-*.h`) headers. A new `te-linux-fdpic.h` has been introduced.
