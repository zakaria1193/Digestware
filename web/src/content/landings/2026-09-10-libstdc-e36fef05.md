A regression in libstdc++'s unordered containers has been fixed by properly value-initializing members of `_Hashtable_ebo_helper`. Previously, aggregate initialization with `{}` was ill-formed when members had explicit default constructors, breaking support for custom hashers, equality predicates, and allocators. This patch uses `Type()` syntax for value-initialization, ensuring correct initialization of all aggregate members and invocation of default constructors.
