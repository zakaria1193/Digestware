This commit addresses an issue in the i386 backend (PR128437) where dead 64-bit TLS local-dynamic base calls could persist and cause ICEs due to missing notes or deleted reloads. The fix makes the generation of these sequences independent of other optimization passes by ensuring that if no local-dynamic TLS symbol is found, a simpler 'xor' instruction is emitted instead of a full call. This also applies to 32-bit patterns and removes a now-unnecessary helper function.
