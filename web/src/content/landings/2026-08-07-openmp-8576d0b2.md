This commit refactors OpenMP loop expansion to avoid redundant calls to OMPT worksharing functions when the `inscan` modifier is used. The `inscan` modifier already implies a call to `GOMP_loop_end` (or its variants), making subsequent calls to `*_worksharing_end` functions unnecessary. The changes adjust the expansion logic to prevent these redundant calls, ensuring correct and efficient handling of OpenMP loops with the `inscan` modifier.
