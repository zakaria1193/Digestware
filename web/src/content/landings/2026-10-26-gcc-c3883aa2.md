GCC's SSA (Static Single Assignment) name information now unifies alignment data with a range bitmask. This change ensures that the bitmask, which can hold more refined information, is consistent with the explicit `align` and `misalign` fields. Assertions are added to verify this compatibility, serving as a temporary measure before the alignment fields are fully removed.
