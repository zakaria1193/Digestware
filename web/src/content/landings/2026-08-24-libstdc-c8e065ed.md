A copy-paste error in the `basic_ospanstream` constructor for `libstdc++` caused it to incorrectly initialize its `basic_spanbuf`. This commit corrects the `openmode` parameter, ensuring the span buffer is properly opened for output operations, and includes a new test case to verify the fix.
