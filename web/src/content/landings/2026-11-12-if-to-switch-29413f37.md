A bug in GCC's if-to-switch optimization pass has been fixed, preventing it from incorrectly merging 'anti-ranges' in logical OR conditions. The pass previously mishandled conditions like `(x not in R1) | (x not in R2)`, leading to generated switch cases that covered the union of R1 and R2 instead of their intersection. This resulted in some values reaching the wrong code path. The fix ensures that IOR conditions are only merged if they involve 'IN_P' ranges, guaranteeing correct switch case generation.
