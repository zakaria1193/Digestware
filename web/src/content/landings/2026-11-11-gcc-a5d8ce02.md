A minor correction has been made to GCC's `cuda.h` header to align the definition of `CU_MEMHOSTALLOC_DEVICEMAP` with the CUDA specification. Previously defined as `0x02U`, it is now defined as `0x02` to match the CUDA standard, preventing potential behavioral differences in specific configurations that include the system's `cuda.h`.
