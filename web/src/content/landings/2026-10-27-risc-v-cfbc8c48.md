This commit enhances the RISC-V Zba extension by optimizing the combination of shift-add operations with word division and remainder instructions. The change extends existing optimizations for unsigned division and remainder operations. It recognizes that these division/remainder instructions only use the lower word of inputs and sign-extend their results. Consequently, an XLEN-wide shift-add instruction can now precede the division or remainder, replacing a sequence of word shifts and additions, leading to more efficient code generation.
