This commit addresses a performance issue on i386 architectures where the BSR (Bit Scan Reverse) instruction can incur a false dependency on its destination register. A new tuning option, `X86_TUNE_AVOID_FALSE_DEP_FOR_BSR`, is introduced to clear the destination register before BSR when it's not used by the source operand. This prevents unnecessary stalls and improves performance, especially on processors where BSR is a single micro-operation.
