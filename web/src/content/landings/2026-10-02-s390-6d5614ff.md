The s390 assembler's instruction format RRF_F0FF, where the first operand is in bits 16-19, has been renamed to RRD_F0FF. This change separates it from the general RRF format starting with the IBM z196 architecture. Several instructions, including madbr, madr, maebr, maer, msdbr, msdr, msebr, mser, myhr, and mylr, are updated to use the new RRD_F0FF format designation.
