This change prevents GCC's testsuite from crashing when it encounters errors related to loading plugins for Link-Time Optimization (LTO) object files. Previously, if a required plugin (like liblto_plugin.so) was not found, it could cause a Tcl error to be thrown, aborting test execution. This patch ensures that such errors are handled gracefully, preventing testsuite integrity issues and allowing tests to complete even in environments where plugins might not be immediately available.
