The RISC-V BFD linker now warns when the `unaligned_access` attribute is not strictly boolean (0 or 1) and converts any non-zero value to 1. This ensures that the attribute is treated correctly, preventing potential issues with systems that might incorrectly set this flag to values other than 0 or 1.
