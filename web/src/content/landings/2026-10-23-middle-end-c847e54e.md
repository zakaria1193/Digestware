This commit corrects an issue where CRC built-in functions used `CONST_SIZE` for their polynomial argument, leading to truncation to 32 bits on 32-bit platforms. This caused `-Woverflow` warnings and incorrect calculations. The fix replaces `CONST_SIZE` with specific unsigned integer types (e.g., `UINT8`, `UINT64`) corresponding to the CRC function's bit-width, aligning with documentation and resolving the truncation problem.
