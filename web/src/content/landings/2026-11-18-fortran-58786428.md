This commit fixes a segfault occurring in GFortran when performing derived-type assignment where the left-hand side (LHS) is a pointer. The issue arose when a component of the derived type had a defined assignment routine that used `INTENT(INOUT)` or `INTENT(OUT)` with finalizable arguments. In such cases, `generate_component_assignments` would copy the LHS to a temporary. If the LHS was a scalar pointer, this temporary also became a pointer but was not allocated, leading to a write through a null pointer. The fix prevents scalar temporaries from receiving the `POINTER` attribute, resolving the segfault.
