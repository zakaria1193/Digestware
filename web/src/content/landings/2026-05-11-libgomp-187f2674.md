This commit improves the organization of the libgomp codebase by extracting the prototype for the `build_indirect_map` function into a newly created `config/accel/target-indirect.h` header file. Previously, this prototype was duplicated across various accelerator-specific `team.c` files. Centralizing the declaration eliminates redundancy and streamlines maintenance, ensuring consistency for OpenMP offloading implementations across different targets.
