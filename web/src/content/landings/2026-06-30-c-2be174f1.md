The C++ standard requires classes with implicit lifetimes to have at least one trivial eligible constructor. This change updates GCC to correctly identify such constructors by checking for unsatisfied constraints, preventing incorrect analysis of types like `std::string_view` and ensuring compliance with C++20 and later standards. A new test case is included to verify the fix.
