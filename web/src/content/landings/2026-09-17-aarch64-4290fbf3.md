This commit ports NEON multiply-accumulate (vmla) and multiply-subtract (vmls) intrinsics to GCC's pragma-based framework, adding corresponding assembly tests. It also removes several `__builtin_aarch64_*` functions that were exclusively used by `arm_neon.h`, streamlining the interface for these SIMD operations.
