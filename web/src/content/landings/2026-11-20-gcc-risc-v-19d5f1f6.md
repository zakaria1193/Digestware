The RISC-V vectorizer previously treated gather and scatter vector operations as scalar loads and stores, leading to inaccurate cost estimations. This commit enables the use of dedicated `gather_load_cost` and `scatter_store_cost` by overriding the `add_slp_cost` function. It ensures that vector loads/stores with the `VMAT_GATHER_SCATTER_IFN` kind are correctly identified as vector gather/scatter operations, improving vectorization cost modeling.
