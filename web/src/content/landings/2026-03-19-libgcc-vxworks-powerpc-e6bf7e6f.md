To enable proper stack unwinding through linker stubs on VxWorks for PowerPC architectures, a new mechanism, `MD_FROB_UPDATE_CONTEXT`, has been implemented. This functionality is similar to existing implementations for other operating systems and ensures that the unwinder can correctly traverse call stacks involving these stubs.
