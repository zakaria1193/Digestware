- term: '[SU]ADDLP'

This commit enhances the optimization of widening sum reductions on aarch64 by fully utilizing the `[SU]ADDLP` and `[SU]ADALP` instructions. It adds support for previously uncovered SIMD shapes and ensures that all power-of-two widening sum reductions are expressed efficiently. This change improves performance by reducing the number of operations required for these reductions, particularly for summing bytes into 64-bit accumulators.
