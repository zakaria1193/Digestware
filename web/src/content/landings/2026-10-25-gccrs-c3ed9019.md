The gccrs compiler now correctly type-checks bitshift compound assignment operations. Previously, the right-hand side operand was not consistently cast to match the type of the left-hand side for bitshift assignments, potentially leading to type errors or unexpected behavior. This fix aligns the behavior with arithmetic and logical compound assignments.
