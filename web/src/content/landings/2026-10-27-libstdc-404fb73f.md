This commit fixes a bug in `std::inplace_vector` where capacity checks could incorrectly succeed for oversized ranges. Previously, the size of the range was unconditionally cast to `size_type`, potentially truncating values and leading to the acceptance of ranges larger than the vector's capacity. The fix introduces `_S_distance_if_fits` and `_S_check_size` to perform proper bounds checking, ensuring that ranges exceeding capacity are rejected.
