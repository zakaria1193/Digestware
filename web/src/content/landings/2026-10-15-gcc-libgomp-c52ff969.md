A race condition in libgomp's GCN plugin could lead to incorrect detection of completed OpenACC async operations on AMD GPUs. This occurred when a queue's drain thread signaled an async placeholder and then dequeued it, allowing a waiting thread to observe the queue as complete prematurely. The fix holds the queue lock during the signaling of the placeholder, making the signal and dequeue operations appear atomic to other threads and resolving intermittent test failures.
