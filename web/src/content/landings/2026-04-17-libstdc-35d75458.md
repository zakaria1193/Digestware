The `operator==` for `std::indirect` was prone to constraint recursion due to CWG 2369.  This commit replaces the non-dependent `std::indirect` function parameter with a dependent one, similar to the fix for `std::expected` and `std::basic_const_iterator`. A new test case has also been added.
