This commit refines the `signed_integer_sat_trunc` pattern in GCC's matcher to require that the precision of the operand and the result of the conversion are uniform. Previously, cases where the comparison narrowed the precision after a variable shift could lead to an internal compiler error in the vectorizer. The updated pattern ensures that the conversion maintains the precision of the operand and that the result is narrower, preventing mismatches and resolving PR126982.
