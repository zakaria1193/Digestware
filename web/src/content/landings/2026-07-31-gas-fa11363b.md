The `sframe_xlate_ctx_cleanup` function in the GNU Assembler (GAS) has been improved to prevent dangling pointers. After freeing memory associated with `first_fre`, `remember_fre`, and `cur_fre`, the function now explicitly sets these pointers to `NULL`. Additionally, related members like `last_fre`, `num_xlate_fres`, and `flex_p` are reset to their default states, ensuring a clean state after cleanup.
