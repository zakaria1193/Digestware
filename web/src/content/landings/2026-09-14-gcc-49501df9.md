This commit removes duplicate include directives for `stringpool.h` across various GCC source files. The duplication arose after functions were moved to `attribs.h`, and headers were included without checking for prior inclusion of `stringpool.h`. While include guards prevent actual compilation issues, removing these duplicates simplifies the codebase and reduces build noise.
