This Binutils commit modifies `gas` to allow local symbols to have non-protected visibility, partially reverting a previous commit that introduced a warning for local symbols with non-default visibility. The change addresses a warning encountered in `glibc` where a local, hidden symbol defined via an `asm` statement would trigger a warning when referenced in C code. This update clarifies that symbol binding (local vs. global) and visibility (default, hidden, protected) are orthogonal concepts in ELF, and only the combination of `STB_LOCAL` and `STV_PROTECTED` is explicitly disallowed by the spec.
