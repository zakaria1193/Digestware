The RISC-V compiler now diagnoses conflicts between the Zclsd and Zcf extensions. These extensions share instruction encodings, leading to ambiguity. The check is performed after expanding implied extensions, ensuring that conflicts are caught even when C and F flags imply Zcf on RV32.
