This commit refactors the PE linker's handling of relocation types. It introduces or enhances a generic `in_reloc_p` function to determine if a relocation is 'in-place' (i.e., modifies the code/data directly). By filling in the necessary relocation type checks in per-architecture implementations, the linker can drop its custom recording of section, section-relative, and absolute relocation types. This leads to cleaner and more maintainable code.
