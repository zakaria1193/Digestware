A recent change in GCC's atomic operations handling broke libgfortran on 32-bit systems by incorrectly assuming `__atomic_fetch_add` worked on `uint64_t`. This commit corrects the `LIBGFOR_CHECK_ATOMIC_FETCH_ADD` configure test to accurately check for 64-bit integer support. This resolves issues for 32-bit systems that lack the necessary 64-bit atomic operations or `libatomic` support.
