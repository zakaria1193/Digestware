This change canonicalizes the operands of commutative arithmetic overflow internal functions, such as ADD_OVERFLOW. Previously, the order of operands could be non-canonical, potentially disagreeing with related addition operations. By using tree_swap_operands_p, the operands are now ordered consistently, improving the predictability of optimization passes and reducing potential discrepancies in generated code. This also applies to MUL_OVERFLOW, while SUB_OVERFLOW retains its original order as it is not commutative. A new test case for aarch64 verifies this behavior.
