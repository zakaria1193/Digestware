GCC now supports new atomic fetch-with-hint intrinsics for the AArch64 architecture, leveraging the FEAT_CMH extension. This addition enables the use of new hints like SHUH and SHUH PH for atomic operations such as fetch-and-add, fetch-and-sub, fetch-and-or, fetch-and-xor, and fetch-and-and. Corresponding test cases have been added.
