This commit updates the Alpha linker to reject references to IFUNC symbols that include a non-zero addend. The `R_ALPHA_IRELATIVE` relocation, used for IFUNCs, embeds the resolver's address in its addend field, leaving no room for an additional offset. References originating from `R_ALPHA_LITERAL` or `R_ALPHA_REFQUAD` with an addend are now diagnosed as errors.
