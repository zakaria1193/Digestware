The test file `gcc.dg/tree-ssa/signbit-1.c` previously included checks for a sign-bit fold simplification that belonged to a separate patch. This commit removes those unrelated expressions and dump scans from `signbit-1.c` to ensure the test accurately verifies the `X + (X | -X)` simplification without interference, allowing its associated patch to be tested independently.
