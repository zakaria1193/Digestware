This commit modifies the AArch64 backend to block the expansion of `COMPLEX_MUL` when the `HONOR_SIGNED_ZEROS` flag is enabled. The current emulation of `COMPLEX_MUL` using `COMPLEX_FMA` with a $+0.0$ accumulator can produce incorrect zero signs due to rounding or flushing behavior. To maintain correctness, this optimization is disabled when signed zero handling is required. This change is accompanied by test case updates to enforce the `-fno-signed-zeros` flag for relevant scenarios.
