This commit ports NEON widening shift intrinsics, specifically `vshll_n` and `vshll_high_n`, to GCC's pragma-based framework on the aarch64 architecture. This change consolidates the handling of these SIMD operations within a unified system, improving maintainability and potentially enabling further optimizations.
