Assembly time for `gcc.c-torture/compile/limits-externalid` on Xtensa has been drastically reduced from approximately 20 minutes to 1 second. This is achieved by optimizing the `next_frag_is_branch_target` function to avoid redundant checks and by modifying `xtensa_fix_target_frags` to process a range of frags only once.
