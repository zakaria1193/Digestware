The `mdspan` deduction guide now accepts `data_handle_type` by value, consistent with the corresponding constructor. This resolves LWG4511, ensuring that the deduction guide matches the constructor's signature for the `_AccessorType::data_handle_type` parameter. A new test case validates this change.
