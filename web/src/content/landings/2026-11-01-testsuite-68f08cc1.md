This change marks the test case `pr125734.c` as 'expected to fail' (XFAIL) on target architectures that do not support the CLZ (Count Leading Zeros) instruction. This prevents the test suite from reporting a failure when the underlying hardware lacks the necessary instruction, improving the accuracy of test results.
