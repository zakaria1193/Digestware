This commit removes the `consteval` conversion for value-preserving conversion from constants that was added by P3844R2. It also modifies the broadcast constructor according to P4012R1. This keeps the design space open for potential changes in C++29. Integer literals in tests are replaced with explicit casts or `cw`.
