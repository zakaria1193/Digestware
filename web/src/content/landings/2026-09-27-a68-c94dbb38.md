The Algol68 frontend has been corrected to avoid generating `TYPE_DECL`s with null `DECL_NAME`s in `a68-low-moids.cc`. This resolves an issue where struct and union types were being emitted with invalid names, aligning with Algol 68's handling of anonymous types until explicitly named.
