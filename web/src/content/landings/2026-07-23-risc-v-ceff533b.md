This commit refines the RISC-V vector code generation to adhere strictly to the Zvabd extension's specification. It disallows the generation of vabd and vwabda instructions for unsupported Single-Element Widths (SEW), specifically limiting them to SEW 8 or 16 as defined by the standard. This prevents the creation of invalid or non-standard vector operations.
