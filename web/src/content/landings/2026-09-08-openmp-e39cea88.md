This change ensures that OpenMP's 'enter' map clauses behave as 'link' when the `requires self_maps` or `unified_shared_memory` directives are in effect. During implicit declare-target discovery for global variables, existing `omp declare target` attributes are now transformed into `omp declare target link`. This aligns the behavior with the specified requirements for self-mapping and unified shared memory, improving the correctness of offload operations.
