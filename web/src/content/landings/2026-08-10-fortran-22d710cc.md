This commit resolves several internal compiler errors (ICEs) related to querying the cobounds of a subobject of a coarray in Fortran. According to the F2018 standard, such subobjects are coarrays themselves unless accessed through specific selectors. The compiler was not correctly handling these cases, leading to errors when querying cobounds. The fixes ensure that the compiler correctly identifies and queries the cobounds of the coarray base symbol, even when accessing its subobjects, and properly handles cases where the declaration itself might have been previously rejected.
