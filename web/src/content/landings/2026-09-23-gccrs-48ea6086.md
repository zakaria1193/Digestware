To align with existing naming conventions, gccrs has renamed several mapping categories, including `pmacro_mappings` and `crate_mappings`, to shorter, consistent forms like `pmacro` and `crate`. This change improves code clarity and maintainability by standardizing how different mapping types are referenced throughout the codebase.
