This commit rectifies several issues with AArch64 'overflow' instruction patterns, specifically for MINUS and NEG operations. It corrects the selection of the CCmode for overflow checks, ensures patterns are in canonical form for better optimization by the 'combine' pass, and fixes an invalid output template that caused an ICE.
