This change optimizes widening sum operations on aarch64 by utilizing the dot product instruction ([SU]DOT). Previously, summing bytes into 64-bit elements required multiple add operations. The new approach uses a single dot product instruction, which, while not reducing instruction count, better distributes the vector work and yields a performance improvement of up to 24% for unsigned char sums into long on compatible processors.
