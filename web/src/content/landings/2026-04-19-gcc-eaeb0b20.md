The Makefile for GNAT tools on Windows hosts was incorrectly overriding adjustments to the LIBS variable, causing link failures. This commit rearranges the Makefile to ensure the correct libraries are linked, resolving the issue. Specifically, it moves the definitions of TGT_LIB, LIBBACKTRACE, LIBIBERTY, LIBGNAT, and LIBS up and ensures TOOLS_LIBS uses $(LIBS).
