The commit resolves a link failure that occurred when building GNAT tools on Windows hosts. The Makefile was overriding an adjustment to the `LIBS` variable, preventing the necessary libraries from being included in the link. The fix involves moving the definition of `LIBS` and other related variables to ensure they are correctly used during the linking process.
