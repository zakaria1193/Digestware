This commit addresses three long-standing bug reports concerning illegal constructs that the Ada compiler was incorrectly accepting. It fixes issues with renaming inherited subprograms of derived untagged types, accepting nonstatic constraints for discriminants of private extension ancestors, and accepting simple aggregates for extension of formal tagged private types. While two of the fixes involve commentary updates to avoid breaking existing code, the third case is fully corrected.
