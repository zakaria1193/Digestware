This commit resolves a bug in the BPF assembler (PR 34558) where `gotol` instructions with signed offsets were silently misassembled. Due to the order of unconditional jump mnemonics in the opcode table, `gotol +1` was incorrectly parsed as `goto l +1`, resulting in the `JA` opcode with a 16-bit offset and an invalid relocation instead of the intended `JAL` opcode with a 32-bit immediate. The fix modifies the assembler's template matching to correctly distinguish `gotol` from `goto`, especially with signed offsets.
