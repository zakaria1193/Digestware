A simplification rule in `match.pd` incorrectly assumed that `parity(~X)` is always equivalent to `parity(X)`. This commit corrects that rule, noting that for integer types with an odd number of bits, `parity(~X)` is actually equivalent to `parity(X) ^ 1`. This ensures accurate parity calculations for bitwise NOT operations on types with odd precision, fixing PR126471.
