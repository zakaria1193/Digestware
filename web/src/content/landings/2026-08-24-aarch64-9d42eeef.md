Several NEON reduction intrinsics for aarch64, including vaddv, vmaxv, vminv, vmaxnmv, and vminnmv, have been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework. Some floating-point variants requiring special NaN handling (e.g., vmaxv_f32) were not ported in this patch due to complexities in mapping them to existing instruction patterns. The changes involve adding new function bases and groups and removing many previously defined functions from arm_neon.h.
