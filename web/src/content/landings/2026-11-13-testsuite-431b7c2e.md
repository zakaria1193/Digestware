A test case in the x86 testsuite, `pr92080-13.c`, previously failed on hardware supporting AVX512F even with `-fno-pie`. This was because it's a link-and-run test, requiring the `-no-pie` flag to prevent Position Independent Executable behavior. The flag has now been added to ensure the test runs correctly.
