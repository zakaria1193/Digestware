The s390 assembler and disassembler now support instructions with up to three optional trailing operands, crucial for VRR variants like VRR-a and VRR-c. This update also fixes an issue where omitted trailing optional operands with constant bits were not correctly handled during disassembly. A new function, `s390_extract_operand_masked`, is introduced to manage constant operand bits for both omission testing and printing.
