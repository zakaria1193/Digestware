An optimization pass, `phiopt`, incorrectly simplified sequences of three comparisons (e.g., checking if a value is equal to, less than, or greater than another) into a single comparison. This led to miscompilation, particularly when dealing with floating-point comparisons and `HONOR_NANS`. The fix ensures that the optimization correctly distinguishes between different comparison outcomes and preserves the necessary checks, resolving the issue for the affected test cases.
