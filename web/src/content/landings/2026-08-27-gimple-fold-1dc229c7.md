This commit corrects the GIMPLE optimization pass for folding vector length load and store operations when a target-specific bias is involved. The issue arose from incorrect handling of the bias, leading to miscalculations that could cause these operations to be incorrectly folded away. The fix adjusts the calculation to correctly incorporate the bias, ensuring accurate behavior, especially on targets like s390.
