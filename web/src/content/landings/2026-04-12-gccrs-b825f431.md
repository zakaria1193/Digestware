This commit refines how gccrs handles control flow within Rust code, focusing on labeled and nested loops. It improves the resolution and compilation of `break` and `continue` statements by introducing specific context functions for loop labels and unifying the handling of break labels. The changes also introduce new test cases to cover various loop and label scenarios.
