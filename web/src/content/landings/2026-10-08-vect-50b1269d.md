This commit corrects issues within the GCC vectorizer related to AVX512 and non-partial vectorization, addressing PR126301. It removes a previous restriction on `vect_use_loop_latch_condition_p` for AVX512 and non-partial vectors, and introduces a new `vect_reuse_loop_latch` function. The logic for loop transformations has been added to `vect_set_loop_condition_partial_vectors_avx512` and `vect_set_loop_condition_normal` to ensure correct behavior.
