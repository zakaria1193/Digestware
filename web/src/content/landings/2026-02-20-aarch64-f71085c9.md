This commit continues the porting of AArch64 NEON SIMD intrinsics to GCC's pragma-based framework, now focusing on permutation operations like `vext`, `vrev`, `vtrn`, `vuzp`, and `vzip`. The changes consolidate intrinsic implementations, simplifying code management and maintenance within the compiler's backend. Associated definitions and function bases are updated or introduced in relevant backend files.
