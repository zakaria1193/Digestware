This change modifies the s390 assembler to treat immediate operands within `.insn` pseudo-mnemonics as accepting both signed and unsigned values. This ensures that the full range of possible values, from the minimum signed to the maximum unsigned, is considered valid. A new `PSEUDO_MNEMONIC` flag and updated fixup data handling in `s390_insert_operand` facilitate this mixed-range checking.
