A miscompilation on LoongArch occurred in `both_non_zero` and `both_non_zero_subreg` instructions when `operands[0]` was identical to `operands[2]`. This is because `operands[0]` was being written before `operands[2]` was fully used, necessitating an early-clobber designation. The fix defers register splitting until after reload and ensures `operands[0]` and `operands[2]` reside in distinct hard registers.
