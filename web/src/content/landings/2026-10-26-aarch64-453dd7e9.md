This commit adds support for 32-bit unsigned saturating multiplication on the AArch64 architecture. It introduces a new `usmulsi3` expansion that utilizes the `UMULL` instruction to compute the full 32-bit product. The result is then saturated to `UINT32_MAX` if the high part of the product indicates overflow. A new test case is included to verify this functionality.
