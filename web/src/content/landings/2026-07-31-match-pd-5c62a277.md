This commit corrects a simplification rule in `match.pd` that handles expressions like `((C << A) & D) != 0`. The previous rule incorrectly folded the expression to false or true based on a simple comparison of shift counts and mask values, without properly considering the type of the shift count. The fix ensures that if the difference between the constants (used in the shift comparison) does not fit within the type of the shift count operand, the simplification correctly deduces that the result is always false or true, preventing miscompilations.
