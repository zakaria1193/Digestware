This commit introduces PCLMUL instruction expanders to the i386 backend, enabling faster Cyclic Redundancy Check (CRC) computations. The new expanders leverage the PCLMULQDQ instruction for both bit-forward and bit-reversed CRC-16 and CRC-32 calculations when the target supports it. This optimization aligns the x86 backend's capabilities with other architectures like aarch64.
