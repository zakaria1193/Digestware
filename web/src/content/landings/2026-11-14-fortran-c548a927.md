This patch enhances the efficiency of copying contiguous array slices in Fortran by leveraging the `memcpy` and `memmove` built-in functions. It extends the conditions under which these optimized functions are generated to handle variable-sized arrays and slices, and introduces `memmove` for cases where overlapping regions might occur, reducing temporary variable usage and improving performance.
