This commit addresses a bug in the `ifcvt` (if-conversion) optimization where the `average_cost` calculation was sensitive to the order of control flow arms. The previous implementation could lead to different decisions about converting `if` statements to conditional moves based on whether the 'then' or 'else' branch appeared first. The fix ensures the calculation is commutative by always starting with the cheaper arm and scaling the difference by the probability of the more expensive arm, improving the robustness and correctness of the optimization.
