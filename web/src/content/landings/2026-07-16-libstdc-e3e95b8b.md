New tests verify the behavior of std::format_to and std::format_to_n with ostreambuf_iterator, covering scenarios like truncation, writing to sentinels, write failures, and large outputs exceeding internal buffers. The tests also demonstrate formatters that check for buffered output before writing to the stream.
