The zeroing of double-precision floating-point registers in Xtensa's `-fzero-call-used-regs` implementation has been refactored to use the standard asm statement infrastructure. This change eliminates the need for a custom `UNSPEC_CONCAT` entry and `movdf_concat` MD instruction pattern, simplifying the code. The asm statement is placed in the function epilogue, posing no risk to optimization.
