This change fixes a miscompilation issue in the C front-end related to bit-precise bit-fields. When a bit-precise integer bit-field is explicitly cast to a non-cv-qualified bit-precise integer of the same signedness and precision, the compiler was incorrectly applying integer promotions. The fix ensures that such casts are correctly handled by distinguishing them from direct use of the bit-field, preventing erroneous promotions. New test cases are added to verify the fix.
