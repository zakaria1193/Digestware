This change prevents crashes and the emission of orphaned debug records by ensuring that local variables without names are skipped during CodeView symbol generation. Previously, attempting to emit symbols for such variables led to null pointer dereferences. The fix applies to both unoptimized and optimized symbol writers, and specifically handles optimized locals by skipping them before emitting location ranges, thus avoiding issues with `S_LOCAL` and `S_DEFRANGE` records.
