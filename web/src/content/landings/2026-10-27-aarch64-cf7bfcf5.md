This commit fixes an ICE in the AArch64 backend that occurred when an intrinsic's result was discarded. The issue stemmed from using a temporary variable as a placeholder LHS, which led to incorrect SSA updates. The fix introduces a `lhs` member function and a `ret_type` member to the `gimple_folder`, ensuring proper handling of discarded results and preventing ICEs, while also improving the handling of aggregate types.
