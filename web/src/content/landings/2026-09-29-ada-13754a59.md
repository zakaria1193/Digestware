This change corrects an issue where parent discriminants of Ada tagged extensions were not properly initialized in certain aggregate expressions. The fix simplifies discriminant initialization logic by removing redundant procedures and consolidating necessary functions. This ensures correct behavior for aggregates derived from tagged extensions, particularly in cases involving ACATS cdd2a01.
