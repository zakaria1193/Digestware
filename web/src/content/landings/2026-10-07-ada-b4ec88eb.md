An oversight in the Ada `GNAT.Sockets.Thin` module caused `Disable_SIGPIPE` to be called even with invalid socket descriptors. This commit modifies `C_Accept` and `C_Socket` to only call `Disable_SIGPIPE` when the descriptor is valid, preventing potential issues and adhering to correct error handling.
