gccrs has refactored its interfaces for looking up and mapping built-in functions. The lookup functions now return `optional<tree>` for cleaner handling of absent builtins. The LLVM-to-GCC builtin mapping has been improved to return an `expected<T>` type, avoiding the need for pointer parameters and providing better error reporting through an associated `emit` method on the error type. These changes enhance the robustness and maintainability of the compiler's backend.
