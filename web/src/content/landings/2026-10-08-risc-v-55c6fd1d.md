The RISC-V assembler now correctly identifies and reports errors for MIPS vendor instructions. Previously, it would output a generic 'internal: unreachable INSN_CLASS_*' message instead of naming the specific MIPS extension involved when the extension was not enabled. This commit adds the necessary cases to `riscv_multi_subset_supports_ext` and includes a new test case to verify the fix.
