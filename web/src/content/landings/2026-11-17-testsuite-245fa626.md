The `fneg-abs_3.c` test in the AArch64 test suite previously failed due to register pressure estimation in the `ivopts` pass selecting an indexed addressing mode over the desired post-increment form. This change updates the test to accept either addressing mode, ensuring the test passes without altering the core check for vectorizing `fneg(fabs(x))` to a single ORR instruction.
