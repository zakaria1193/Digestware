The HPPA backend's cost calculation for the `shadd` instruction has been fixed to properly handle subregister operands. Previously, `hppa_rtx_costs_shadd_p` did not correctly strip `SUBREG` wrappers from inner operands, leading to incorrect cost estimations and issues with certain patterns on specific HPPA configurations.
