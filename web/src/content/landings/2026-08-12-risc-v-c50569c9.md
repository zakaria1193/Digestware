GCC can now recognize and fuse pairs of logical instructions on RISC-V architectures, such as ANDI, ORI, and XORI, when specific constraints on their destination and source registers are met. This new fusion capability, which reuses existing logic for canonical ANDI operations, is disabled by default and is supported by new test cases.
