An ICE was occurring on the s390x architecture due to how the `usubc5` pattern interacted with immediate operands. The pattern, intended for use with general operands, was sometimes being applied to immediate values in a way that conflicted with the `xor` instruction's requirements. This fix forces the problematic operand into a register before it's used in the `xor` instruction, allowing subsequent optimizations like CSE or combine to simplify it correctly.
