GCC's inline assembly syntax for output operand modifiers, traditionally limited to single characters (e.g., `%w0`), has been expanded to support multi-character names. This enhancement uses a bracketed notation, such as `%[name:0]`, allowing targets to define more descriptive modifiers. This change avoids the need for cryptic single-character modifiers or falling back to C output blocks for target-specific printing, making inline assembly more readable and maintainable.
