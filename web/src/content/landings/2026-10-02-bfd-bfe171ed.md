This commit fixes an issue in `bfd/elfnn-aarch64.c` where the `record_relr_dyn_got_relocs` function experienced a "union phase confusion" regarding GOT entries for `DT_RELR` relocations. The change modifies the condition for detecting unallocated GOT entries to match the updated offset semantics after `allocate_dynrelocs`.
