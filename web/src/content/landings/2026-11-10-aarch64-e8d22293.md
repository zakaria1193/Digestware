This change optimizes the registration of AArch64 NEON and SVE intrinsics by dynamically sizing the internal function table. Previously, a fixed-size table (1023 slots) frequently caused reallocations and rehashing as thousands of intrinsics were registered. The new approach calculates a size based on the number of functions in the intrinsic group tables, providing a sufficient lower bound and reducing reallocations. This also makes the calculation `constexpr`, further improving performance for translation units that include these headers, especially during fast compilation modes like `-fsyntax-only`.
