Two test cases in the `minmax-swar` suite previously had a hardcoded shift count of 31, assuming a 32-bit integer. This commit modifies them to dynamically derive the shift count from `sizeof(int)`, making them robust across different integer sizes and ensuring proper testing for various platforms.
