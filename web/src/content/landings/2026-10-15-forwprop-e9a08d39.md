The `simplify_vector_constructor` pass in `forwprop` has been updated to prevent folding `CONSTRUCTOR` expressions into abnormal PHI nodes. This optimization previously extended the live ranges of vectors, causing conflicts with SSA form when the vector was an SSA name in an abnormal PHI and redefined before the constructor. The change ensures correct SSA form and avoids out-of-SSA errors.
