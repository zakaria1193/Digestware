The aarch64 assembler now supports the immediate PC-relative forms of the `FEAT_PAuth_LR` instructions: `AUTIA<k>SPPC` and `RETA<k>SPPC`. These instructions use a 16-bit unsigned offset, scaled by 4, to specify a PC-relative address. The assembler handles this as an internal fixup, emitting no object-file relocation. Disassembly and testing have been updated accordingly.
