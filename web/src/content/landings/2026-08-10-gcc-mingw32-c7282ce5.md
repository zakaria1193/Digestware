primer_expert: The `HOST_EXTRA_OBJS_SYMBOL` in `sym-mingw32.cc` is used to implicitly
  pull in the manifest resource for an image. When `--export-all-symbols` is enabled
  during the build, the linker attempts to export all symbols. For i686, `ld.exe`

GCC MinGW32 builds with `--enable-plugin` encountered linker errors because `HOST_EXTRA_OBJS_SYMBOL`, an artificial symbol, was being exported. The linker, expecting symbols prefixed with an underscore for the i686 architecture, tried to export `_HOST_EXTRA_OBJS_SYMBOL` which was undefined. This change marks the symbol with `visibility("hidden")` to prevent its export, resolving the build failures. `extern "C"` was also added for C++ correctness.
