The `-fcf-protection=check` compiler flag previously caused the `__CET__` macro to be defined with a value indicating that both indirect branch tracking (IBT) and shadow stack (SHSTK) were enabled, even when they were not. This commit modifies the definition of `__CET__` to only reflect the `CF_BRANCH` and `CF_RETURN` bits when they are actually set, preventing incorrect GNU_PROPERTY_X86_FEATURE_1_AND notes in assembly files.
