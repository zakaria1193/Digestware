The definitions for the AArch64 `FEAT_TPS` and `FEAT_TPSP` system registers have been updated to align with the latest specification. The change replaces the use of `tpmin*`/`tpmax*` register pairs with individual `tpcr*` registers, ensuring correct behavior and compliance with current ARM architecture standards.
