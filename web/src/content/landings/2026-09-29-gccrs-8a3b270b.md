This commit introduces a new test case to Rust-GCC that specifically highlights collisions between top-level modules and extern crate names. It ensures that such errors are reported on the module itself, rather than the extern crate, which might lack a source location. This improves the accuracy and clarity of error messages related to module name conflicts.
