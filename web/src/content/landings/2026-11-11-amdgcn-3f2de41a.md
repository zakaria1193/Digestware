New patterns `zero_extendsidi` and `extendsidi` have been added for the AMD GCN target. These patterns help register allocation by ensuring that 64-bit values, including those resulting from sign or zero extensions, are maintained in their full width throughout the compilation process, even past the reload stage. This change improves performance, notably yielding speedups in the miniBUDE benchmark.
