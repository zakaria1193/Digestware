A new tuning for the ARC-V RPX-100 series RISC-V microarchitecture has been added, supporting RV64. This change includes modifications to multiplication instructions to differentiate between high and low parts of the result and adds bypass logic for load and store instructions. New test cases verify the functionality for both RV32 and RV64 configurations.
