This commit refines how subregisters are handled for Variable Length Scalar (VLS) vectors on RISC-V. Previously, changes in register mode natural sizes caused unnecessary spilling of subregisters, even when they were safe in a VLS context. The update modifies `can_change_mode_class` and `riscv_regmode_natural_size` to treat VLS vectors as unsplittable while still allowing safe lowpart subregs, preventing incorrect spills and improving vectorization.
