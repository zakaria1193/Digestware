This commit changes how conditional fused multiply-add (FMA) operations are handled on i386. Previously, it used a combination of FMA and masked move instructions, which could lead to exceptions even for masked-out values. The new implementation uses masked FMA instructions directly, avoiding these unexpected exceptions and improving correctness for conditional FMA operations.
