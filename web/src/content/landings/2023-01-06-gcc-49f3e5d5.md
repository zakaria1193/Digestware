The `libgo/misc/cgo/test/callback_c.c` file now consistently defines `WIN32_LEAN_AND_MEAN` before including `<windows.h>`. This change, related to PR middle-end/108300, prevents potential macro conflicts that arise from the inclusion of Wine's updated headers, specifically concerning the `abort()` function.
