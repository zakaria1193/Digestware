The masked load support for `ds_read`/`ds_write` instructions in the AMD GCN backend incorrectly handled pointer sizes when accessing Local Data Share (LDS) memory. This issue, while not previously causing problems due to infrequent masked vector access to LDS, has been fixed by introducing a new instruction specifically for LDS address spaces. This ensures correct behavior for masked loads from LDS.
