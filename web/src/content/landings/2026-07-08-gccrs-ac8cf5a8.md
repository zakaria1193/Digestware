gccrs now includes the `alloc` crate in its test suite, significantly expanding the scope of testing for Rust's standard library components. To accommodate current compiler limitations, certain unsupported features in `lib.rs` and the `ForceResult` enum in `collections/btree/node.rs` (related to issue Rust-GCC/gccrs#4687) have been temporarily commented out. This enables broader integration testing.
