Support for several `FEAT_PAuth_LR` instructions has been added to the aarch64 assembler. This includes instructions with no operands (`PACI<k>SPPC`, `PACNBI<k>SPPC`, `AUTI<k>171615`), a register variant of `AUTI<k>SPPC` (`AUTI<k>SPPCR`), and a register variant of `RETA<k>SPPC` (`RETA<k>SPPCR`). A new operand type `Rd_NOT_ZR` is introduced for `RETA<k>SPPCR` to reject the `XZR` register, while `AUTI<k>SPPCR` accepts it.
