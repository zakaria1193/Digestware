This change prevents the incorrect folding of negated Fused Multiply-Add (FMA) operations when the compiler honors signed zeros. Previously, folding `-fma(a, b, c)` into `fnms(a, b, c)` could result in a loss of the negative sign for exact zero results. The fix ensures these folds are only applied when signed zeros are disabled, preserving mathematical accuracy for floating-point calculations.
