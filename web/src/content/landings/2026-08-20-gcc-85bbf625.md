The `equiv_oracle::clear` method's performance is enhanced by introducing a mechanism to track basic blocks containing equivalences for SSA names. Instead of re-processing all blocks, `clear` now focuses only on the necessary blocks, improving efficiency for equivalence tracking during optimization passes. This change is related to PR tree-optimization/126856.
