This change modifies the folding of `MINP*/MAXP*` built-in functions on i386. Previously, these builtins were always folded into conditional moves when optimizing, which inadvertently dropped Signed-Aggregate Exception (SAE) information. The fix ensures this folding only occurs when the `flag_trapping_math` is unset, preserving SAE semantics by avoiding the folding that would suppress denormal exception behavior.
