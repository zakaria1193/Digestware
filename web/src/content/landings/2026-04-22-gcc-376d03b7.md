The compiler's implementation and the standard library's support for C++ reflection have been updated. The values of `__cpp_impl_reflection` and `__cpp_lib_reflection` are increased to 202603L. This indicates the full implementation of P3795R2, except for `is_applicable_type`, `is_nothrow_applicable_type` and `apply_result` metafunctions.
