A regression affecting the MSP430 target has been fixed. The issue arose from handling of NOOP_MOVE_INSN_CODE within the `msp430_insn_cost` function, which previously caused an early exit and subsequent errors. The change ensures these instructions are handled correctly, preventing testsuite regressions.
