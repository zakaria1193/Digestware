The h8300-elf linker script generation was updated to use USER_LABEL_PREFIX. This change ensures consistency with the GCC compiler's handling of symbols, which use a leading underscore. Previously, differences in prefix handling could lead to subtle bugs, particularly with weak symbols, and complicated cross-platform development. Test cases for the linker and H8/300 simulator have been updated to reflect this.
