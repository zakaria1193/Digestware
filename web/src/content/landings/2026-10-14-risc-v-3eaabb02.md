This commit introduces minimal support for the RISC-V Zvdota and Zvbdota extension families by registering them and their dependencies. It follows the 1.0 draft specifications for these extensions, which enable specialized dot-product operations. The changes include enabling BF16 data types for these dot-product extensions, ensuring that the compiler recognizes and can utilize these new capabilities.
