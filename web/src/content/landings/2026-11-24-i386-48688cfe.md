This commit addresses an issue on the i386 architecture with AVX10.2 and `-fno-trapping-math` where BFmode (BFloat16) equality and inequality comparisons were incorrectly handled. Previously, they were treated as single-flag tests, leading to NaNs being compared as equal. The fix converts BFmode EQ/NE comparisons to SFmode (single-precision float) when NaNs are honored, allowing the `vucomxss` instruction to process them correctly with a single flag.
