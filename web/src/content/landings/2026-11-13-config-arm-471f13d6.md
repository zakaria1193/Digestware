The declaration for `thumb_call_via_label` in `config/arm/arm.h` has been restricted. It is now only included when the `GENERATOR_FILE` and `USED_FOR_TARGET` macros are false, meaning it's relevant only during the build of GCC itself, not when cross-compiling for an ARM target.
