This commit introduces several standard operators for the `compl` (complement) operation on the `SIZETY` type within the Algol68 compiler. These operators, including equality, inequality, addition, subtraction, multiplication, division, and logical complement, are now defined in the `standard.a68` library. Consequently, these operator declarations have been removed from the compiler's prelude (`a68-parser-prelude.cc`) to avoid redundancy.
