The NEWS files for binutils, gas, and ld now include entries detailing the deprecation of the RISC-V privileged spec version selection. This change informs users that options like `-mpriv-spec` and `--with-priv-spec` are deprecated and will have no effect in future versions, directing them to use binutils 2.47 or earlier for older specifications.
