This commit enhances the align_fuzz function in final.cc by accepting an rtx_insn* argument, allowing it to work directly with instruction details. It also updates shorten_branches to maintain the alignment chain as rtx_insn* and uses label_ref_label to read ADDR_DIFF_VEC bounds, improving the precision of branch shortening and alignment logic.
