This commit continues the porting of AArch64 NEON SIMD intrinsics to GCC's pragma-based framework, focusing on lane-access operations like `vget_lane`, `vset_lane`, `vdup_lane`, and `vcopy_lane`. These changes consolidate intrinsic logic, simplifying the compiler's internal handling of NEON instructions. Old pragmas, macros, and unspec definitions related to these operations are removed, and new functions for shape checking are added.
