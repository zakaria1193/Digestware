An Internal Compiler Error (ICE) in the `simplify_binary_operation_1` function has been fixed. The issue occurred when simplifying `VEC_CONCAT` operations where `gen_lowpart` was called on an `UNSPEC` type, which it did not support. The fix replaces `gen_lowpart` with `rtl_hooks.gen_lowpart_no_emit`, which is safer and does not emit instructions, preventing the ICE and ensuring correct handling of vector concatenations involving specific machine instructions.
