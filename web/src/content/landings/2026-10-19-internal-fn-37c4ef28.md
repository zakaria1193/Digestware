This commit improves the handling of scalar `__builtin_clzg` and `__builtin_ctzg` calls when the argument is zero. Previously, the intrinsic expansion sometimes ignored the second argument (the expected value at zero), leading to incorrect results on certain targets or with specific compiler transformations. The fix introduces a check to compare the provided zero-value with the target's actual behavior, ensuring the correct result is emitted, particularly for targets like GCN and x86, and for bit-integer types.
