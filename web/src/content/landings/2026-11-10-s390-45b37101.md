The s390 assembler now supports `.insn` pseudo-mnemonics `rsy_a` and `rsy_b`, corresponding to variants of the RSY instruction introduced with the z990 processor. This update includes defining the instruction formats, documenting them, and adding a new z/Architecture test case.
