This commit corrects the behavior of aarch64 `vadd` family of intrinsics concerning signed integer overflow. Previously, they followed C's undefined behavior, but the ACLE standard mandates wraparound semantics. The fix involves casting operands to unsigned types before performing the addition, ensuring the specified wraparound behavior. Additionally, tests for floating-point addition intrinsics have been added.
