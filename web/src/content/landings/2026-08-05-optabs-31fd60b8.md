The `widen_ssum` and `widen_usum` optabs, exclusively used for implementing reduction accumulators in the vectorizer, have been renamed to `reduc_widen_ssum` and `reduc_widen_usum`. This change aligns them with other `reduc_*` names and frees up `widen_[us]sum` for potential future lane-preserving widening add operations. The internal optab identifiers were also adjusted for consistency. This is a pure rename, with no impact on generated code.
