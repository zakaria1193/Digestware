The `copy_coarray_desc_part` function, used for copying parts of coarray descriptors, has been moved from `trans-expr.cc` to `trans-descriptor.cc` and is now declared in `trans-descriptor.h`. This makes the function visible externally and prepares it for further refactoring, addressing PR122521.
