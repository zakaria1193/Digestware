This commit modifies the folding of AArch64 `vaba_` intrinsics to perform the accumulation in an unsigned type. Previously, for signed element types, the accumulation used signed types, leading to undefined overflow behavior and subsequent incorrect folding. By accumulating in unsigned types and then converting back, the compiler correctly models the intrinsic's wrapping behavior, preventing miscompilations and ensuring correct results, particularly in operations like `vshr_n_s8` after `vaba_s8`.
