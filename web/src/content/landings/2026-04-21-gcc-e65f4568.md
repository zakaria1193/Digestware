This commit merges the `exts_sve2` handling into `exts` in the AArch64 testsuite. This change is a partial revert of a previous commit and is motivated by the ability to enable SME without SVE2. The separate handling for `exts_sve2` is no longer necessary, simplifying the testsuite logic.
