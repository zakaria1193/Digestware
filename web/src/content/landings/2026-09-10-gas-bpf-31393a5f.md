This commit adds a new test case (`jump-gotol-cond-pseudoc.s`, `.d`, `.l`) to the BPF assembler test suite. The test verifies the handling of compound conditional jumps, specifically when they involve `gotol`. Previously, the assembler could silently misassemble these as unconditional jumps with invalid relocations due to ordering issues in the opcode table. The new test ensures that such cases are properly diagnosed or assembled correctly.
