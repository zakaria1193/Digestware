This commit enhances the `gimple-fold` pass by allowing callers to explicitly indicate whether comparisons should short-circuit. This is crucial for maintaining correct trapping semantics, especially with floating-point NaNs. The change modifies `combine_comparisons` to accept a comparison code argument, enabling accurate behavior for different contexts like reassoc, if-conversion, and predicate analysis, preventing incorrect loss or addition of exceptions.
