This commit adds a check to ensure that when initial equivalences are added to the frontier search, none of them are already present in the free list. This corrects an oversight in the `dom_oracle::start_search` function within `value-relation.cc`, preventing potential issues with redundant or invalid equivalences. A new test case (`pr127781.c`) has been added to verify this fix.
