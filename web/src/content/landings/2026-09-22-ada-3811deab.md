This commit addresses a spurious validity failure in the Ada compiler related to arrays with reverse scalar storage order that are converted to floating-point types. The compiler's expander already has logic to handle such cases with special care, but it was not applied in this specific conversion scenario. The change ensures that the 'Valid attribute correctly handles these conversions by introducing a predicate `Is_Reverse_Storage_Order_Object` to detect when a copy is necessary.
