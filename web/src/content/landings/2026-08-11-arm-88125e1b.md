A fix has been applied to the ARM assembler's expression parsing logic to address an uninitialized `exp.X_op` field. The initialization of the `expressionS` structure is now performed before the first return in the `my_get_expression` function. This ensures that all fields within the expression structure are properly initialized, preventing potential errors or undefined behavior during the parsing of ARM assembly expressions.
