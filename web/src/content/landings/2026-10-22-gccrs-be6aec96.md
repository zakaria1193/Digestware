This commit fixes an assertion error in Rust-GCC's intrinsic handler for `ctlz` (count leading zeros). The error occurred due to an incorrect comparison between a `tl::optional<tree>` and a `tree` after recent improvements to builtin lookup mechanisms. The fix ensures that the validity of the optional value is checked before comparison, preventing the assertion failure.
