This commit resolves an issue where GCC's re-associative optimization pass could enter a loop, generating thousands of multiplications when encountering expressions with both positive and negative power terms. The problem occurred because `stmt_is_power_of_op` incorrectly treated explicit factors as part of negative `pow` calls, leading to repeated removal of terms without actually reducing the `pow` exponent. The fix synchronizes `stmt_is_power_of_op` with `acceptable_pow_call`, ensuring that only terms correctly handled by `acceptable_pow_call` are considered for elimination, thus breaking the loop and fixing incorrect code generation.
