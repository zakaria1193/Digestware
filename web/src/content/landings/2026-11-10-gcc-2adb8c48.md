This commit resolves linking issues that occur when the host compiler is not GCC. In such cases, `hwint.h` declares `floor_log2` and related functions out-of-line, requiring `hwint.o` to be linked into generator programs and `libcommon`. Previously, `hwint.o` was only part of the general `OBJS` list, causing build failures.
