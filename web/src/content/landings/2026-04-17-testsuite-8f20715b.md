The testsuite was updated to accommodate targets that use short enums. Due to the fold optimization not applying to sizes smaller than a word, the test case ``match-shift-cmp-3`` produces different output on these targets. The expected results were adjusted to pass the test on such platforms, with the expectation that the optimization will be expanded to support shorter input values in the future.
