This commit introduces the necessary infrastructure within gccrs to recognize and process the `#[repr(transparent)]` attribute. It adds a variant to the `ReprKind` enum and modifies the type-checking logic to parse this attribute, enforcing that transparent structs or enums must have exactly one field. This lays the groundwork for the subsequent implementation of its compilation.
