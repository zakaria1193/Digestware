The `vec-insert-word-runnable.c` test on PowerPC big-endian systems was failing because it expected little-endian results for `vec_insertl` and `vec_inserth` intrinsics. This commit updates the test to use correct, endian-dependent expected values, resolving the issue on big-endian targets. The fix accounts for differences in byte and element numbering within registers between big-endian and little-endian architectures.
