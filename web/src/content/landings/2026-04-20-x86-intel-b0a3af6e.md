This commit fixes a regression in the x86 assembler that caused PC-relative expressions to be mishandled. The issue arose when fixups were associated with overly complex expressions, preventing the assembler from recognizing and converting expressions that were actually PC-relative. The fix modifies the logic for simplifying expressions to correctly identify PC-relative addresses.
