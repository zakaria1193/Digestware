This commit fixes an issue that prevented the assembler from detecting and converting PC-relative expressions in Intel syntax, particularly when converting O_index expressions.  The fix modifies `tc-i386-intel.c` to correctly handle complex expressions, including those with O_add or O_symbol, and accounts for S_GET_SEGMENT values.  New tests are also added to verify the fix.
