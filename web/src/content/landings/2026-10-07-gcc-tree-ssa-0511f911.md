This change moves the `ptr_info_def` pointer from a union within `ssa_name_info_type` into a dedicated `prange_storage` structure. This allows a complete pointer range (`prange`) to be stored with an SSA name, rather than just its non-null attribute, enabling more precise compiler analyses.
