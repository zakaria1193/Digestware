GCC's C++ parser previously accepted an incorrect syntax in initialization statements, specifically a stray colon after a 'using' declaration, by mistaking it for a range-based for loop syntax. This commit corrects the parser to properly handle such cases, ensuring adherence to the C++23 standard and providing more accurate error reporting by pointing to the 'using' keyword instead of the erroneous colon.
