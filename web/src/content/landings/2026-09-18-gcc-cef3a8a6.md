This commit refactors the range_from_dom routine in GIMPLE's range cache to be more efficient. Instead of performing separate walks for abnormal edges and inferred ranges, it now marks blocks with inferred ranges during the initial dominator tree traversal. The subsequent walk then integrates both inferred ranges and outgoing edge ranges, reducing overall computation and improving performance.
