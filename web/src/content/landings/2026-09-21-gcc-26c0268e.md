This commit corrects the `__has_builtin` operator to properly recognize the `__builtin_bitreverseg` and `__builtin_bswapg` magic keywords. These builtins were recently added, but support for checking their availability via `__has_builtin` was missing, leading to incorrect code generation or compilation errors in certain scenarios.
