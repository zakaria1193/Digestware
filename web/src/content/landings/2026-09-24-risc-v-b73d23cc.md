Previously, the RISC-V bare-metal ELF target (`riscv*-elf`) refused to generate shared libraries and Position-Independent Executables (PIE). This was due to specific configurations in `elf32lriscv-defs.sh` that withheld `GENERATE_SHLIB_SCRIPT` and `GENERATE_PIE_SCRIPT`. This commit removes those restrictions, aligning the target with other bare-metal ELF targets that support these features and do not explicitly set the `EMBEDDED` flag.
