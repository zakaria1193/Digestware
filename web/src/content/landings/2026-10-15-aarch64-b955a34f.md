The `PACM` instruction, part of the `FEAT_PAuth_LR` feature set but residing in the NOP (No Operation) space, has been added to the aarch64 assembler and disassembler. This means `PACM` can be assembled and disassembled without explicitly enabling the `+pauth-lr` feature. Associated tests have also been included.
