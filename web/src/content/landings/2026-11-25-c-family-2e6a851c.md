This commit addresses an issue in GCC's handling of atomic fetch-and-nand operations for _BitInt types. The previous implementation incorrectly applied bitwise logic, deviating from the documented behavior introduced in GCC 4.4. The fix ensures that the operations now correctly compute `~(*ptr & value)` instead of `(~*ptr) & value`, aligning with the standard.
