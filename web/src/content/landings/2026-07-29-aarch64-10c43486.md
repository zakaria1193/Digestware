This commit enhances the AArch64 vectorizer's accuracy by improving the costing of truncated stores. Previously, the vectorizer overestimated costs for scalar assignments that were effectively free as part of a store operation, potentially leading to incorrect vectorization. A new function, `vect_is_truncating_store`, is introduced to identify these cases, ensuring that code with truncating stores is not mis-vectorized.
