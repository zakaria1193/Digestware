This commit corrects the `noce` lifetime check to ignore debug instructions that mention variables but do not represent ordinary uses. Such debug-only payloads were inadvertently suppressing the `min`/`max` if-conversion optimization. By excluding these debug instructions from the overlap scan, the compiler can now properly apply if-conversion even when debug information is present.
