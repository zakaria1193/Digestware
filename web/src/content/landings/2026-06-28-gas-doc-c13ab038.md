The GAS assembler documentation for the AVR target has been significantly overhauled. This includes a complete rewrite of `gas/doc/c-avr.texi` and the addition of documentation for the `gs()` function, which is used for defining GCC-style inline assembly functions. This change improves the accuracy and completeness of the assembler's manual.
