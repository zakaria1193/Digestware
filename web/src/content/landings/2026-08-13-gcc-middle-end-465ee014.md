This commit refines vector conversion optimizations by addressing issues in `supportable_convert_operation` and `vectorizable_conversion`. It ensures that no-operation (NOP) conversions and sign-preserving conversions are correctly handled, preventing test suite failures. The changes allow `NOP_EXPR` and `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` for same-mode types, and modify `vectorizable_conversion` to prefer `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` for vector types, ensuring that these conversions are recognized and optimized appropriately without introducing incorrect behavior.
