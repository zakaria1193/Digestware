This change prevents the Alpha ELF linker from merging .got subsections after GPREL16 relocations are in place. Previous logic incorrectly allowed merges in a subsequent relaxation pass, leading to relocation errors. The fix adds a flag to track relaxation passes and inhibits merging once GPREL16 relocs exist.
