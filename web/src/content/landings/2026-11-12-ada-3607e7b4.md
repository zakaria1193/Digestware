This Ada compiler change optimizes the special expansion of `if_expression` by setting the `Cannot_Be_Superflat` flag. This applies to cases where a unidimensional unconstrained array has a result type, and its dependent expressions have constrained subtypes with known bounds. This special expansion avoids dynamic stack allocation and by setting the flag, further aids in optimizing subsequent processing of these types.
