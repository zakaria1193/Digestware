primer_expert: The `phiopt` pass in `tree-ssa-phiopt.cc` now implements `replicate_conds_over_phis`.
  This pass identifies patterns where a PHI node merges boolean results of comparisons
  (e.g., `t_1 = PHI <t_9(3), t_6(4)>` where `t_9` and `t_6` are comparison results),
  and replicates the subsequent conditional test across the predecessors of the PHI.
  This avoids the overhead of merging comparisons into a single PHI before testing.

  loop invariants, leaving loop-related cases to the DOM pass's jump threading.

This commit reintroduces an optimization in the `phiopt` pass that replicates conditional logic over a PHI node formed by comparisons. This optimization was lost during a rewrite of GCC's jump threading machinery but was partially recovered by the DOM (Dominator) pass. The `phiopt` pass now handles these cases directly, improving performance by avoiding unnecessary conditional merges. The optimization is deliberately not applied to loop boundaries to preserve loop structure, though the DOM pass's version remains available for such cases. This change recovers over 94% of the joins previously handled by DOM's special case.
