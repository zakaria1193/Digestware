This commit enhances the vectorizer on x86-64 to efficiently convert between `_Float16` and `float` vectors, even when AVX512FP16 is not fully supported. It achieves this by enabling existing pack and unpack expanders for various vector modes using F16C and AVX512VL instructions. New specific expanders and adjustments to existing ones ensure that conversions, including different lane counts, are vectorized using the most appropriate instructions available, preventing scalar fallback and improving performance.
