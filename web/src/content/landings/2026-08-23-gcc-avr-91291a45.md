This commit resolves an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) in the AVR backend's fuse-move RTL pass. The ICE occurred when an optimization attempted to replace a libgcc call for multiplication with a constant, but failed to account for all registers clobbered by the original libgcc call. The fix ensures the optimization pass correctly identifies and ignores all such clobbered registers, passing tests and fixing an ICE for targets lacking hardware multiplication.
