Libstdc++ has improved `inplace_vector`'s `constexpr` support by implementing the remaining aspects of the P3726R2 standard, specifically concerning union lifetime rules. It now uses `std::start_lifetime` in `_M_init` and utilizes `__builtin_constexpr_diag` for better compile-time error messages. This change also removes an older optimization-hindering approach and ensures better compatibility with compiler behavior regarding union member writes.
