This optimization in `match.pd` combines two vector reductions that feed into the same scalar operation into a single reduction of an elementwise operation. This transformation, `REDUC(a) op REDUC(b) -> REDUC(a op b)`, trades a potentially expensive lane-crossing reduction for a cheaper elementwise operation, significantly reducing instruction count for operations like MIN, MAX, AND, IOR, XOR, and floating-point sums.
