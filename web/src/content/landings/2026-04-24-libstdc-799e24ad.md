The `basic_const_iterator` in libstdc++ now provides the `iterator_type` member, conforming to the resolution of LWG issue 4253 from the C++ standards committee. This provides a way to refer to the iterator type that `basic_const_iterator` is wrapping.  This change ensures standard compliance and may be required for certain template metaprogramming techniques.
