This commit significantly reduces GCC's build time on the s390 architecture by optimizing how scheduler descriptions are processed, particularly impacting partial rebuilds. The bottleneck was identified in `genattrtab`, where complex attribute definitions led to massive intermediate expression (RTX) structures. The changes refactor the construction of these expressions, moving from a right-biased insertion that leads to O(n^2) complexity to a more efficient method that avoids quadratic blowup in expression simplification and insertion.
