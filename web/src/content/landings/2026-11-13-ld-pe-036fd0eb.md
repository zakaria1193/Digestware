This commit modifies the PE linker to correctly handle relocation types such as `R_*_ABS` and `R_*_NONE`. Previously, the linker would error out when encountering these relocation types. Now, they are ignored and can be used for various purposes without causing a linker failure. This change also includes new test cases to verify this behavior.
