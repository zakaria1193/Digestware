This commit adds constant folding support for the SSE4.2 CRC32 intrinsics (`_mm_crc32_u8`, `_mm_crc32_u16`, `_mm_crc32_u32`, `_mm_crc32_u64`) on the i386 architecture. It leverages the `calculate_reversed_crc` function with the CRC32C polynomial to precompute results for constant arguments. Additionally, it removes an assertion in `hwint.cc` that previously restricted folding when `crc_bits` was greater than or equal to `data_bits`, enabling folding for `_mm_crc32_u64`.
