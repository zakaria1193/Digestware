This commit introduces validation for subregister replacements within the Common Subexpression Elimination (CSE) pass. This prevents the generation of invalid RTL (Register Transfer Language) that could lead to compiler crashes, specifically addressing an ICE (Internal Compiler Error) reported on the SH architecture.  The change ensures that CSE verifies the subregisters it creates and tries to simplify them down to hard registers.
