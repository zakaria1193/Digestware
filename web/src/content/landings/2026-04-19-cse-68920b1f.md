The commit fixes an ICE caused by CSE replacing a pseudo register with a hard register in a way that resulted in an invalid subreg.  The fix ensures that CSE validates subreg replacements, even those that appear to be register-for-register, and simplifies them to hard registers where possible. A new test case `pr124643.c` was added to verify the fix.
