This commit addresses a PR related to an ICE in the CSE pass caused by an invalid subregister replacement. The CSE pass was attempting to replace a pseudo register with a hard register (T) within a `zero_extend` expression involving a subregister. This commit modifies `canon_reg` to handle and canonicalize subregisters, validating replacements and preventing the generation of invalid RTL.
