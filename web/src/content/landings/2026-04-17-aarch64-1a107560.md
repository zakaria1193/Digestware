This commit addresses minor issues in the AArch64 narrow-gp-writes optimization pass. It removes redundant checks for SUBREG and TRUNCATE, prevents infinite recursion in `narrow_dimode_src` when an operand remains DImode, and uses `HOST_WIDE_INT_PRINT_HEX` for printing masks. These changes improve the reliability and efficiency of the optimization.
