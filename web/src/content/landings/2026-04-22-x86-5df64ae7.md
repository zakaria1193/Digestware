The x86 common subexpression elimination (CSE) pass now eliminates redundant thread-local storage (TLS) calls even when SSE2 is disabled. Previously, a check for SSE2 prevented this optimization in SSE-only builds. This change removes that check in `x86_cse::gate`, enabling TLS optimization regardless of SSE2 status, and adds a new test case.
