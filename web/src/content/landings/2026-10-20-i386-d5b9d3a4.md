This commit fixes a typo in the `__enclv_generic` macro in the Intel SGX intrinsics header (`sgxintrin.h`). The typo caused the `bx` register's value to be stored into the same macro argument (`__D[0]`) as the `cx` register's value, instead of `bx` going to `__D[0]` and `cx` to `__D[1]`. This correction ensures that the macro correctly handles register assignments for Intel SGX enclave entry/exit operations.
