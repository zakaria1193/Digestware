The H8 port incorrectly calculated instruction lengths, leading to mispredicted branches and potential assembler issues. Specifically, the length of the `xor.l` instruction was not fully accounted for in some cases, and certain SImode sequences required additional instructions or longer `xor.l` forms. This commit corrects these calculations and also fixes incorrect output templates and missing '%' characters, resolving a PR and preventing regressions.
