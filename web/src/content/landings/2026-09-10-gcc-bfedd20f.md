The internal function `__builtin_assume` (marked with `IFN_ASSUME`) was incorrectly marked as `const`, implying it had no side effects and did not read global memory. This commit changes its attribute to `pure`, which allows reading global memory. This corrects analysis inaccuracies, especially when the `assume` body references fields of structures, even though the body itself is not executed.
