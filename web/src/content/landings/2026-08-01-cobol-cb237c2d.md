This commit refactors the `int256` structure used in `libgcobol`'s `gmath.cc` for fixed-point arithmetic. The `rdigits` member, representing the number of digits to the right of the decimal point, is now integrated into the structure itself. This change simplifies operations by carrying the scale information with the number, reducing the need for separate scale management logic in functions like addition, subtraction, and multiplication.
