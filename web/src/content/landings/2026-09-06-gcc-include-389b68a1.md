This commit corrects a potential parsing issue in the sdiv_qrnnd macros within longlong.h. Previously, arguments containing commas or assignments could be misparsed due to a lack of parentheses. This fix ensures correct parsing for i370/s390 and VAX architectures, addressing a latent bug that could have led to incorrect behavior. The change mirrors a similar fix in libgcrypt.
