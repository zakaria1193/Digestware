The transitive relation table in `value-relation.cc` has been corrected to accurately reflect the transitive properties of equality and inequality. Previously, it failed to deduce `x != z` from `x == y` and `y != z`, or from `x != y` and `y == z`. This update ensures proper propagation of these relations.
