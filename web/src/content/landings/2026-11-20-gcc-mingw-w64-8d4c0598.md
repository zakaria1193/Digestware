The mingw-w64 compiler configuration incorrectly undefined the `_REENTRANT` macro when the `-pthread` flag was used, despite pthreads being enabled. This could lead to threading issues. This commit modifies the `CPP_SPEC` to ensure `_REENTRANT` is always defined when pthreads are requested, whether by default or explicitly via `-pthread`, and removes an unnecessary empty spec for `SPEC_PTHREAD2` to prevent potential misuse.
