This commit resolves an issue in `constexpr` evaluation where `break` statements within expansion statements were mishandled. The compiler incorrectly processed these breaks, leading to incorrect constant evaluation results or failures, such as misinterpreting a `break` as a `return` or causing outer loops to terminate prematurely. The fix involves wrapping the instantiated bodies of expansion statements in an artificial `do...while(0)` loop, ensuring that `break` statements correctly target the intended loop scope within the `constexpr` context.
