This change ensures that the masks associated with shift counts are preserved when rewriting a shift count as a complement on RISC-V architectures. Previously, dropping these masks could lead to incorrect code generation when later optimizations selected XLEN-wide instructions. The fix applies to both native and word splitters, maintaining existing masked shift patterns and adding runtime tests to verify correctness.
