This commit fixes an issue in gccrs where extern crates were not being inserted at the beginning of the extern prelude, but rather at the end. The change modifies the Crate::inject_extern_crate function to ensure correct placement. This resolves potential confusion and unexpected behavior by properly integrating external crates into the prelude namespaces.
