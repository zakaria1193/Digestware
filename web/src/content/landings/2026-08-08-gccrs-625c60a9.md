gccrs has been updated to support the compilation of `let` statements that utilize struct patterns. This enables destructuring of struct fields directly into variables, improving ergonomics for Rust developers. The changes involve modifications in `rust-compile-var-decl.h` and `rust-compile-pattern.cc`.
