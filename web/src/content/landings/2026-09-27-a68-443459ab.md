The Algol68 compiler frontend has refactored its context management by removing the `A68_GLOBAL_CONTEXT` macro and associated `get_global_context` function. These have been replaced by the more language-specific `a68_range_context` function. This change corrects an issue where the builtin initialization was incorrectly using the global context through the `pushdecl` function, ensuring a more accurate and consistent handling of contexts during compilation.
