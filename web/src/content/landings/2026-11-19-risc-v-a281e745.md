This commit enables the use of the `zext.h` pattern for unsigned halfword extensions on the RISC-V architecture when the Zbkb extension is active. It ensures that the compiler correctly utilizes the `pack` and `packw` instructions for this purpose and accurately accounts for the single instruction cost in register extension operations.
