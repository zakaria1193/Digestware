This commit resolves Local Register Allocation (LRA) spill failures on the PA-RISC architecture that involve the %r1 register. Redundant clobbers on %r1 in call patterns were removed, and PIC label address patterns were merged and fixed. Fallback alternatives using general registers and frame marker slots were added, along with post-reload peephole optimizations to rewrite reloads back to %r1. The `TARGET_PREFERRED_RELOAD_CLASS` is updated to prefer %r1 for certain loads, and register move costs are adjusted to allow LRA to use floating-point registers as an alternate spill area.
