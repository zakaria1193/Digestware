Fixes a bug in C++ reflection where access checks were not consistently disabled during dependent splice processing.  Specifically, the call to `build_offset_ref` in `tsubst_splice_expr` was missing the necessary disabling of access checks, leading to erroneous compilation failures. The fix adds `push_` and `pop_deferring_access_checks` around the call to `build_offset_ref` in `tsubst_splice_expr`, mirroring the existing logic in `cp_parser_splice_expression`. Addresses PR124989.
