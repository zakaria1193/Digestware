This commit enhances the LoongArch ELF backend in binutils by adding a check to reject any non-LoongArch ELF inputs during the relocation process. This prevents potential errors and misinterpretations when the linker attempts to handle object files not intended for the LoongArch architecture, addressing a reported bug.
