Checks for dangling references in std::pair and std::tuple constructors are now enabled by default, not just in debug mode. This change makes it ill-formed to create pairs or tuples with dangling references, preventing potential undefined behavior. Programs that previously relied on such constructs in unexecuted code paths may need to be updated.
