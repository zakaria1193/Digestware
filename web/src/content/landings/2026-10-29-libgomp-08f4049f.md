The GCN plugin for libgomp now rounds its device thread stack allocation stride to an odd multiple of 4 kiB, avoiding performance degradation caused by power-of-two strides. Previously, a 32 kiB stride on certain AMD GPUs caused memory conflicts, as specific stack offsets mapped to the same hardware resources across different wavefronts. This change improves bandwidth in memory-intensive kernels, such as BabelStream's dot product, by up to 13% (500 GB/s to 560 GB/s) and offers minor improvements for other kernels.
