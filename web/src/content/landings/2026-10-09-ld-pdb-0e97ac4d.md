Symbol information in PDB files, specifically within S_ENVBLOCK records, requires a null-terminated string list. Previously, the linker omitted this terminator. This omission could lead to readers (like llvm-pdbutil) running past the end of the record when the strings happened to align on a 4-byte boundary, causing stream errors. The change adds the necessary null terminator.
