This commit introduces a comprehensive suite of test cases for the RISC-V vwmaccu.vv vector instruction, specifically targeting register group overlap. A variety of data types (u8, u16, u32) and different mask/element counts (m1, m2, m4, mf2, mf4, mf8) are covered to ensure the instruction behaves correctly when destination and source registers overlap.
