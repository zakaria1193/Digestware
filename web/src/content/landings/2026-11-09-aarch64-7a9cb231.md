This commit improves AArch64 vectorization of reduction operations, particularly those involving left folds. Previously, the compiler's cost model did not always accurately account for the serial chain of additions created by fold-left reductions, which share a single accumulator. By classifying these reductions as having a single def-use cycle, the compiler can better estimate their latency. This allows it to select more appropriate vectorization modes, such as `VNx4QI` for byte-element vectors, which avoids unnecessary widening operations and potentially improves performance.
