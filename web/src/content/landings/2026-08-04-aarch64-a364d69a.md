The `vabd` and `vaba` families of NEON intrinsics for the AArch64 architecture have been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework. This change standardizes their implementation and includes assembly tests for all variants. Consequently, several `__builtin_aarch64_fabd` and `__builtin_aarch64_uabd` built-in functions are now obsolete and have been removed.
