The `GlobbingVisitor` in gccrs has been updated to handle 'ribs' by copying existing definitions rather than always creating new ones from the AST. This change refactors the import resolution process, making `GlobbingVisitor` more efficient and accurate when dealing with nested modules and imports. It removes redundant member functions and introduces new ones focused on visiting and globbing definitions within these 'ribs'.
