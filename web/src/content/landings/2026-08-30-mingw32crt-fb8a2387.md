The `i686-mingw32crt` target, which supported the MinGW runtime, has been removed from GCC. This target was found to be unmaintained, unbuildable due to a persistent typo, and no longer used by anyone. Its removal streamlines the compiler's configuration tree and reduces maintenance overhead.
