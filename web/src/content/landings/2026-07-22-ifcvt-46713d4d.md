GCC's ifcvt pass previously used three separate RTL optimization passes with near-identical logic. This change consolidates them into a single pass, `pass_rtl_ifcvt`, parameterized by an `ifcvt_phase` enum. This simplifies the codebase by removing duplication while maintaining the existing pipeline and behavior.
