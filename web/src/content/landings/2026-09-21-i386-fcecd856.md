This commit corrects the CPU feature requirements for GFNI V32QImode rotates. Previously, these operations were incorrectly enabled with just AVX, leading to compiler internal errors (ICEs) because AVX alone does not support the necessary V32QImode shifts. The change mandates AVX2, aligning with the actual hardware capabilities and resolving PR127498.
