This change introduces validation for relaxation deletion ranges in thirteen simple ELF backends within the BFD library. It utilizes `_bfd_elf_relax_delete_range_ok` to ensure the integrity of deletion operations, preventing potential memory corruption. New shared test helpers and regression tests for CRX and H8/300 architectures are added to verify these checks.
