This commit corrects the operand validation for LoongArch's `lsx_vbitrevi` instructions. Previously, these instructions incorrectly used a predicate designed for branch offsets (`const_lsx_branch_operand`) to validate their immediate values. The immediate for `vbitrevi` represents a bit index, and its valid range depends on the element size (3-6 bits). By switching to the correct `const_<bitimm>_operand` predicate, the compiler now accurately checks the immediate range, preventing out-of-range values from being emitted and causing assembler errors.
