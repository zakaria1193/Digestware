This commit reverts a change that caused incorrect parsing of the `__has_include` preprocessor directive when used outside of its intended context. The previous modification introduced non-determinism and incorrect error handling, leading to uninitialized variables and confusing diagnostic messages. The fix ensures that `__has_include` and similar built-ins are handled robustly, even when misused, and also removes a problematic function that consumed `CPP_EOF` tokens.
