Fortran's ASSOCIATE construct has a restriction (F2023:C1105) that its selector cannot be a designator of a procedure pointer or a function returning one. Previously, the compiler incorrectly allowed function references returning procedure pointers to proceed, leading to an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) when verification checks were enabled. This patch hoists the check to correctly diagnose these cases, preventing the ICE and adhering to the Fortran standard.
