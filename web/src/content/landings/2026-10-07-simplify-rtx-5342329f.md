A bug in `simplify_logical_relational_operation` caused incorrect floating-point trap behavior when combining comparisons involving NaNs and `-ftrapping-math`. The simplification could either add a trap where none existed or remove an existing trap. This change corrects the behavior by ensuring the combined comparison preserves the trapping semantics of the original comparisons, particularly concerning quiet NaNs.
