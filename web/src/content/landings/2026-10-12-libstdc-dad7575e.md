This change addresses an issue in `std::chrono::time_zone::get_info` where very negative timestamps could lead to incorrect calculations when subtracting `info.begin`. This resulted in a negative value being passed to `vector::reserve`, causing a `length_error`. The fix clamps the timestamp to prevent this issue and also modifies `vector::reserve` to use `size_t` for improved robustness against potential future negative values.
