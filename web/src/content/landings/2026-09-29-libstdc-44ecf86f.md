This change updates `std::ranges::repeat_view` to properly handle integer-like types, including unsigned ones, as bounds for the range. Previously, unsigned integer types were rejected because they did not meet certain C++20 concepts, and the calculation of the difference type was flawed for these bounds. This fix implements LWG 3875, ensuring that `repeat_view` behaves correctly with a wider range of integer types.
