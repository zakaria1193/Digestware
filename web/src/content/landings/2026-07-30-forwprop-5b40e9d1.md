A new forward propagation pass, `simplify_phi_result_movdiv`, is introduced to simplify division and modulo operations where the divisor is a power of two within PHI nodes. It transforms DIV into RSHIFT and MOD into BIT_AND, provided certain conditions on the operands are met. This change resolves most use cases for PR101179 by enabling these simplifications for positive power-of-two divisors and specific conditions on the PHI node's arguments.
