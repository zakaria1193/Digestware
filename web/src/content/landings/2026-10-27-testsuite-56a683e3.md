Two GCC testsuite programs, `time-profiler-2.c` and `gcov-pr86536.c`, now correctly wait for their forked child processes to complete. Previously, the parent process exited without waiting, which could lead to the child being killed by a SIGHUP signal before writing profile data. This caused failures in time profiling and incorrect line counts in gcov, particularly under simulators or specific pty configurations.
