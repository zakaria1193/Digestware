The test case `pr128080.c` exhibits slow performance at the `-O0` optimization level on certain targets, particularly ARMv6s-m. The lengthy compilation time, caused by extensive shuffle operations surviving to RTL, leads to test timeouts. To mitigate this, the test is now skipped when `-O0` is used, ensuring test suite execution remains within reasonable time limits without affecting correctness for optimized code.
