The order of extension definitions in `bfd/elfxx-riscv.c` has been corrected to ensure that implied extensions are processed correctly. The `xsmtvdotii` extension, which implies `xsmtvdot`, was listed after `xsmtvdot`. This commit swaps their order so that `xsmtvdotii` now correctly implies `xsmtvdot` and its own dependencies, such as `zve32x`. A test case has also been added to verify this behavior.
