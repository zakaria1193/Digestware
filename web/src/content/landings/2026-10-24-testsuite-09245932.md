The diagnostic-cfgs.c test, which generates control flow graphs (CFGs), fails on systems without the Graphviz 'dot' tool. The compiler framework attempts to execute the missing binary, leading to cascading errors that are incorrectly flagged as test failures. This patch adds a `dg-prune-output` directive to ignore these specific 'dot' execution errors, allowing the test to pass gracefully on minimal build environments.
