This commit enables the vectorizer to leverage non-wrapping bounds derived from data-reference (DR) analysis. It integrates this information by collecting the minimum bounds and ANDing a new assumption (`niter < bounds_min`) into the existing iteration assumptions. This change also involves reordering DR analysis to occur earlier in the loop analysis pipeline, allowing for better reuse of iteration count checks and dumps.
