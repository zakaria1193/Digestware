GCC's match pass can now expose constant factors hidden by signed widening conversions in multiplication and division chains. This allows for more aggressive optimization, transforming expressions like `(long long) (a * 30) * b / 6` into `((long long) a * b) * 5`. The optimization applies to signed integral types with undefined overflow and non-narrowing conversions, with specific checks for division and multiplication to ensure correctness and prevent overflow.
