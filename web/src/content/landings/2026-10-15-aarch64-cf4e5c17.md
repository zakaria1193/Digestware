On AArch64, handling of vector operations (vops), particularly those involving floating-point exceptions, has been moved to the main `gimple_folder::fold` routine. This change aims to simplify the logic and improve robustness by ensuring vops are correctly managed whether folding occurs before or during SSA form, and by properly handling cases where vops might be forgotten or lead to ICEs (Internal Compiler Errors), especially with `-ftrapping-math`.
