This change prevents the RISC-V compiler from inserting vsetvl configurations on edges that are part of shared jump tables. Previously, such insertions could occur on edges from shared jump-table dispatches, leading to an ICE when the jump table was split for one dispatch but used by others. The fix ensures local configurations are maintained in blocks with incoming shared-table edges, resolving an ICE and allowing a new test case to pass.
