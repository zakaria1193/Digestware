This commit addresses bugs related to shadow variables used with type specifications in Fortran's `DO CONCURRENT` and `FORALL` statements. It ensures that each shadow variable gets a unique name, records the shadowed outer symbol, and prevents incorrect substitution into nested constructs that shadow the same symbol. This improves the robustness and correctness of Fortran's handling of these constructs.
