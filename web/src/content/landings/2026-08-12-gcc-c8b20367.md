This commit refines `scanasm` instruction pattern matching in the testsuite for x86 on Darwin systems. Previously, patterns could incorrectly match content within assembler directives or debug frame sections. The fix ensures that matches are more precise by explicitly including or excluding surrounding whitespace and characters, preventing false positives and improving test accuracy.
