This commit ports various NEON shift intrinsics that operate with a vector as the shift amount (e.g., vshl, vrshl, vqshl) to the pragma-based framework on aarch64. This refactoring centralizes the handling of these SIMD operations, removing them from direct expansion in aarch64-builtins.cc and consolidating their definitions within the pragma framework. The change also cleans up associated .def files and removes no-longer-called builtins.
