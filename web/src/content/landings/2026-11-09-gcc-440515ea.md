This commit modernizes the management of instruction locations within GCC's RTL (Register Transfer Language). It replaces the older `INSN_LOCATION` and `INSN_HAS_LOCATION` macros with new member functions (`location`, `set_location`, `has_location`) directly on the `rtx_insn` structure. This change improves code clarity and consistency across various compiler passes and target backends.
