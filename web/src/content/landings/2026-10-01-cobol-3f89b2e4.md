GCC's COBOL runtime now supports filenames that begin with a dollar sign (`$`). This behavior mimics other COBOL compilers, where such a prefix causes the runtime to look for the actual filename in an environment variable. The change involves copying relevant subroutines from GnuCOBOL and includes necessary adaptations for GCC's internal file handling. Some compiler warnings and unused variables are also addressed.
