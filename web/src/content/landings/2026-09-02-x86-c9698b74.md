GCC's x86 vectorizer has refactored how it costs moves between general-purpose registers (GPRs) and XMM registers during vector constructor operations. Previously, this costing was performed in a less efficient manner within the `add_stmt_cost` hook, potentially leading to redundant calculations. The change moves this costing logic to the `add_slp_cost` overload, which is invoked once per SLP node, eliminating duplicate costing and improving the accuracy of cost estimations for vectorization.
