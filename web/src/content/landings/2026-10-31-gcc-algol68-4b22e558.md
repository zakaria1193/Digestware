This patch enhances the Algol 68 compiler support by adding GNU extensions, including `word compl` operators, to the `libga68` library. A new `GNU` module is introduced, and prelude sources are processed by `sppp`. The changes ensure that extended standard modes are always built, even when using strict language standards, as they are required by `a68_decode_modes`. The `find_operator` function is also corrected to properly handle standard power operators.
