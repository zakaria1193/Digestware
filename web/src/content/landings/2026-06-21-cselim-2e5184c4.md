This commit introduces a compile-time optimization in GCC's Conditional Store Elimination (cselim) pass. Previously, after inserting new load and store statements, the virtual uses and definitions (`vops`) were not immediately updated, requiring a later register renaming pass to perform this work. By manually updating the `vuse` for the new load and both `vuse`/`vdef` for the new store, the compiler avoids redundant processing, leading to a small but measurable reduction in compilation time.
