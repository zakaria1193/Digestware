This change ensures that the Ada representation of the `timespec` structure matches its C counterpart on 32-bit big-endian systems, specifically addressing issues on Glibc platforms. A bug in `gcc/ada/s-oscons-tmplt.c` caused an incorrect size calculation for `SIZEOF_tv_nsec` when targeting these architectures. This adjustment aligns the structures, preventing potential data corruption or incorrect time handling in Ada programs that interface with C time functions.
