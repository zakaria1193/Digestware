This change integrates AArch64 NEON widen and narrow intrinsics, including saturating versions, into GCC's pragma-based framework. Previously, these operations were handled through separate, now-deleted macros and builtins. The new approach standardizes their implementation, making them more manageable and consistent with other NEON intrinsics.
