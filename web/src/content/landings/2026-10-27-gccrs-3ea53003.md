GCC's Rust front-end (gccrs) has been updated to include a new intrinsic function, `ptr_offset_from<T>`, which allows for calculating the offset between two pointers. This intrinsic is now registered and handled within the backend and type-checking components of gccrs. A new test case has been added to verify its functionality.
