The `:c` qualifier has been removed from the plus operation within the `mul_cross_sum` pattern in `match.pd`. This qualifier indicated a condition that was already implicitly handled by the pattern's structure, making it unnecessary. While this change slightly reduces the generated code from `genmatch`, it does not alter the final compiled output.
