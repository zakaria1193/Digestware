Previously, vectorized `floor`, `ceil`, and `trunc` instructions on x86 were disabled when the `flag_trapping_math` option was active. This commit removes that restriction, allowing the use of packed vector instructions (e.g., `vroundps`) which are compliant with ISO 60559 standards regarding exceptions for NaN inputs. The scalar 'round' function still requires `flag_trapping_math` to be disabled due to its use of a floating-point add operation.
