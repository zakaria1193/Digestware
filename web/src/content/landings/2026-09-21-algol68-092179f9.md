This change addresses a bug in the Algol68 compiler where imported modes from 'revelations' were not correctly processed if they were equivalent to standard modes. Due to how these imported identifiers were synthesized with dummy nodes, the `compute_derived_modes` pass didn't handle their equivalences, which are normally compared by pointer. The fix ensures that mode equivalences are resolved for these imported identifiers, allowing the compiler to correctly understand and use them, especially when interacting with modules.
