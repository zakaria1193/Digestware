A typo in `cp_fold_r` prevented the compiler from correctly checking `ff_genericize`, leading to incorrect behavior with consteval functions, arrays, and modules. This commit fixes the typo, ensuring correct compilation in these cases. The fix also adds the missing `-O` flag to a test case.
