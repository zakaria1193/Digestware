The `ranges::distance` function in libstdc++ now includes an optimization for segmented iterators. For iterators that span multiple contiguous segments (like those used in `std::deque` or certain `ranges` views), `ranges::distance` is now calculated by summing the distances of each individual segment. This improves performance for operations involving such iterators.
