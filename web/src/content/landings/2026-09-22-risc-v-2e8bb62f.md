The RISC-V vector extension (RVV) now permits register overlap for dual-widening floating-point instructions, including vfwadd.vv, vfwsub.vv, and vfwmul.vv. This change is accompanied by new test cases in the testsuite to verify correct handling of register group overlaps for various floating-point types and vector mask configurations.
