The `mkoffload` utility for amdgcn and nvptx targets now uses `read_collect_gcc_options` instead of `getenv` to read `COLLECT_GCC_OPTIONS`. This change ensures safer handling of environment variables used to pass compiler options, consistent with recent driver modifications that spill long option strings to response files. While these specific instances only checked for the variable's existence, the update prevents potential future issues and maintains uniformity.
