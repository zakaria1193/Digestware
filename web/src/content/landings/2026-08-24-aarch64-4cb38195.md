The NEON saturating add and subtract intrinsics, vqadd and vqsub, along with their scalar variants (e.g., vqaddb, vqsubh), have been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework for aarch64. The patch also enhances the gimple_ifn handler to automatically convert single-element vector modes (V1XX) to their corresponding scalar XX modes for processing, mirroring existing behavior in arm_neon.h and reusing scalar patterns without impacting codegen quality.
