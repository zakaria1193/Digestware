This commit corrects a pattern matching rule in GCC's tree-optimization passes. Previously, the rule for `1 / X` when `X` was positive was not correctly identified as equivalent to `X == 1`. This change uses `tree_expr_nonnegative_p` to ensure signed positive values are handled, leading to more accurate optimization of division operations.
