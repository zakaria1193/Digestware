GCC and libstdc++ now implement the C++26 P3726R2 paper, adjusting union lifetime rules. This includes a new builtin for `std::start_lifetime` that operates on pointers, and improvements to `reduced_constant_expression_p` to correctly handle union-nested arrays and ignored holes. The changes ensure correct constant evaluation and object lifetime management in C++26, particularly for unions.
