GCC's frange optimization pass has been enhanced to support two sub-ranges, up from one. This change allows for better representation of inequalities such as non-zero values, which were previously missed. With two sub-ranges, the optimization now captures 99.63% of ranges encountered in a Fortran corpus, significantly closing the gap with internal tables used by DOM. This improvement is expected to fix several open PRs related to nonzero folding and signed zero optimizations, while also yielding modest gains in jump threading and constant propagation opportunities.
