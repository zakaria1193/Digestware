A bug in libgfortran caused incorrect positioning after formatted STREAM reads that extended beyond the end of a record. The unit position was advanced by the record terminator length for each subsequent item, leading to wrong POS= values and incorrect starting points for later reads or writes. This change corrects the counting of the record terminator, ensuring accurate positioning.
