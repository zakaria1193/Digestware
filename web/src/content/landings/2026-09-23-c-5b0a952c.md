This commit resolves an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) that occurred during error recovery for the `__builtin_is_within_lifetime` intrinsic in C++. The issue arose when `error_mark_node` appeared unexpectedly within constructors, causing assertions to fail. The fix simplifies error recovery by returning `error_mark_node` directly in such cases.
