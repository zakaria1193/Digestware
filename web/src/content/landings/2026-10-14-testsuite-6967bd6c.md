This commit adjusts GCC's testsuite to preserve Dead Store Elimination (DSE) coverage for character arrays and enums. The character-array test now correctly asserts that DSE removes a whole-object clear overwritten by a string assignment, including implicit trailing zeros. An enum variant is moved to a separate test to maintain its specific DSE assertion.
