This change updates libstdc++'s common_iterator to align with the C++ standard library's LWG 3749 resolution. It modifies iterator_traits for common_iterator to correctly determine the iterator category, ensuring compatibility and proper behavior when dealing with different iterator and sentinel types. The changes involve adding a new helper, `__detail::__common_iterator_iter_cat`, and updating the `iterator_traits` definition.
