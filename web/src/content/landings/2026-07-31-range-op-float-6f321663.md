This commit improves the handling of infinity in reverse floating-point operations, addressing miscompilations that occurred with specific range analyses. Previously, ranges like `[-inf, -inf]` were incorrectly propagated in reverse operations, leading to wrong results when converting between floating-point types. The fix now correctly widens the bounds of infinity ranges by 1 unit in the penultimate representation, ensuring accurate results for conversions and other operations.
