This commit optimizes the handling of whole-register moves in the RISC-V Vector extension. Previously, the `vsetvl` pass could unnecessarily insert `vsetivli` instructions before and after moves, even when a suitable register configuration was already available. This change allows whole-register moves to be represented as demands that are satisfied by any legal `VTYPE` configuration, rather than requiring specific `SEW`, `LMUL`, or `VL` constraints. This avoids redundant `vsetvl` operations and simplifies the instruction sequence for these moves.
