Reverts a previous fix for PR41127 to improve error diagnostics when parsing enums in C++. The reverted change re-enables parsing of the enum-base (the underlying type of the enum) after encountering a colon in an enum specifier, which allows for better detection of errors related to invalid underlying types. This addresses PR118374 and improves the compiler's ability to diagnose incorrect enum declarations.
