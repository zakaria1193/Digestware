A previous fix allowed bitfield widths to follow `enum identifier :`, but this is not standard C++. This commit reverts that change to enforce the standard, where a colon after `enum identifier` indicates an enum-base, not a bitfield width. This improves diagnostics for incorrect underlying types in enums.
