Reverts a previous change that relaxed enum parsing in order to support bitfield widths after an enum identifier and a colon (e.g. `enum identifier :`). The ISO C++ standard requires that the colon in this context is parsed as an enum-base, not as part of a bitfield declaration. This change improves diagnostics for incorrect enum declarations by eagerly parsing the enum-base.
