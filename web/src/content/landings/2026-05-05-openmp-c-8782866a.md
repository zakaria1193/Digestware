This change removes the `PRAGMA_OMP_ALLOCATE` case from the `cp_parser_omp_construct` function. The OpenMP `allocate` directive is not considered a construct and should not be handled by this specific parser function. While this change is not expected to have a functional impact as the case was likely never correctly handled, it serves to clarify intent and prevent potential future misuse of the parser.
