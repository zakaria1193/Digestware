This change refactors the `ppc32elf.em` linker script to conditionally define and use the `old_got` variable. Previously, `old_got` was always defined, leading to unused variable warnings with newer compilers on VxWorks targets. It is now restricted to non-VxWorks builds, resolving the warning and correctly handling the `--sdata-got` option's absence for VxWorks.
