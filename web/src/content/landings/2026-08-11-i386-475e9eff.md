This commit prevents the use of APX EGPRs with FMA4 and XOP instructions on i386. These instructions are VEX-only and cannot be promoted to EVEX form. The change modifies instruction patterns to use a 'jm' constraint instead of 'm' and adds the 'gpr16' attribute to the address, thereby avoiding EGPR usage in specific scenarios and resolving an unencodable instruction issue.
