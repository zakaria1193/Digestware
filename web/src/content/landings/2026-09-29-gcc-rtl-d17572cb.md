GCC's RTL (Register Transfer Language) optimization pass now detects and simplifies specific forms of the `smax` (signed maximum) operation. For integer operands, the patterns `smax(a, -a)` and `smax(-a, a)` are recognized and transformed into `abs(a)`. This optimization can lead to more efficient code by replacing a conditional-like operation with a direct absolute value computation.
