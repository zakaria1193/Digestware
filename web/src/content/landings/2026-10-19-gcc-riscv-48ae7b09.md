This commit refines the selection of arithmetic operations (AOP) for RISC-V RV64 targets. By preferring the 'PLUS' operation, the compiler can leverage sign extensions for free through 'addw' and 'addiw' instructions. This change can eliminate later sign extension operations, as demonstrated in a specific test case, potentially improving code performance. The impact on identifying 'bseti' will be monitored, with future work planned to potentially defer AOP resolution until after the combine pass.
