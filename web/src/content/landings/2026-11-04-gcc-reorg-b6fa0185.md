The `get_branch_condition` and `condition_dominates_p` functions in `reorg.cc` now accept a `const rtx_jump_insn *` parameter. This change enhances type safety by ensuring that these helpers operate on jump instructions that are not modified. Additionally, `steal_delay_list_from_target` casts the first instruction in a sequence, further aligning with the use of constant jump instructions.
