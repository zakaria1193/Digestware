This patch restricts a workaround related to `std::optional`'s state transitions (engaged to disengaged) to the Clang compiler. The original issue, where the constexpr evaluator treated an explicitly destroyed union member as still active, has been resolved in GCC. Therefore, the workaround is no longer needed for GCC and is now limited to Clang, which exhibits similar behavior.
