This change updates the GCC COBOL compiler to correctly handle the `REDEFINES` clause when it appears in conjunction with a `TYPEDEF`. Previously, the compiler might not have associated these constructs properly, potentially leading to incorrect symbol table information. The modification ensures that `REDEFINES` are recognized as part of a `TYPEDEF` definition.
