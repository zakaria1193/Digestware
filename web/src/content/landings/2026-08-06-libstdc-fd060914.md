This commit makes progress towards C++26's P3372 standard by enabling `constexpr` for `std::deque` and `std::queue` containers and their related internal operations. This allows more of their functionality to be evaluated at compile time. For debug builds, iterator instrumentation is disabled during constant evaluation to avoid issues with mutex locks and non-inline functions. Formatter specializations for queue and priority_queue are also made `constexpr`.
