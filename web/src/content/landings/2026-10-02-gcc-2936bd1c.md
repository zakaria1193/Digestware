This commit revises the `arm_fp16_ok` effective target check in the ARM testsuite to improve its handling of compiler flags. Previously, it sometimes exited early or incorrectly rejected valid combinations of `-mfpu` and `-march` flags, leading to test failures. The rework makes it iterate through sets of flags, similar to other `_ok` checks, ensuring that a working combination is found and allowing previously failing tests related to FP16 support to pass.
