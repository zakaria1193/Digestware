This commit enhances gccrs' ability to handle ambiguous glob imports by offloading the details of globbing definitions to a new `Rib::insert_globbed` function. This change simplifies the `GlobbingVisitor` and ensures that ambiguities arising from glob imports are managed more robustly. A new test case, `glob-import-ambiguous.rs`, is added to verify this functionality.
