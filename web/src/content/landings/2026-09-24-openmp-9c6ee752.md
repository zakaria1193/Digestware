The GCC compiler now pretty-prints the OMP_ITERATOR_BLOCK structure when debugging OpenMP iterators. This change adds the OMP_ITERATOR_BLOCK to the dump_omp_iterators function in tree-pretty-print.cc, specifically when the TDF_DETAILS flag is set, aiding developers in understanding OpenMP loop structures.
