This commit optimizes masked vector multiplications where only the low halves of vector elements are used. It recognizes that `(x & lowhalf_mask) * (y & lowhalf_mask)` is equivalent to a widening even or odd multiply operation. On x86, this replaces slower emulation sequences with single `pmuludq` instructions, or AVX512DQ `vpmuludq` where available, improving performance.
