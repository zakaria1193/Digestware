This change enables Interprocedural Analysis (IPA) for assumed function bodies by ensuring they inherit optimization attributes from their parent function. Previously, assumed bodies were created with attributes that prevented IPA optimizations. This patch modifies `create_assumption_fn` to omit these optimization-disabling attributes unless the originating function also possesses them. It also corrects a bug where the `assume_function` field was not copied during cloning.
