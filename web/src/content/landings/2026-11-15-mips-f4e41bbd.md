The MIPS assembler's handling of HI/LO register hazards for Toshiba TX39's MADD and MADDU instructions has been corrected. Previously, these instructions were not flagged as reading HI/LO registers, causing the assembler to miss potential pipeline hazards. This change adds the necessary flags, ensuring that extra NOP instructions are emitted to maintain correct operation and avoid issues where subsequent division instructions could encounter undefined HI/LO register values.
