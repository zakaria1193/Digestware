This commit optimizes a specific bitwise operation pattern within the `match.pd` pattern matching system. It folds the expression `umin(a, 1) | umin(b, 1)` into the more efficient form `umin(a | b, 1)`. This optimization is applied to integer types and is accompanied by new test cases to verify its correctness.
