- term: Defect Report (DR)
  definition: A formal report submitted to a standards committee (like the C++ standards
    committee) detailing a perceived error, ambiguity, or omission in the standard
    itself. The committee then reviews these reports and may issue clarifications
    or changes to the standard.

    for 'diagnostic'). Test files here are compiled and checked for errors or specific
    output.

    of the compiler by testing various language features, optimizations, and target-specific
    behaviors.
