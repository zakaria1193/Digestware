The assembler and disassembler now support the AArch64 FEAT_LSC64B architecture extension and its associated instructions: LDA64B, STL64B, STL64BV, and STL64BV0. This update includes dependency management, operand reuse, and comprehensive test cases for assembly, disassembly, and instruction constraints.
