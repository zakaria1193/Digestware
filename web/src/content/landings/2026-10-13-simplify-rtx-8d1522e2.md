This commit corrects a bug in GCC's `simplify-rtx` pass where it could incorrectly merge two identical volatile memory operands. This merging occurred in certain `PLUS` and `MINUS` coefficient folds when operands had side effects, leading to one of two sequenced volatile reads being lost. The fix ensures that such operands with side effects are not merged, preserving the integrity of volatile memory access sequences.
