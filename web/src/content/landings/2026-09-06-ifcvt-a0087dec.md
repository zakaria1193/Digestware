This commit modifies the GCC ifcvt pass to count converted if-blocks within the `noce_finish_if_conversion` helper function, aligning it with the CFG change it represents. Additionally, the flag `cond_exec_changed_p` is renamed to `ifcvt_changed_p` to more accurately reflect its purpose, as it signals that any conversion has occurred, not just conditional execution. These adjustments enhance accuracy and code clarity.
