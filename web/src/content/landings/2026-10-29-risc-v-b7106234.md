This commit introduces new test cases to the RISC-V testsuite specifically for the `vwabda.vv` and `vwabdau.vv` vector instructions. These tests ensure that the source registers (`vs1`, `vs2`) do not fall within the destination register group (`vd`), preventing potential register allocation conflicts and ensuring correct behavior according to the RISC-V vector specification.
