The GNU Assembler (GAS) now optimizes the `IMUL` instruction on x86/APX when the immediate operand is all ones (equivalent to -1). This pattern can be more efficiently represented by a `NEG` (negate) instruction. The change covers various forms of `IMUL` including word, long, and quad-word operations, potentially leading to smaller and faster code.
