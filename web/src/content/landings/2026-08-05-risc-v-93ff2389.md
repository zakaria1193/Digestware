This commit adds a suite of test cases to validate the RISC-V Vector extension's `vwmulsu.vv` instruction. The tests specifically focus on scenarios involving register group overlap, ensuring that the compiler correctly handles these situations when overlap is enabled. The goal is to ensure the instruction's behavior aligns with its specification, especially concerning register allocation and usage.
