Support for register overlap has been extended to the `vwmaccsu.vv` instruction in the RISC-V Vector extension. Similar to other widen ternary instructions, this commit enables the reuse of vector registers for `vwmaccsu.vv`, which handles mixed signed and unsigned narrowed sources, improving instruction scheduling flexibility.
