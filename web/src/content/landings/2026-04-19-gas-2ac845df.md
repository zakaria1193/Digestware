This commit fixes a potential buffer overrun in the assembler's `operand` function within `expr.c`. The issue occurred when a single quote appeared at the end of a line or statement, causing the `input_line_pointer` to increment past the allocated buffer. The commit also corrects an issue where `input_line_pointer` was being incremented before processing MRI mode ':' hex floats.
