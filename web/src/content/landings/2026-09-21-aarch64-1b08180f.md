This commit enhances the AArch64 backend's if-conversion profitability check by incorporating parallel instruction costs, moving away from a solely serial cost estimation. This allows the compiler to better identify short, independent computations within loops that can be efficiently converted into conditional selects, aligning with the target's instruction issue rate. This change results in more conditional selects and fewer branches in optimized code, leading to performance improvements, as demonstrated by increased throughput in the Snappy compression benchmark.
