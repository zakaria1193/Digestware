The libstdc++ `generate_canonical` function now supports generators that produce more than 128 bits of entropy. Previously, generators requiring computations that could overflow 128 bits were unsupported. This change implements a method to handle such cases by checking for potential overflows and rejecting values early, ensuring correctness even for generators with very large outputs.
