A recent RTL simplification improvement introduced a regression that broke the build for the CRIS architecture, specifically for `cris-elf`. The issue stemmed from `cris.md`'s `cbranch` instructions accepting more machine modes than the `define_insn` it lowered to could handle. This commit resolves the problem by adding the necessary modes to the `btst` instruction definition, restoring the build.
