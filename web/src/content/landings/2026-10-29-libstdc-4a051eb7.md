A bug in `pmr::unsynchronized_pool_resource` could lead to a crash due to improper exception handling during initial memory allocation for pool chunks. If allocation failed, the exception was swallowed, leaving the pool in a corrupted state with an incorrect capacity. This commit corrects the allocation logic to rethrow exceptions on failure and ensures the capacity is only updated if the allocation succeeds, thus maintaining the pool's integrity and fixing the crash.
