This commit rectifies issues in the `caf_shmem` implementation of Fortran's coarray features, specifically concerning the handling of stopped images. Image control statements like `SYNC ALL` and `SYNC TEAM`, as well as collective subroutines, now correctly report `STAT=STAT_STOPPED_IMAGE` or `STAT=STAT_FAILED_IMAGE` when a team contains a terminated image, adhering to the F2023 standard. Previously, these operations could deadlock or block indefinitely.
