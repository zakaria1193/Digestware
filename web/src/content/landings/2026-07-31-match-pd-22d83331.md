This commit introduces an optimization rule to `match.pd` that transforms the product of two quotients, `(A / B) * (C / D)`, into a single division: `(A * C) / (B * D)`. This is beneficial on architectures where division is significantly slower than multiplication. The new rule excludes infinities and NaNs and requires non-trapping math to ensure correctness. New test cases are added to `gcc.dg/tree-ssa/` to verify this transformation.
