This commit enhances the RiscV backend by adding support for Extended Control Flow (ECF) flags to its built-in functions. The `riscv_builtin_description` structure is updated to include `ecf_flags`, and related macros and initialization routines are adjusted. New test cases specifically for ECF built-ins are also introduced, allowing developers to leverage these new instructions.
