This commit fixes a regression in the `gcc.dg/builtin-clear-padding-4.c` test case. On targets that do not support `_BitInt` or have a `__BITINT_MAXWIDTH__` less than 65535, the test now correctly declares necessary variables `c3` and `c4` in its `#else` branch, ensuring the test compiles and runs as expected across different architectures.
