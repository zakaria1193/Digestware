These commits update the s390 assembler to enforce architectural constraints on register operands for several instructions. Specifically, instructions such as stpq, clclu, kimd, klmd, kmac, km, kmc, mvcl, kxbr, cmpsc, clcle, and clclu now correctly validate that their register operands are either register pairs or even-numbered registers as required by the s390 architecture. This prevents the generation of incorrect machine code and ensures adherence to the hardware's specifications.
