The ``noexcept`` specification for ``indirect::operator==`` has been adjusted to align with the standard draft (LWG4325) for heterogeneous comparisons. This ensures that the exception guarantees for comparing ``indirect`` objects are correctly specified. A new test case has been added to verify the ``noexcept`` specification.
