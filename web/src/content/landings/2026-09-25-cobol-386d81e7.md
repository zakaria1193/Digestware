This commit improves COBOL compilation performance by reducing middle-end processing time. It consolidates multiple calls to `libgcobol` into a single call and passes parameters in a structure, rather than individually. Additionally, it fixes the behavior of `-fstatic-call` and `-fno-static-call` options and moves a warning message to runtime.
