This change introduces TLSDESC relaxation for the RISC-V linker, allowing Thread-Local Storage (TLS) descriptors to be correctly handled in static executables. It rewrites TLS access sequences to work without runtime resolution, ensuring TLS functionality even when dynamic linking mechanisms are unavailable.
