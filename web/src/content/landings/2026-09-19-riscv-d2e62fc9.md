Recent improvements in GCC's handling of saturating arithmetic, specifically for the RISC-V vector extension, caused a test case (`vec_sat_u_sub_trunc-1-u8.c`) to fail. The optimization replaced a branchy sequence with more efficient saturating instructions, altering the expected output in the tree-dump. This commit updates the test's expectation to align with the improved output, simplifying the test by removing differing expectations for different optimization levels (-O2 vs -O3).
