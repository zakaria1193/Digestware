The `float_binary_op_range_finish` function has been refactored to use sub-ranges and the `contains_zero_p` check. This change improves the precision of range analysis by examining sub-ranges of the left-hand side (lhs) rather than just the hull endpoints. This is particularly useful for floating-point operations where sub-range analysis can lead to more accurate results.
