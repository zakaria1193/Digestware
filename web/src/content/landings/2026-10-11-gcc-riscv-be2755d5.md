This commit refines a RISC-V optimization splitter that rewrites expressions of the form `(x << C1) + C2` into `(x + C2') << C1`. The change prevents this rewrite from occurring when the `Zbkb` extension is enabled and the shift count (`C1`) is half the word size, a scenario that is better handled by specialized instructions within `Zbkb`.
