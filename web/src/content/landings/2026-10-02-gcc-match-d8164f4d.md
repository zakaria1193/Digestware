A pattern in GCC's pattern matching for `(trunc)abs((ext)a)` was fixed to prevent potential integer overflow and undefined behavior. The pattern has been restricted to non-integral types, ensuring that integer types are handled by a separate, more appropriate pattern. This resolves a bug where casting from unsigned types could lead to incorrect results.
