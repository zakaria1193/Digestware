The `back_threader` class and related enumerations have been moved from `tree-ssa-threadbackward.cc` to a new header file, `tree-ssa-threadbackward.h`. This change facilitates the integration of the backward threader into other compiler passes, such as FRE (Function Reordering Engine), enabling them to utilize its value numbering capabilities.
