Libstdc++ has updated its build configuration to use `LT_INIT` in `configure.ac`, replacing the deprecated `AM_PROG_LIBTOOL`. This change modernizes the way `libtool` is integrated into the `libstdc++` build process, bringing it in line with current Autoconf and Libtool best practices. The update primarily affects the configuration and build system of `libstdc++`, ensuring ongoing compatibility and maintainability.
