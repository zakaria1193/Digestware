The range-operation library has been extended to implement relation handling for bitwise AND, OR, MIN, and MAX operations. When operands are equal, AND and OR operations simplify to the operand itself. When x <= y, MIN(x,y) simplifies to x, and when x >= y, MAX(x,y) simplifies to x. These improvements, identified during an audit of the DOM pass, will enable further simplifications by moving logic into range-ops.
