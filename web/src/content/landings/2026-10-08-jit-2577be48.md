This commit resolves a build failure in GCC's JIT (Just-In-Time) compilation on MinGW platforms, as reported in PR125229. Previously, `JIT_LDFLAGS` was not set for MinGW, preventing the use of user-defined `LDFLAGS`. The fix ensures that `JIT_LDFLAGS` is appropriately set for MinGW, aligning its behavior with other platforms and allowing custom linker flags to be applied during JIT builds.
