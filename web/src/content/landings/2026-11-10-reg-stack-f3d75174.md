The reg-stack optimization pass now correctly rejects inline assembly operands that require multiple x87 stack registers. Previously, complex operands like `long double` or `float _Complex` could lead to incorrect register tracking and assertion failures. This change enforces the limitation that each asm operand must occupy a single register, improving robustness and preventing crashes.
