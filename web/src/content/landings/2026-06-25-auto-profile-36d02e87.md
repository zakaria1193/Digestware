This patch modifies gcc-auto-profile scripts to accept a perf.data filename as a parameter, rather than hardcoding it. Previously, Makefile.tpl and gcc-auto-profile scripts used separate definitions for the perf data file, making it difficult to manage. This change centralizes the filename in Makefile.tpl and passes it to the scripts, simplifying maintenance and future modifications.
