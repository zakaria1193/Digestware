The `vneg` family of NEON intrinsics for the AArch64 architecture has been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework. This change standardizes the implementation and includes assembly tests for all variants. A test case was fixed due to a codegen regression where `vneg` on `INT_MIN` is no longer considered undefined behavior.
