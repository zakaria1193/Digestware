This commit removes the 'null' field from the `pt_solution` structure, as it is now redundant. Pointer non-nullness is fully tracked by `prange`, and the explicit 'null' bit was a relic from when this information was managed differently. Helper functions are added to `prange_storage` and `ptr_nonnull_p` replaces the old query. This change simplifies pointer analysis by consolidating non-nullness tracking within the `prange` mechanism.
