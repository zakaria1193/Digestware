primer_expert: The Fortran front-end's `gfc_simplify_transfer` function in `simplify.cc`
  suffered from GMP memory leaks, particularly when simplifying `TRANSFER` operations
  that resulted in arrays. The issue stemmed from creating a scalar expression and
  then changing its type, losing track of allocated memory. This patch reorganizes
  the expression generation to directly handle the known rank of the result (0 or
  1) and also modifies `gfc_target_interpret_expr` in `target-memory.cc` to properly
  clear GMP integers and reals before reinitialization, fixing PR127892.

This commit addresses memory leaks in GCC's Fortran front-end related to the simplification of the `TRANSFER` intrinsic function. It corrects how array expressions are generated and avoids redundant initialization of numerical components, preventing leaks and improving stability.
