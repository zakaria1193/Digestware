The RISC-V BFD library now reflects the latest extension ordering from the 20260818 draft specification. This change moves the 'V' extension before 'P' and removes the 'N' extension, ensuring compatibility with current RISC-V standards. The update corrects the internal representation of extension order in the assembler and linker.
