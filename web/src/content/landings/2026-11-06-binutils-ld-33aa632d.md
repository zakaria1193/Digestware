This commit optimizes the linker's PDB (Program Database) handling by skipping auxiliary symbol entries in the `handle_debugs_section` function. Previously, these entries were treated as symbols, leading to unnecessary lookups and slowdowns, especially in large objects with many sections. By identifying and skipping auxiliary entries, which contain unrelated data in their section number field, link times for PDB-generated files can be reduced by up to 10%.
