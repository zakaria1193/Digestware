The `make_decimal32`, `make_decimal64`, and `make_decimal128` functions in `decimal/decimal.h` were previously declared `static`, giving them internal linkage and preventing their use in C++ modules. By changing them to `inline`, these functions now have external linkage with vague-linkage semantics, allowing them to be shared across translation units and exported when consumed as header units or within module purview, resolving PR122071.
