GCC's configuration for MinGW cross-compilation targets has been updated to remove references to the `/mingw` path. This path was primarily used by the obsolete MSYS 1 environment. Current users of MinGW cross-compilation, including MSYS2 and development toolkits like w64devkit, either use custom prefixes or the default `/usr` path, making the `/mingw` configuration redundant.
