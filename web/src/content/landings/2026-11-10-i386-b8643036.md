This commit enables the `TARGET_IRA_LOOP_REGION_FLATTEN_COST` hook for the i386 architecture, specifically for the APX instruction set. This allows the instruction register allocation (IRA) pass to keep loop regions with a single entry and exit as separate regions when their internal calls are more frequent than the surrounding move instructions. This is particularly beneficial for APX, where most general-purpose registers are call-clobbered, thus improving register pressure management.
