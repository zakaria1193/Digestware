This commit removes SSE instructions from the x86 target that were incorrectly implemented. Specifically, `*fix_trunc<mode>si_sse_zext` and `*fix_trunc<mode>si_sse_2_zext` did not perform the expected zero-extension of SImode to DImode. The changes also include a new test case to verify the fix.
