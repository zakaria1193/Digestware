The unlabeled landing pad PLT for RISC-V's Zicfilp extension has been updated to align with the latest processor ABI (psABI). This change modifies the PLT entries and header to use register `t2` for target addresses instead of `t3`, and optimizes the PLT header size. These adjustments are backward compatible as they are generated by the linker.
