This commit addresses memory leaks in gccrs related to filename lifetimes within the linemap. Filenames are now leaked to live as long as the linemap itself, preventing them from being prematurely deallocated. This change involves removing `include_extra_file` usage, copying and leaking the filename parameter in the lexer, and removing related members from `Session`.
