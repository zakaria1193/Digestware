This commit modifies the order of operations in the `cgraph_edge::redirect_call_stmt_to_callee` function to ensure that callback edges are included in the call graph dumps. Previously, the printing logic occurred after callback edges were redirected, causing them to be omitted from the output. By moving the printing sequence before the callback redirection branch, this patch ensures that all callback edges are now visible in the generated dumps.
