This commit fixes an ICE (Internal Compiler Error) in the Fortran compiler related to coarray deallocation. It modifies the `deallocate_allocated_coarrays` function to correctly locate the `END TEAM` statement by traversing the team block's code list from the head. The guarded deallocation is then spliced in front of the `END TEAM` statement, ensuring proper execution order.
