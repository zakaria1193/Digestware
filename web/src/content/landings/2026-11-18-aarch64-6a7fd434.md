This commit removes several unused NEON (Advanced SIMD) built-in functions from the aarch64 backend: `smlal_hi`, `umlal_hi`, `smlsl_hi`, and `umlsl_hi`. These functions became obsolete after a prior change that migrated similar builtins to a pragma-based framework, deleting their definitions. Since no other code used these specific functions, they are now safely removed.
