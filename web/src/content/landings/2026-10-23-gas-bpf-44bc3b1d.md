This commit introduces support for BPF ISA version 5 by adding the UHMUL (unsigned high-half 64-bit multiply) and SHMUL (signed high-half 64-bit multiply) instructions. These instructions are encoded as variants of the ALU64 MUL instruction. The assembler and disassembler now default to BPF ISA v5, and new test cases are included.
