This commit updates the COBOL compiler's parser to correctly recognize figurative constants when an implicit PICTURE clause is present. This change affects how data fields are validated and updated, ensuring that figurative constants like 'ZEROS' or 'SPACES' are handled appropriately when they are assigned to variables without an explicitly defined PICTURE clause. The `update_prior_invalid_field` and `field_add` functions are modified to accommodate this.
