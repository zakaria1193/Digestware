  on many different computer processors. These tests are organized into a 'testsuite.'
  Sometimes, a particular test might only make sense or be valid for certain processor
  configurations. For example, some ARM processors can handle a mix of different types
  of floating-point numbers (numbers with decimal points), while others handle them
  differently. A special flag or setting, `arm_mixed_fp`, helps the testsuite know
  which kind of ARM processor it's running on. This update simply adds a clear explanation
  of what `arm_mixed_fp` means and why it's used in the GCC testsuite documentation.
  This makes it easier for anyone working with the testsuite to understand why certain
  tests are run or skipped for different ARM devices.
