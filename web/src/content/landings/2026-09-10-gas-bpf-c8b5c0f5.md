This commit refines the BPF assembler's parsing of mnemonics by applying the `%t` directive more broadly. This directive signifies the end of a mnemonic, ensuring that subsequent operands are not misinterpreted as part of the mnemonic itself. The change updates 65 sites in `tc-bpf.c` and `opcodes/bpf-opc.c`, addressing potential assembly errors, especially when mnemonics are immediately followed by operands without whitespace. It also adjusts parsing rules for the `lock` mnemonic to correctly handle its operand.
