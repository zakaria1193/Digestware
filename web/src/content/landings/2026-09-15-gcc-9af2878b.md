This commit improves GCC's vectorization capabilities by adding a crucial check to prevent the vectorization of conversions from `_BitInt` types. Specifically, it ensures that `_BitInt` values are appropriately sign-extended or zero-extended to match the target mode before vectorization occurs, avoiding potential incorrect results. A new test case, `vect-bitint-1.c`, is included to verify this correction.
