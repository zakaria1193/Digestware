A bug in the handling of nested [[assume]] attributes during constant evaluation has been fixed. Previously, when one [[assume]] was evaluated within the predicate of another, the side effects of the inner assumption were not correctly rolled back. This led to incorrect state, which is now resolved by properly restoring the previous context after the inner evaluation completes.
