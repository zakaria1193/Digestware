The `gccrs` test suite has been updated to compile Rust's `libcore` up to the High-Level Intermediate Representation (HIR) lowering phase. This change, while increasing test execution time by approximately four minutes on a local machine and ten minutes on CI runners, is necessary to ensure `libcore` compiles successfully. The test suite's timeout has also been extended to accommodate the longer compilation duration.
