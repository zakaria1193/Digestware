This commit enhances the i386 backend in GCC by extending its PCLMULQDQ instruction expanders to efficiently compute CRC64 checksums. The changes support both forward and reversed CRC64 calculations, including specific handling for polynomials with LSB=0 and LSB=1, building upon previous work for 16-bit and 32-bit CRC. New test cases are included to verify the correctness of these optimizations.
