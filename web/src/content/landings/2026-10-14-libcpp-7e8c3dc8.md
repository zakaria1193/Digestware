This commit updates libcpp's build configuration to assume that the 'abort' function is available for C89 targets. It removes the explicit check for 'abort' in configure.ac and system.h, simplifying the build process by relying on C89 standard guarantees. This is part of a series to align GCC's build with C89 compliance.
