This commit modifies GCC's pattern matching generator (`genmatch`) to relax a specific ordering barrier related to `DT_TRUE` nodes. The barrier was preventing efficient merging of patterns when a `DT_TRUE` node separated them, even if the patterns didn't truly depend on strict ordering. The change now only enforces the barrier when necessary to maintain source order, specifically when an intermediate sibling has a `DT_MATCH` descendant. This optimization reduces the size of generated GIMPLE and GENERIC matchers.
