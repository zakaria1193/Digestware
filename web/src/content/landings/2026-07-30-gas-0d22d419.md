This commit reverts a problematic change in the gas assembler's i386 Intel syntax simplification logic, which inadvertently caused unbounded recursion and symbol loop issues (PR 30308). The fix introduces immediate failure detection for symbol loops and revises the simplification process to correctly handle cases where symbols are identical or equated, preventing simplification after a loop is detected.
