This commit addresses a latent bug in the AMD GCN (Graphics Core Next) backend by ensuring that load instructions are restartable and marked as no-clobber when XNACK is enabled. This is necessary even for instructions that will be split later, guaranteeing correct behavior in the presence of XNACK acknowledgments.
