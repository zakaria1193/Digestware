This commit optimizes CRC64 calculations on 64-bit targets by leveraging the `crc32q` instruction for a specific polynomial (0x1EDC6F4100000000). This replaces a sequence of `pclmulqdq` and `movq` instructions with a single `crc32q` instruction when the input data is XORed with the CRC value. It also corrects an invalid use of the `crc32` instruction for SImode CRC with DImode data.
