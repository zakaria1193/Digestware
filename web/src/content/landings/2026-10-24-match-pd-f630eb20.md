This commit corrects a flaw in the `match.pd` patterns where signed vector addition results that wrap around were incorrectly folded into the `IFN_AVG_FLOOR` intrinsic. For signed types with defined overflow behavior, the previous logic required only one leading zero bit, which was insufficient to prevent incorrect results when addition overflowed. The fix now mandates two leading zero bits for signed types with defined overflow, ensuring accurate calculations and preventing misinterpretation of wrapped values.
