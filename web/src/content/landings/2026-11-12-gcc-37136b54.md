This commit renames several functions responsible for handling D attributes in GCC's D frontend. All identified functions, such as `handle_noreturn_attribute` and `handle_malloc_attribute`, are now consistently prefixed with `d_` (e.g., `d_handle_noreturn_attribute`) for better organization and clarity.
