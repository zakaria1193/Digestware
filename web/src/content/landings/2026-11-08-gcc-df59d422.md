This commit replaces the `INSN_FROM_TARGET_P` macro with a dedicated function, `set_insn_from_target`, which operates on `rtx_insn*`. This change improves the robustness and clarity of instruction flag management within GCC's internal representation. The flag is used in instruction reordering and delay slot filling, and this refactoring ensures safer access and modification.
