The Cobol compiler now performs validation to ensure that operands used in arithmetic operations (ADD, SUBTRACT, MULTIPLY, DIVIDE) are numeric. It also checks that outputs are either numeric or numeric-edited, improving runtime stability by catching potential data type errors early in the compilation process.
