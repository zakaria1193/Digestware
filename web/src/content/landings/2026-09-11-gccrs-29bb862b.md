Owen Avery fixed issues with copying `HIR::ClosureExpr` in `gccrs`, ensuring that `locus` information is set correctly for both the expression and its parameters.
This change improves the reliability of closure handling within the compiler's intermediate representation.
It also includes a new test case to cover the corrected behavior, addressing Rust-GCC/gccrs#4740.
