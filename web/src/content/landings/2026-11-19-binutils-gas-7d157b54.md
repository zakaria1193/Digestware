The RISC-V assembler has been updated to support the Shlcofideleg extension. This extension allows for delegation of local counter-overflow interrupts to VS-mode. The changes include updating the BFD library to recognize the extension and its implicit dependency on the 'H' extension, as well as adding corresponding tests to the assembler's test suite.
