This commit fixes a bug on the i386 architecture where the AES, SHA, and PCLMUL instruction sets remained enabled even when SSE2 was explicitly disabled via compiler flags like `-mno-sse2`. These instruction sets depend on SSE2, and disabling SSE2 should have disabled them. The fix adds these instruction sets to the SSE2 unset mask, ensuring that disabling SSE2 also disables its dependents, preventing the compiler from attempting to use SSE-unavailable instructions.
