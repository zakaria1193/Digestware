The test pr126355.c failed on hppa64-hp-hpux11.11 due to the target's assembler emitting DWARF attributes with trailing comments (e.g., '.ascii "name\0" ; DW_AT_name') instead of the standard format ('DW_AT_name: "name"'). This commit modifies the `scan-assembler` regex in the test to accommodate this HPPA-specific layout, allowing the test to pass on both standard ELF and HPPA targets without needing separate validation checks.
