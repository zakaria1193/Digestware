Libstdc++ has updated its Autoconf configuration files to replace instances of the deprecated `AC_TRY_COMPILE` macro with `AC_COMPILE_IFELSE`. This change modernizes the way `libstdc++` performs compile-time checks for various features and capabilities, ensuring adherence to current Autoconf best practices. The update affects numerous `GLIBCXX_CHECK` macros across `acinclude.m4`, `configure.ac`, `crossconfig.m4`, and `linkage.m4`, also removing an unused macro `GLIBCXX_CHECK_STDLIB_DECL_AND_LINKAGE_0`.
