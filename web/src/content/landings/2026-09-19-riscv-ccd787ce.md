This change enhances RISC-V optimization by improving how conditional zeroes are generated for comparisons against immediate values. It introduces new split patterns and maps greater-than conditions to less-than-or-equal, allowing them to be efficiently handled by the Zicond extension. This results in replacing redundant immediate loads with instructions like `slti` or `sltiu` followed by `czero.nez` or `czero.eqz`, producing more compact and efficient code.
