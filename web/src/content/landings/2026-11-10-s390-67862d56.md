This commit introduces support for the .insn pseudo-mnemonics vrs_a, vrs_b, vrs_c, and vrs_d in the s390 assembler. These mnemonics correspond to different versions of the VRS instruction format, introduced in IBM z13 and z14 processors. The change includes updates to opcode definitions, documentation, and new test cases.
