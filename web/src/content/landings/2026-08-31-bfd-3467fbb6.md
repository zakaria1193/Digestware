This commit introduces `R_XTENSA_PDIFF_ULEB128`, a new relocation type for the Xtensa architecture, to support DWARF 5's use of unsigned LEB128 (uleb128) encoding for differences in location and range lists. Previously, such differences prevented linker relaxation for entire functions. The new relocation allows the assembler to pad the uleb128 to a fixed width, enabling the linker to rewrite the difference in place after relaxation, thus preserving relaxation for functions with DWARF 5 debug info.
