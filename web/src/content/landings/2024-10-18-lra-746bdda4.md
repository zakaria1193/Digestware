The Local Register Allocation (LRA) pass now has a new target hook, `cannot_substitute_const_equiv_p`, to prevent unsafe substitutions of floating-point constants. This addresses a problem where LRA could emit incorrect constant loads when mode switching is involved, as seen on the SH architecture. This hook allows targets to express constraints on such substitutions, ensuring correctness across different floating-point modes.
