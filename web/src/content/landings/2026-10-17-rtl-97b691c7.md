Functions like `rest_of_insert_bti` and `riscv_mode_needed` in the AArch64 and RISC-V backends previously could incorrectly consider leading `DEBUG_INSN`s when determining the first executable instruction. This commit introduces `get_first_nonnote_nondebug_insn` to reliably find the first actual instruction. This prevents redundant `BTI C` insertions on AArch64 and ensures correct hoisting of `VXRM` settings on RISC-V, which were previously hindered by debug directives.
