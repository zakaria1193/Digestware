The predicate `aarch64_general_reg`, used to fuse comparison and subtraction into the `SUBS` instruction, has been modified. Previously, it relied on `REGNO_REG_CLASS`, which incorrectly reported subclasses for registers x8-x15 when SME2 support was enabled. The predicate now directly tests the register number, ensuring `SUBS` instructions are correctly generated even when their destination is in these registers, while still rejecting the stack pointer as intended.
