The BFD library now correctly handles PE weak externals that have a real fallback definition within an archive. Previously, the archive map generation might have skipped these symbols, preventing the linker from resolving them. This fix ensures that such weak externals are included in the archive map when their fallback points to a defined symbol, and it also prevents duplicate extraction of archive members during resolution. This addresses a known issue with PE weak external symbol resolution in archives.
