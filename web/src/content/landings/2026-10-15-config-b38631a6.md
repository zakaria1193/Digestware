This change allows specific GCC ports to opt out of sharing jump tables when the dispatch mechanism requires the table to be adjacent to the jump instruction. This adjacency is necessary for various reasons, such as computing table entries from the jump's address or ensuring correct behavior during machine reordering passes. Ports like ARM, CRIS, M68k, SH, and others now refuse sharing for specific dispatch forms, while others like PA, C6x, and MIPS refuse only under certain conditions (e.g., PIC code). This prevents incorrect behavior, assembly failures, or broken optimizations on these architectures.
