This commit fixes an issue where clearing a `std::optional` (i.e., transitioning from engaged to disengaged) would leave the underlying union without an active member, violating constant expression requirements. The fix involves activating a dummy union member after destroying the contained value. This ensures that the union always has an active member throughout its lifetime, resolving compilation errors.
