The s390 assembler documentation has been updated to reflect that the immediate operands in instruction formats RIE, RIL, and RSI are PC-relative. These are now consistently documented as 'RIx' (relative immediate) in the instruction format descriptions, aligning with the Principles of Operation manuals.
