The build system for `rclex.c` and `mclex.c` has been simplified by removing special handling that exempted them from the `-Werror` flag. Originally, this exemption was for hand-written lexers generated by `flex`. However, `rclex.c` has been hand-written for many years, and `mclex.c` was always hand-written. This change ensures that new warnings in these files are caught during compilation, improving overall code quality.
