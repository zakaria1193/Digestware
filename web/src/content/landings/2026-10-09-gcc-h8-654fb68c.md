A recent change in GCC caused a regression on the H8/SX architecture where the calculated length for HImode shift operations by powers of two was incorrect. This led to mispredicted branch targets, specifically for the `pr69714.c` test case. The fix adjusts the `compute_a_shift_length` function to prevent special length adjustments for H8/SX when using 'shift special' instructions, resolving the correctness issue.
