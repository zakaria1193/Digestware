This change refactors GCC's CRC calculation functions, `calculate_crc` and `calculate_reversed_crc`, moving them to `hwint.cc` and making them public. The functions are extended to handle arbitrary data sizes and initial CRC values, moving beyond their previous limitations of 8-bit data and a fixed initial CRC of 0. This lays the groundwork for implementing compile-time constant folding of CRC built-ins.
