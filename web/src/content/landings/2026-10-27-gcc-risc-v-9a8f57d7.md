This commit addresses several issues found in the RISC-V GCC port through an LLM review. It refines the zero-extension logic to ensure mode consistency, generalizes a bit manipulation instruction pattern to support both RV32 and RV64, and corrects the use of subregisters in conditional shift operations to prevent nested subregister issues. These fixes enhance the robustness and correctness of the RISC-V backend.
