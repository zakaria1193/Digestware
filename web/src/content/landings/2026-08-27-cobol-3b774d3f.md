This commit enhances GCC's COBOL support by refining the handling of reserved words and introducing new parsing capabilities. Reserved words that are not defined by the ISO standard are now renamed to prevent conflicts with user-defined names, ensuring that reserved words can be reliably identified by consulting the token list. Additionally, a new compiler option, `-Wdisplay-screen`, has been added to enable the parsing of GnuCOBOL's screen syntax within `DISPLAY` statements. While this syntax is parsed, it does not affect the compiler's output.
