The `wait_until` and `wait_for` methods for `std::condition_variable` and `std::condition_variable_any` in libstdc++ have been updated to accept their timeout parameters by value. This change aligns the implementation with the resolution of Library Working Group (LWG) issue 4301, ensuring consistent behavior across different C++ standard library implementations.
