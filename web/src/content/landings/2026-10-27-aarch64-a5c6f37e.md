This commit introduces constant folding capabilities for the ARMv8-A CRC32 intrinsics (`__crc32` and `__crc32c`) on the aarch64 architecture. Similar to the existing x86 implementation, it uses `calculate_reversed_crc` with the appropriate polynomial to precompute results for constant arguments. New test cases are included to verify the correctness of this folding.
