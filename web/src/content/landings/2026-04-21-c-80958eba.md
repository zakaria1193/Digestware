The C++ grammar includes `splice-type-specifier` as part of `mem-initializer-id`, but it wasn't being parsed in `cp_parser_mem_initializer_id`.  This patch adds the parsing logic for `splice-type-specifier` in that function.  It defers typename diagnostics and calls `cp_parser_splice_type_specifier` when nested-name-specifier nor `::` don't appear.
