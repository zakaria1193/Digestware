The AArch64 cost model's handling of vector statements that expand to multiple instructions was previously bugged, leading to incorrect cost calculations by double-counting statement repetition. This commit refactors the relevant helper function (`aarch64_ifn_vect_stmt_p` renamed to `aarch64_vect_stmt_cost`) to use `fractional_cost`. This aligns it with the rest of the cost model, fixes the bug where statement counts were applied twice, and simplifies the integration of future costing enhancements.
