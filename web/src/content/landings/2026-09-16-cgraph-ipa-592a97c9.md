This commit adds new logic for redirecting internal callback edges and modifying their associated call statements within the cgraph and IPA components. Internal callback edges require a distinct redirection approach compared to regular ones, allowing for more extensive call statement modifications beyond simple address replacements. The changes update functions responsible for call modification to accommodate this, including argument remapping based on summaries describing the changes made. This ensures that call statements involving internal callback edges are handled correctly.
