This commit refactors the value numbering driver (`vn_driver`) to ensure that cleanup operations related to value number elimination are performed in the `finalize` method. This change guarantees that the Intermediate Language (IL) state seen by subsequent passes, like the jump threader, accurately reflects the elimination process. The adjustment is primarily for correctness and does not introduce functional changes, ensuring that value numbers remain valid between optimization passes.
