The s390 assembler's RRR_F0FF instruction format has been renamed to RRF_F0FF. This change aligns with the architectural evolution where the RRR format was merged into RRF-a starting with the IBM z196. Instructions such as adtr, ddtr, mdtr, and sdtr are updated to reflect this new format name.
