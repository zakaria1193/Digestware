This commit adds the `override` keyword to a member function in `config/i386/i386.cc`. This change aligns with C++ best practices for virtual function overrides and addresses potential warnings from other compilers, such as Clang's `-Winconsistent-missing-override`, without impacting the functionality of the x86-64 backend.
