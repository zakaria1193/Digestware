This commit removes the deprecated ENUM_BITFIELD macro from libcpp's header files. As C++14 and later standards directly support enum bitfields, this macro is no longer necessary and its removal simplifies the codebase. The change affects `cpp_token` and `cpp_hashnode` in `cpplib.h` and `line_map_ordinary` in `line-map.h`.
