GCC's COBOL front-end has been updated to correctly parse reference modification patterns that may include newline characters. This involved modifying the lexer's recognition rules and fixing an `is_refmod()` function to ensure proper handling of these patterns, alongside other minor lexer and parser corrections for compatibility and robustness.
