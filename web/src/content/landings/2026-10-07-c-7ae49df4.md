This commit resolves a crash in the C++ compiler that occurred when a lambda expression, which generates a TARGET_EXPR, was used in an assignment. The issue stemmed from the TARGET_EXPR being incorrectly treated as a prvalue instead of an lvalue. The fix modifies the internal handling of TARGET_EXPRs to preserve their lvalue nature, ensuring correct behavior for copy-initialization scenarios.
