This commit introduces support for the double-precision floating-point unit (DFPU) on Xtensa processors. It enables generation of DF (double-precision floating-point) mode comparisons and conditional moves, and correctly handles DF mode registers. The changes include updates to constraints, prototypes, cost tables, and emission logic in `xtensa.cc`, along with modifications to `TARGET_HARD_FLOAT` to reflect the availability of either single or double-precision hardware FP support. This allows Xtensa targets to leverage double-precision floating-point arithmetic.
