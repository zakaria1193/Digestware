gccrs introduces runtime 'Drop flags' for conditional drops in the backend. These flags, managed by the compiler context, track initialization and are cleared after a move. The backend uses BIR analysis to identify locals needing drop flags and moves that clear them, ensuring correct cleanup execution before function exit or scope end. This improves the robustness of drop management for complex control flow.
