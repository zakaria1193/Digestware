The GCC Rust front-end (gccrs) now includes a validation mechanism that enforces intrinsic signatures during the type-checking phase. This ensures that intrinsic declarations adhere to predefined rules, and if a mismatch occurs, the compiler will issue a diagnostic message. This new enforcement prevents incorrectly declared intrinsics from being passed to the compiler's backend, improving compiler robustness and catching potential errors earlier in the compilation process.
