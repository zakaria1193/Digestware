The i386 architecture's CRC32 instructions previously used a single internal representation (UNSPEC_CRC32) for all operand widths. This allowed common subexpression elimination (CSE) to incorrectly merge results of CRC32 operations with different widths when a constant was used. This change introduces distinct internal representations for each width (byte, word, long, quad) to prevent such misoptimizations and also adds support for the 64-bit zero-extended CRC32B.
