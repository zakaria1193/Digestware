GCC introduces SRLI_ADD macro-fusion recognition for RISC-V, combining right shift immediate (SRLI) instructions with a matching-width ADD-type operation. This optimization applies when the shift amount is small (0-3) and producer-consumer dependencies are met. The fusion reuses existing shift and add classifiers. It is disabled by default, and its positive dump checks are XFAILed, pending CPU enablement.
