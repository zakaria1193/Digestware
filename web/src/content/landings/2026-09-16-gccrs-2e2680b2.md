This commit completes the implementation of the `#[used]` attribute in GCC Rust. It ensures that static variables marked with `#[used]` are not garbage collected by the compiler or linker, mirroring the behavior of C's `used` and `retain` attributes. The `#[used(linker)]` variant specifically instructs the linker to preserve the static item, even if it appears unused.
