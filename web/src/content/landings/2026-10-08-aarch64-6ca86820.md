This commit ports several AArch64 NEON widening multiply (`vmull`) and multiply-accumulate (`vmlal`, `vmlsl`) intrinsics to GCC's pragma-based framework. The changes include handling of constant operands, upper halves of vectors, and ensures correct accumulation behavior for signed overflow according to ACLE standards. Some 32-bit forms and PMULL-based intrinsics remain handled by existing mechanisms.
