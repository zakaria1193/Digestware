This commit enhances the x86 assembler's APX extension support by optimizing AND instructions that use small negative immediate values. It allows the use of shorter byte or word instruction forms when the non-EVEX.ND byte/word operations do not alter upper register bits. This optimization is specifically for reducing code size, considering that byte/word forms might introduce length-changing prefixes or cause partial register stalls.
