This commit resolves an issue in the BFD library's M32R ELF relocation handling where a left shift of a negative value was being performed, leading to undefined behavior according to the C99 standard. The fix replaces the use of `bfd_signed_vma` with `bfd_vma` for the relocation calculation, ensuring that the shift operation is performed on an unsigned type, thus preventing the undefined behavior and promoting more robust relocation processing.
