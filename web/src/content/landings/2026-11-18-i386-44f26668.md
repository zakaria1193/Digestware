primer_expert: i386 `define_split` for `and $-65536, reg` was firing too early. The
  split, which converts this to a `strict_low_part` HImode store of zero, could execute
  before reload. This resulted in `gen_lowpart` operating on a temporary pseudo, causing
  the `strict_low_part` store to update an unused pseudo and leave the original register
  unmodified. Requiring `reload_completed` ensures `gen_lowpart` operates on a hard
  register, yielding a plain REG that correctly aliases the original, resolving the
  miscompile.

A miscompile on the i386 architecture is fixed by ensuring that a specific instruction split for `and $-65536, reg` only occurs after register reloading is complete. Previously, this split could happen prematurely, leading to incorrect handling of subregister values and a failure to update the intended register, causing the original register to retain an old, incorrect value.
