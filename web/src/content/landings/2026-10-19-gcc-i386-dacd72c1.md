The expander for the `extendhfdf2` instruction on i386 has been fixed when using the `-mgeneral-regs-only` option. Previously, the expander incorrectly assumed that the second step of the conversion (HF to SF, then SF to DF) was always available, even when neither x87 nor SSE math hardware was present. This commit ensures that the expander now correctly requires the necessary hardware support (x87 or SSE2/SSE_MATH) for the SF to DF conversion when AVX512FP16 is not used, preventing expansion failures.
