This commit corrects an issue in the POPCOUNT pattern matching logic, specifically within the Hacker's Delight matcher. Previously, distinct captures for the low half of a final add (`@step_4`) and a shifted temporary (`@step4`) led to the incorrect folding of `(other & 0xffff) + (x >> 16)` into `.POPCOUNT(x)`, causing the other operand to be dropped. The fix ensures both operands use the same capture (`@step4`), requiring the masked and shifted values to be identical.
