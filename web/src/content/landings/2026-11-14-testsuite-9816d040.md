This commit marks a specific test case (`consteval-prop6.C`) as expected to fail (XFAIL) on HP-UX systems with hppa architectures. The failure is related to a warning that is not being emitted at the expected line number during constant evaluation promotion. This change prevents the test suite from incorrectly failing on these platforms.
