GCC's COBOL compiler has been updated to permit larger data areas in `REDEFINES` clauses when using the `-dialect ibm` option. The ISO standard restricts `REDEFINES` if the redefining area is larger than the redefined area, with exceptions for level 01 data. The IBM dialect, however, does not impose this size restriction. Additionally, this commit refines the parsing of the `-preprocess` argument.
