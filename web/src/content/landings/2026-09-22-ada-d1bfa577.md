This commit refactors how Ada conditional expressions, specifically `N_Case_Expression` and `N_If_Expression`, are expanded. It introduces a single, uniform criterion for deciding when to create temporary variables, aiming to eliminate large temporaries for `N_If_Expression` and `N_Expression_With_Actions` and avoid indirection for `N_Case_Expression`. The layout machinery is also improved to compute array type sizes more accurately, addressing issues with `Component_Size` clauses.
