This commit introduces new test cases in gccrs to cover the scenario of declaring language items within an external crate. Previously, the test suite focused on independent component testing and did not adequately verify inter-crate language item declarations. The new tests, located in `rust/minicore`, establish an external crate boundary to ensure that language items from external crates are correctly resolved and utilized by the compiler.
