This commit rectifies how GCC's Rust frontend, gccrs, numbers operands within the `asm!` macro. Previously, operands were numbered based on their appearance order in the macro call, but GCC expects outputs to be numbered before inputs. This mismatch caused incorrect operand references, particularly when inputs preceded outputs, potentially leading to faulty assembly generation. The fix renumbers placeholders to align with GCC's convention and introduces an error for placeholders appearing after the last defined operand.
