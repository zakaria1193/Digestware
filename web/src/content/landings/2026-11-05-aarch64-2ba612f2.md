This commit ports a large set of NEON narrowing shift and round-shift intrinsics to the pragma-based framework on the aarch64 architecture. This includes functions like `vshrn_n`, `vrshrn_n`, `vqshrn_n`, and their variants, consolidating their implementation within GCC's intrinsic handling system.
