An ICE (Internal Compiler Error) in the aarch64 backend is fixed by correctly handling the `svcount_t` type in `svwhilel*_c*` builtins. The issue occurred when these builtins were incorrectly folded as predicate-as-mask operations. The fix ensures proper handling for predicate-as-counter scenarios and punts on unsupported cases.
