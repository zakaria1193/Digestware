GCC has gained the ability to combine shift-add operations (from the RISC-V Zba extension) with word rotations. This optimization targets specific instruction sequences, particularly when performed at XLEN width before a word rotation. Benchmarks show significant reductions in retired instructions and execution cycles on RV64 systems, with improvements up to ~10% in some microbenchmarks.
