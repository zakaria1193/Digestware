This commit resolves a deadlock issue in libgfortran's coarray implementation where an image killed by a signal would not properly detach from its teams' barriers. This left other images blocked indefinitely. The fix introduces a mechanism where teams register with a supervisor, allowing it to detect and process killed images, simulating their detachment from barriers. This ensures that active images can proceed and correctly report the status of failed images.
