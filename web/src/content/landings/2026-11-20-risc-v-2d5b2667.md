CRC64 calculations on RV64 systems are now optimized by leveraging the clmul and clmulh instructions provided by the Zbkc extension. This change replaces multiple byte table lookups with more efficient multiply operations, leading to significant performance gains for 32- and 64-bit updates. The implementation also correctly handles polynomial shifts and XOR operations for accurate CRC calculation.
