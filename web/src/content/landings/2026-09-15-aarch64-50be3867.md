This commit enables the use of the UDOT instruction for calculating the absolute difference (SAD) of 8-byte vectors (V8QI) on AArch64 targets that support dot products. The SAD can now be lowered into two instructions: UABD/SABD to compute lane-wise differences, followed by a UDOT instruction. This UDOT effectively accumulates the results from the eight byte lanes into a V2SI vector, leveraging the dot product hardware for better performance.
