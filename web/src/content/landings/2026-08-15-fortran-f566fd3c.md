The Fortran intrinsic `IS_CONTIGUOUS` has been updated to correctly handle assumed-rank scalar arguments. If such an argument has a rank of zero at runtime, `IS_CONTIGUOUS` will now return `true`. This corrects a bug where the intrinsic incorrectly returned `false` due to a missing rank check in its span calculation.
