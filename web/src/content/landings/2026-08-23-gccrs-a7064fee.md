gccrs has improved its trait resolution logic to distinguish between regular generics and const-generics when binding implementations. This ensures that trait implementations are correctly associated with the appropriate generic or const-generic parameters, resolving issues related to Rust-GCC/gccrs#4812. A new test case has been added to verify this functionality.
