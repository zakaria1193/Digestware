This commit introduces two new variants (`is_constant_evaluated3a.C` and `is_constant_evaluated3b.C`) of an existing test case. These variants are designed to specifically test the behavior of `is_constant_evaluated` within `constexpr` function scopes and `maybe-constexpr` lambda scopes, respectively. They help uncover and verify fixes for issues related to constant folding in different evaluation contexts.
