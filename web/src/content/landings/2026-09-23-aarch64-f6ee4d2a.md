The AArch64 backend ensures that the Vector Length (VF) cap is applied consistently across both the statement body cost adjustment and the main loop comparison logic. This resolves discrepancies where the compiler might have ranked the same loop differently due to uncapped VF in one part of the cost model and capped in another, leading to more accurate optimization decisions for fixed-trip loops.
