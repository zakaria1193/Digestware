This commit corrects a typo in the `ipa_find_agg_cst_from_init` function, ensuring it correctly identifies aggregate constants. The function previously had a flawed check that would reject non-constant, non-declaration variables unless they were `TREE_READONLY`. The fix clarifies that `TREE_READONLY` is a valid condition for such variables, improving the accuracy of interprocedural analysis.
