This commit reorders the instruction sequence generated by GCC for `(plus (reg) (const_int))` on RISC-V when dealing with nontrivial constants. The previous sequence, often involving `lui`, `addi`, and `add`, could lead to suboptimal scheduling, especially when the result was used in memory references. The new order, typically `lui`, `add`, `addi`, improves instruction fusion and reduces execution cycles, yielding a performance gain of over 2% in benchmarks like x264.
