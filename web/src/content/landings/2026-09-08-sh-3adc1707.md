Previously, __builtin_sh_{get,set}_fpscr was enabled on SH targets even if they lacked an FPU, leading to an ICE. This change modifies the condition for enabling these built-ins to correctly check for the presence of TARGET_FPU_ANY, preventing the ICE and ensuring they are only available when an FPU is present. New test cases verify this fix.
