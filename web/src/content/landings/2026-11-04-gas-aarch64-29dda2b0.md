This commit corrects the AArch64 assembler's instruction table to allow the `MOVPRFX` instruction prefix with the `bfmmla` and `sve-f16mm fmmla` instructions. These instructions were incorrectly marked as not supporting `MOVPRFX`. The change includes fixes to the instruction definitions and adds corresponding test cases to verify the correct assembly and disassembly.
