GCC now recognizes ascending-address integer load/store pairs for the RISC-V architecture. This new macro-fusion capability enables the compiler to combine adjacent integer loads or stores with increasing addresses. The implementation reuses common pair matching logic and includes specific handling for subregister bases on RV32. The fusion is disabled by default, and its associated tests are XFAILed.
