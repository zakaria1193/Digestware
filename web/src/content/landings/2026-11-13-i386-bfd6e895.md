This commit rectifies issues in the i386 APX ccmp pattern that incorrectly handled comparisons and condition flags. Previously, the pattern compared a value against zero, which only matched flags for EQ/NE conditions, leading to misfolds of ordered and unsigned conditions. It also failed to preserve the CC mode of previous comparisons and produced incorrect scc output. The fix ensures direct comparison of operands and correctly propagates the CC mode, resolving incorrect optimizations and code generation for conditional instructions.
