An Internal Compiler Error (ICE) occurred on big-endian targets when handling a subregister with an outer QI mode and inner HI mode, specifically when the offset was 0. This was due to an invalid offset calculation. The fix replaces `gen_rtx_SUBREG` with `gen_lowpart_SUBREG`, which correctly computes the offset (1 for big-endian, 0 for little-endian), resolving the ICE.
