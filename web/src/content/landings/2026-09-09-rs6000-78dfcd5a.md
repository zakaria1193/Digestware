This patch introduces new builtins to GCC for the PowerPC architecture, specifically for SHA3 cryptographic operations. These builtins map to new `UNSPEC` entries and will support instructions that may appear in future processor revisions. The change also includes documentation for these new SHA3-related builtins and adds a test case to verify their functionality.
