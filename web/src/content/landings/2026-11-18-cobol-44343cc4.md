The UNSTRING operation in libgcobol has been corrected to adhere to the ISO specification's general rules for delimiters. It now ensures that the first delimiter encountered at a given position is honored, resolving issues where subsequent delimiters at the same position might have been incorrectly prioritized. This change affects the `unstring_fixed` function in `unstring.cc`.
