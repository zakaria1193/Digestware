This change adds declarations for `strtoll` and `strtoull` to `libiberty.h` when the compiler is set to C++11 or later. This resolves a C++11 bootstrap failure observed on the hppa64-hp-hpux11.11 platform by ensuring these standard C functions are correctly declared.
