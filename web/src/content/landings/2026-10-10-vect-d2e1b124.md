This commit improves vectorization of CLZ and CTZ operations by correctly handling the 'defined-at-zero' value. It ensures that during pattern matching for vectorization, the defined-at-zero value for the natural vector mode is used, rather than a scalar mode's value, as these can differ. A final check is performed during vectorizable_call to ensure consistency between the natural and final vector modes.
