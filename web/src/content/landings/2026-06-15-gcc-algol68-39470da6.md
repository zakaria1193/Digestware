The Algol 68 frontend in GCC previously included non-publicized mode indicants in module interfaces, even though other types of indicants were correctly marked. This commit rectifies the issue by ensuring that the `PUBLICIZED` attribute is correctly set to false for mode indicants during the extraction process. This change prevents unintended exposure of internal mode definitions in module exports, thus maintaining proper module encapsulation.
