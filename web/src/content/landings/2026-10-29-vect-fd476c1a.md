This commit enhances the GCC vectorizer's ability to handle `vec_extract` operations by querying target capabilities through a permutation pattern (`vec_extract_perm_p`). Previously, the vectorizer used a direct `vec_extract` optab check, which might not have perfectly aligned with what the target could actually expand. By using `can_vec_extract`, which now consults `vec_extract_perm_p`, the vectorizer is better synchronized with the backend's capabilities. This change might lead to slightly different codegen in some scalar/vector corner cases, potentially requiring target-level fixes or RTL optimizations.
