The RISC-V assembler and linker now ignore the deprecated `Tag_RISCV_priv_spec` attributes. This change removes warnings related to older privileged spec versions and simplifies attribute merging in the linker. Test cases have been updated to reflect this new behavior, ensuring that these attributes are dropped rather than causing conflicts or being emitted.
