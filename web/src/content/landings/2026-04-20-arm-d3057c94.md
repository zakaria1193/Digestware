This ARM backend commit corrects instruction selection for half-precision floating-point (`fp16`) move operations (`mov<mode>_vfp_<mode>16`). It ensures that `VLD1` and `VST1` instructions are only used when auto-increment addressing is needed and when `NEON_FP16INST` is available. Otherwise, it uses `VLDR` and `VSTR` to ensure compatibility with targets lacking Advanced SIMD (NEON).
