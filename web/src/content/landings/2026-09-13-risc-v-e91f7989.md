This commit enhances the CRC32 calculation for the RISC-V 32-bit architecture (rv32) by utilizing the `CLMULR` instruction. Previously, calculating a reflected polynomial for 32-bit CRC required an operand that was 33 bits wide, exceeding register capacity and forcing a fallback to a less efficient table-based method. By leveraging the `CLMULR` instruction's property to produce specific bits of a carry-less product, the compiler can now use the unshifted 32-bit reflected polynomial, resulting in a performance improvement for CRC32 operations on RV32 systems with the `Zbc` extension.
