The RISC-V backend's expansion of 32-bit min/max operations now includes the ``SUBREG_PROMOTED`` annotation.  Without it, the compiler was emitting redundant sign extension instructions after min/max, because it didn't know they already sign-extend their inputs.  This change improves code generation by avoiding unnecessary sign extensions.
