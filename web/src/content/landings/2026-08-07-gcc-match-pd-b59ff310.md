GCC's pattern matching system (`match.pd`) has gained a new rule to simplify a common, overflow-free way of calculating the average of two integers. The idiom `((x >> 1) + (y >> 1)) + (x & y & 1)` is now transformed into the mathematically equivalent but more efficient `(x & y) + ((x ^ y) >> 1)`. This optimization reduces the number of operations, leading to performance improvements on various architectures, including aarch64, and is also beneficial for vectorization.
