The Fortran intrinsic functions LEADZ (leading zero count), POPCNT (population count), POPPAR (population parity), and TRAILZ (trailing zero count) have been updated to properly support unsigned integer arguments. Previously, LEADZ and TRAILZ were rejected for unsigned types, while POPCNT and POPPAR worked but were undocumented for unsigned operands. This change extends the functionality and documentation to cover unsigned numbers, with a new test case added to verify the behavior.
