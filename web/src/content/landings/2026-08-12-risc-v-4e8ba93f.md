This change corrects how the RISC-V dynamic linker calculates the size of relocation entries. Previously, it incorrectly included the `.rela.plt` section in `DT_RELASZ`, causing tools like `llvm-bolt` to process the same relocation information twice. By defining `elf_backend_dtrel_excludes_plt`, the calculation now excludes `.rela.plt`, aligning with other targets and resolving the issue.
