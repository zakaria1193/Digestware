This commit rectifies a bug in gccrs's FNV-128 hash calculation logic. The `FNV::write` function contained a precedence error when applying `prime128Shift` and did not effectively utilize `prime128Lower` fitting into a `uint32_t`. The fix refines the calculation to ensure correct hashing.
