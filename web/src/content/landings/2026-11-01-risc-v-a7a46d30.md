This commit refactors how RISC-V handles block moves, particularly when a copied word is immediately overwritten by a subsequent store. Previously, the expansion deferred trailing whole words, causing the RTL Dead Store Elimination (DSE) pass to fail to prove the load was dead, resulting in unnecessary dead load and store instructions. By keeping the trailing word within the block move group, all loads precede all stores, allowing DSE to correctly identify and remove the dead code.
