This commit enhances error reporting within GCC's backward threader, specifically for failed block copies. Previously, when `back_jt_path_registry::duplicate_thread_path` failed, the registered path was silently dropped without indication. Now, such failures are reported through `cancel_thread` with a `failure_reason`, aligning with how other thread termination reasons are handled. This change, motivated by PR tree-optimization/126906, aids in diagnosing why optimization paths might be discarded.
