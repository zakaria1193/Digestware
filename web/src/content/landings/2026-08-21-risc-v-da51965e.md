This commit introduces initial support for modeling hardware autoprefetchers on RISC-V processors, similar to the existing AArch64 model. It enables weak autoprefetcher modeling and sets up tuning parameters, preparing for more advanced modeling of strong autoprefetchers in future work, which is beneficial for scheduling store instructions on relevant hardware.
