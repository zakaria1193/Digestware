Vectorization tests that would require mixing of length and mask vectors on the s390x architecture are now ignored. This is because s390x lacks the `vcond_mask_len` optab, and GCC's current handling of lengths and masks in these loops causes vectorization to abort. The change affects two specific test cases in the vectorization test suite.
