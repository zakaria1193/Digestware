GCC's libgcc has removed configuration checks for headers like inttypes.h, stdint.h, sys/auxv.h, sys/mman.h, sys/stat.h, sys/types.h, and unistd.h. These headers were found to be unused, simplifying the build process and resolving bootstrap issues.
