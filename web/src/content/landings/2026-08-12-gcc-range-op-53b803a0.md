This commit enhances the range analysis optimization pass by adding a new relation effect for the division operation. Specifically, when the compiler can determine that both the dividend (`A`) and the divisor (`B`) are positive, and that `A` is strictly less than `B`, it can now infer that the result of `A / B` will always be zero. This improves optimization by providing more precise value range information for division operations.
