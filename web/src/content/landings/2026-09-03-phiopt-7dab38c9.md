The `hoist_adjacent_loads` optimization in GCC moves loads from within a conditional block to its head. However, it previously retained the range information computed for those loads under the condition, leading to incorrect analysis for subsequent optimizations like range propagation. This change clears that stale range information, ensuring the optimizer uses accurate data and preventing potential bugs, especially when combined with future range analysis improvements.
