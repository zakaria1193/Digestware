The `aarch64_expand_vec_perm_const_1` function normalizes vector permutations by rotating indices and swapping operands. However, it incorrectly left the `zero_op0_p` and `zero_op1_p` flags pointing to the original operands' zeroness status. This caused subsequent recognizers to read the wrong vector, leading to incorrect results, such as zero vectors being returned when they shouldn't be. The fix ensures these flags are swapped along with the operands, resolving PR126597.
