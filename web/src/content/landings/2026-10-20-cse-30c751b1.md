An optimization in GCC's common subexpression elimination (CSE) pass, specifically `cse_vec_duplicate_match`, was incorrectly identifying and optimizing repeated `vec_duplicate` instructions. This occurred when the scalar value used in the `vec_duplicate` was modified between the occurrences of the `vec_duplicate` instructions. The fix strengthens the safety check to ensure that the scalar register is not modified between the original `vec_duplicate` and its potential CSE replacement, preventing incorrect code generation.
