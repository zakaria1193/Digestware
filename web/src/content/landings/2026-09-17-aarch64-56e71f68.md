This commit ports NEON fused multiply-accumulate (vfma) and fused multiply-subtract (vfms) intrinsics to GCC's pragma-based framework. It includes adjustments to assembly patterns for consistency and to correctly handle negated inputs. Additionally, it removes now-unused builtin functions, simplifying the codebase.
