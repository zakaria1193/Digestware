The GCC middle-end now folds `VEC_UNPACK_LO_EXPR` and `VEC_UNPACK_HI_EXPR` when operating on suitable variable-length vector constants. This enhancement extends existing folding capabilities for fixed-length vectors to variable-length ones, allowing more constants to be resolved before RTL expansion and potentially eliminating unnecessary instructions.
