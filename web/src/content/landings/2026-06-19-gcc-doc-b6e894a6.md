This commit corrects an issue in GCC's documentation where internal links (`@anchor-s`) incorrectly redirected to the end of unrelated nodes. By moving these anchors to appear immediately after their corresponding `@node` lines, the documentation's cross-referencing mechanism now functions as intended, ensuring users are directed to the beginning of the correct section.
