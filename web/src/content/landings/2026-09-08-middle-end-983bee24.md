This commit propagates a change across many parts of the GCC middle-end to use `build_int_cst(integer_type_node, cst)` for creating integer constants, replacing older patterns that used `NULL_TREE`. This standardization aims to simplify the compiler's internal representation and facilitate future maintenance and refactoring efforts.
