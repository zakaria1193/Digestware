This commit enables the use of `rori` (rotate immediate) and `xori` (exclusive OR immediate) instructions for generating a complemented sign bit with the RISC-V Zbkb extension. This optimizes the implementation of certain bit manipulation operations by leveraging specific hardware capabilities provided by Zbkb.
