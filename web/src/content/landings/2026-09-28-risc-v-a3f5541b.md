The RISC-V GCC backend now allows register group overlap for wide floating-point vector instructions, specifically `vfwadd.vf`, `vfwsub.vf`, and `vfwmul.vf`. This change aligns the handling of these instructions with their vector-vector counterparts (`.vv`) and leverages the Wvr constraint in the vector.md file, potentially enabling more efficient instruction scheduling.
