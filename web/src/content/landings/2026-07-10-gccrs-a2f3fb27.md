GCC's Rust frontend has registered the `needs_allocator` attribute as a stub. This attribute is required for compiling the `alloc` crate, but its full implementation is deferred due to dependencies on `global_allocator` infrastructure and the `core::alloc::GlobalAlloc` trait, which are not yet fully supported in GCC Rust. The stub emits a compiler warning when the attribute is encountered.
