This change improves loop optimizations by handling shifts with unknown or out-of-range counts. Previously, such shifts could be costly or lead to incorrect masking, impacting vectorized loops. The optimization now hoists the shift operation, reduces the shift count to be within the valid range, and correctly costs the operation, preventing unnecessary masking and improving performance in certain loop scenarios.
