This commit refactors how in-memory BFD buffers (`memory_bwrite` and `memory_bseek`) grow. Previously, they increased in small, fixed 128-byte increments, leading to repeated memory reallocations and copies for large buffers. This resulted in quadratic performance, particularly affecting operations like writing PDB streams during linking. The change introduces a geometric growth strategy, allocating an additional 1/8th of the current capacity, which significantly reduces the overhead and improves the performance of building large in-memory BFDs.
