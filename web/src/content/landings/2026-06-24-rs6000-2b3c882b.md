This patch introduces built-in support for 21 new Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) acceleration instructions defined in RFC02669, targeting future Power processors. These instructions, categorized into multiply-multiply, scaled multiply-sum, quadword add/subtract, merge, and rebase operations, operate on 128-bit unsigned integers using VSX registers, providing substantial performance gains for P-256 and P-384 elliptic curve operations.
