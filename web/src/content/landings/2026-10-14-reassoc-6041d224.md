The `reassoc` optimization pass has been fixed to prevent incorrect insertion of range tests. Previously, when a statement defining an operand also ended its basic block (e.g., due to a function call with exceptions or a trapping comparison), inserting a range test immediately after it would lead to internal compiler errors. The pass now treats such definitions like default definitions, improving stability and correctness for code involving complex control flow or exception handling.
