This commit corrects the parsing of the GCC-specific `__contract_assert` keyword used in C++26 contracts. Previously, the compiler incorrectly relied on string matching for `contract_assert`, failing to recognize the extended spelling. The fix identifies assertion statements by their parsed token rather than a raw string, ensuring both `contract_assert` and `__contract_assert` are treated as reserved identifiers.
