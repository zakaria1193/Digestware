The `vabs` family of NEON intrinsics for AArch64 has been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework. This update standardizes the implementation and adds comprehensive assembly tests. As a result, the corresponding `__builtin_aarch64_abs` built-in functions are now obsolete and have been deleted.
