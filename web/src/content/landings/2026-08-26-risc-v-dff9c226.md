This commit optimizes RISC-V bit-test branches by utilizing the `BEXT` (Bit Extraction) instruction. Previously, masked bit tests with XLEN-wide indices involved a `BSET` and `AND` instruction pair before the branch. By matching the masked form of `BEXT` when the mask is XLEN-1, the compiler can now emit a single `BEXT` instruction followed by the branch, reducing the dynamic instruction count by approximately 0.5% on SPEC CPU 2017 Integer benchmark 541.leela_r.
