This commit updates libcpp's build configuration to assume the 'errno' macro is available for C89 targets. It removes explicit checks for 'errno' in configure.ac and system.h, simplifying the build process by relying on C89 standard guarantees. This change aligns with broader efforts to conform GCC's build to C89 standards.
