This commit enhances GCC's pattern matcher to recognize and optimize certain bitwise expressions. Specifically, patterns like `x | (x >> (TYPE_PRECISION - 1))` and `x & (~ (x >> (TYPE_PRECISION - 1)))` are now transformed into `max(x, -1)`, `min(x, -1)`, `min(x, 0)`, and `max(x, 0)` respectively. This optimization produces simpler and potentially more efficient code by replacing complex bitwise logic with direct minimum or maximum operations.
