The AArch64 backend's cost model has been updated to accurately reflect the performance of Signed Absolute Difference (SAD) and Absolute Difference (ABD) reduction operations. This change accounts for extra work required by Adv.SIMD for 128-bit vectors and the more efficient SVE approach using `udot`, ensuring the compiler makes better vectorization and instruction selection decisions.
