This commit enables the use of the `clmulr` instruction for reversed CRC64 calculations on the RISC-V rv64 architecture. Previously, a guard prevented this optimization on rv64. The change adapts the quotient calculation to handle 65-bit values correctly, ensuring that the `clmulr` instruction's output is accurate for CRC64 operations. New tests are added to verify this functionality.
