This commit fixes a bug in GCC's `__builtin_clear_padding` where bit-fields within unions were not properly accounted for. The implementation now correctly calculates and applies bitmasks to clear only padding bits, irrespective of the union's active member, ensuring that non-padding bits within bit-fields are preserved.
