The Fortran compiler's intermediate representation for integer constants is now constructed more consistently. Changes in `trans-intrinsic.cc` replace a previous method using `NULL_TREE` with `build_int_cst(integer_type_node, cst)`, aligning with ongoing efforts to simplify and maintain the compiler's internal data structures.
