The PHI optimization pass has been enhanced to correctly factor and merge operations involving several builtins that require constant arguments. Previously, operations like CRC builtins could not be factored out if their third argument differed, leading to errors. This change consolidates logic into a new `factor_operation_ok` function and extends its checks to cover CRC, expect, object_size, clz, ctz, prefetch, and frame address builtins, enabling better optimization while maintaining correctness.
