The constant propagation pass (`cprop`) now caches the results of `find_bypass_set` on a per-block basis. This optimization significantly speeds up compilation for code with chains of identical conditional checks, such as `if (v == K)`. By caching the results of searching for register sets available at the end of a block, the pass avoids redundant computations, reducing compile times by up to 30% for the affected test cases.
