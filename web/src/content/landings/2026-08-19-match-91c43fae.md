This commit refactors the `genmatch` tool by combining four separate loops into a single loop. This optimization reduces the amount of generated C++ code, making the output more compact. Additionally, it ensures that the fourth loop is correctly enclosed within an `INTEGRAL_TYPE_P` check, resolving a syntactic issue with an extra closing parenthesis in the third loop.
