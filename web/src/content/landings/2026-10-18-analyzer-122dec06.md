This commit refines the type-checking logic within the GCC static analyzer's `kf_bind::matches_call_types_p` function. Specifically, it now requires arguments at indices 0 and 2 to possess integral types. This tightening of the checks enhances the analyzer's accuracy by ensuring that calls involving these specific argument positions conform to expected integral types, potentially preventing misinterpretations or bugs.
