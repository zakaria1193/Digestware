This commit rectifies a regression in the C++ front-end that caused a `typedef` declaration within a lambda, when part of a pack expansion, to be improperly skipped by the `cp_walk_subtrees` function. This skipping prevented `extract_locals_r` from correctly identifying the `typedef`'s usage, leading to subsequent substitution failures. The fix specifically modifies `cp_walk_subtrees` to correctly traverse the `DECL_ORIGINAL_TYPE` of a `typedef DECL_EXPR`, ensuring that these definitions are properly processed by the compiler.
