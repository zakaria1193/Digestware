primer_expert: The AArch64 backend's vectorizer cost model in `aarch64.cc` incorrectly
  assigned costs to `vec_deconstruct`. It was treated as a `vec_to_scalar` operation
  per lane, whereas the AArch64 hook charges it per-vector based on elements/2 + 1.
  This change reverts to per-lane costing for `vec_deconstruct` by treating it as
  a scaled `vec_to_scalar` at the `add_stmt_cost` stage, restoring the original behavior
  and resolving performance regressions. The `count_ops` case for `vec_deconstruct`
  is now unreachable, relying on the scaled `vec_to_scalar` logic.

The AArch64 vectorizer's cost model for `vec_deconstruct` operations has been corrected. Previously, it was charged as one `vec_to_scalar` per lane, leading to overly high costs. The update aligns it with the hardware's `vec_deconstruct` behavior, which is more efficient and prevents regressions seen in benchmarks and real-world applications like Calculix.
