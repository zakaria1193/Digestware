This commit corrects an issue in gimple-fold where helper functions for AND/OR comparisons incorrectly handled NaNs when inverting comparison operations. This could lead to incorrect optimization results, particularly when combined with `-fnon-call-exceptions`. The fix involves passing NaN-handling information to the comparison inversion logic and giving up if the inverse doesn't exist, along with new test cases.
