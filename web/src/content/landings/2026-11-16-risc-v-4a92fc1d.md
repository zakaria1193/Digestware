GCC's RISC-V backend now properly supports SImode (single integer precision) for masked bit indices and rotation counts. Previously, unsigned-short promotion on RV64 led to redundant masks. This change eliminates those masks, enabling native rotations and improving performance. Microkernels show reduced retired instructions and execution cycles, with gains up to ~14% in instruction count and ~20% in cycles.
