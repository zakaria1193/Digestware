The `build_ifn_call` helper function on AArch64 has been enhanced to accept `array_slice` and `std::initializer_list` for its arguments. This allows arguments to be stored on the stack rather than requiring heap allocation of a vector, improving efficiency and simplifying call sites. This change also fixes related call sites that were previously broken by this modification.
