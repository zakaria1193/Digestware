A new instruction type, 'fminmax', has been introduced for RISC-V to specifically handle floating-point minimum and maximum operations. Previously, these were incorrectly categorized as 'fmove', leading to inaccurate latency predictions. This change separates them, allowing for precise scheduling without altering current performance profiles.
