The patch resolves an ICE that occurs during the folding of `svld1rq` instructions when compiling for AArch64 with `-msve-vector-bits=128`. It addresses a failing assertion in `can_vec_perm_const_p` by emitting a `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` instead of a `VEC_PERM_EXPR` when the SVE vector length equals the quadword width. A new test case is added to verify the fix.
