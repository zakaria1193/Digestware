GCC's constant folding logic has been improved to more accurately identify when an expression cannot result in a signaling NaN. This commit updates `tree_expr_maybe_signaling_nan_p` in `fold-const.cc` to reflect that binary arithmetic operations and standard math functions (like `sqrt`, `sin`, `cos`, `tan`) always produce quiet NaNs, not signaling ones. This refinement helps optimization passes by reducing unnecessary caution around signaling NaNs.
