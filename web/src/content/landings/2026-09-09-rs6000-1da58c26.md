The `mma-double-test.c` test case for GCC's rs6000 backend failed on big-endian PowerPC systems due to an incorrect order of arguments passed to the `__builtin_mma_assemble_pair` intrinsic. This commit fixes the test by swapping the vector arguments when running in big-endian mode, ensuring the test accurately reflects the behavior of matrix multiply-accumulate operations on that architecture.
