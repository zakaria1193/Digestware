This commit corrects an issue in libgfortran's coarray implementation where memory for coarray parts was allocated based on the current team's size, not the total program images. This led to buffer overflows when child teams were formed, as images with indices exceeding the child team's size wrote past the allocated memory. The fix ensures that a coarray part is allocated for every image in the program, guaranteeing sufficient space regardless of team structure.
