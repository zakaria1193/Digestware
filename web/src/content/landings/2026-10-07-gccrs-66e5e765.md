An internal compiler error (ICE) occurred in gccrs when processing nested patterns within Rust multi-field constructors. This was due to misaligned sub-patterns and their types in the compiler's internal representation, causing an assertion failure. The fix repositions sub-patterns to align correctly with their types, resolving the ICE and ensuring proper handling of complex pattern structures.
