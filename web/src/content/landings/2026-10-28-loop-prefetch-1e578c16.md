This commit introduces saturating multiplication to the reuse-volume calculations within the loop-prefetch optimization pass. This prevents the multiplication results from wrapping around to zero when they exceed the maximum value of unsigned integers. By capping these estimates at `UINT_MAX`, the accuracy of prefetch and non-temporal store decisions is improved, leading to more effective memory access optimization.
