GCC adds PREINDEX_ST macro-fusion recognition for RISC-V, enabling the compiler to combine an in-place address update with a subsequent scalar integer or floating-point store. This optimization reuses existing indexed-memory helpers. The fusion is disabled by default, and its associated dump checks are XFAILed, awaiting explicit CPU enablement.
