The RISC-V vector Floating-Point Fused Multiply-Add (FMA) patterns have been rewritten to use specific `fma` RTX (Register Transfer language eXpression) forms instead of matching generic multiply-add trees. This change enables the compiler to correctly fuse operations into FMA instructions, even when floating-point contraction is disabled (`-ffp-contract=off`). The rewrite also addresses several pattern variations and fixes a performance regression in `vf-3-f32.c` by ensuring proper handling of scalar reuse and widening broadcasts.
