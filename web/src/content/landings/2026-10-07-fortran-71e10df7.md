This commit addresses errors related to Parameterized Derived Types (PDTs) in Fortran's namelist I/O, specifically for PR82173. The changes involve removing redundant NOPs from the data pointer in `nml_get_addr_expr` and ensuring that descriptors for PDT arrays are correctly passed in `transfer_namelist_element`. A new test case is included to verify these fixes.
