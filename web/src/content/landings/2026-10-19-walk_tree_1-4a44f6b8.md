This commit corrects a bug in GCC's tree-walking mechanism where the `OMP_METADIRECTIVE` construct was not being fully processed. This omission led to missing 'omp declare target' attributes in the generated code. The fix explicitly handles `OMP_METADIRECTIVE` during tree-walking, ensuring that all associated attributes are correctly identified and applied.
