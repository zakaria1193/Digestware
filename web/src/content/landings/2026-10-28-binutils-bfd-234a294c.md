The `RELOC_FOR_GLOBAL_SYMBOL` macro in BFD's ELF support previously included a check for `sym_hashes` being NULL. This check is now considered redundant because a more thorough check in `elf_link_read_relocs_from_section` already handles potential issues with symbol hashes. Removing this duplicate check simplifies the code.
