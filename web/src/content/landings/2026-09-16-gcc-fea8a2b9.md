This commit enhances GCC's value relation tracking by ensuring that normal relations are preferred over partial equivalences when both exist. Partial equivalences are now excluded from the path oracle and are not combined with normal relations during recording. This prevents spurious connections in the value graph and improves the accuracy of optimizations like Value Range Propagation (VRP).
