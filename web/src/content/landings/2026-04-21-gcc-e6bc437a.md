This commit excludes the `cris-elf` target from the `gcc.dg/hoist-register-pressure-*.c` tests. These tests were failing because of a missed optimization that was exposed by a previous typo correction. The missed optimization doesn't affect many targets, and the exclusion allows the release to proceed.
