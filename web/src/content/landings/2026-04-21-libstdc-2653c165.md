This commit implements LWG4486, which addresses the duplication of the `integral-constant-like` and `constexpr-wrapper-like` exposition-only concepts. The `__integral_constant_like` concept is now defined in terms of `__constexpr_wrapper_like`, reducing code duplication and improving maintainability.
