The PE COFF linker has been updated to prefer weak external symbols with a defined fallback over those with a null fallback. This resolves a critical issue where a weak declaration (null fallback) linked before a weak definition could cause the symbol to resolve to address zero at runtime, leading to crashes. By prioritizing defined fallbacks, the linker ensures more robust symbol resolution for weak externals, aligning its behavior with that of the LLVM linker.
