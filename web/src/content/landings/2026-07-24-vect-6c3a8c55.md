This commit removes the setting of specific tree codes (like PLUS_EXPR or MINUS_EXPR) on synthetic nodes created during SLP (Superword-Level Parallelism) vectorization for `two_operators` cases. These codes are not needed by the downstream complex add pattern, which relies on the representative statement's code. Leaving these fields as ERROR_MARK aligns with the recent assert that restricts SLP node codes and is considered a non-functional cleanup.
