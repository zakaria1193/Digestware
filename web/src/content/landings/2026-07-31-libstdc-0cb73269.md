This commit adds C++20 feature-test macros `__cpp_lib_spanstream` and `__cpp_lib_syncbuf` to the `<iosfwd>` header in libstdc++. These macros indicate the availability of the `std::spanstream` and `std::syncbuf` features, respectively, as defined by the C++ standard. The macros are verified in the test suite.
