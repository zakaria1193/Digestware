This commit resolves an internal compiler error on SPARC targets related to floating-point register allocation. The error occurred when the backend created an invalid floating-point register to pass a 12-byte structure in slot 15. The fix adjusts the register allocation logic to correctly account for the field's offset within the slot, ensuring valid register assignments and preventing the internal error.
