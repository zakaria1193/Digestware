The libstdc++ formatting library has been updated to correctly format `std::float16_t` and `std::bfloat16_t` types. Previously, these types were incorrectly formatted by casting them to `float`, leading to potential precision loss and incorrect output. This patch removes the specialized formatters for these types, allowing the generic floating-point specialization to utilize their dedicated `std::to_chars` overloads, ensuring accurate representation.
