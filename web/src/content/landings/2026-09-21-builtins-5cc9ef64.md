  optabs (like `TARGET_ISINF`, `TARGET_ISNORMAL`, `TARGET_ISNAN`) when they are defined
  for the given mode. Previously, `fpclassify` always expanded to comparisons, problematic

  applicable.

The `__builtin_fpclassify` function now leverages target-specific classification optabs, such as `isnan`, `isinf`, and `isnormal`, when available. This change replaces direct floating-point comparisons, which could lead to incorrect results or trap on signaling NaNs and subnormal numbers on certain architectures like AArch64 and LoongArch. The new implementation uses bit manipulation for improved accuracy.
