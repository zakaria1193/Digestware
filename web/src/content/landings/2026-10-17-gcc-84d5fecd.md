This commit introduces a temporary workaround for issues in C++ OpenMP lambda mapping, particularly with aggregate types containing lambdas or references to lambdas. The change optimizes the search for relevant declarations within target regions and adjusts subtree walking to correctly handle TARGET_EXPR initializers, aiming to fix problems where lambdas within complex structures were not properly mapped.
