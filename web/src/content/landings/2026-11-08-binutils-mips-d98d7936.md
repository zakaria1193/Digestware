This commit corrects the classification of the LSI MiniRISC CW4010 processor core to MIPS II within binutils. It adjusts the `aoutx.h` header to correctly associate `bfd_mach_mips4010` with MIPS II and modifies `gold/mips.cc` to ensure gold also recognizes this processor under the MIPS II category. This aligns with previous BFD-specific classification fixes.
