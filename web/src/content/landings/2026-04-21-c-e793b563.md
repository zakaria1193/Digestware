The commit fixes an ICE in the compiler related to expansion statements. The ICE occurred because the `TARGET_EXPR` had a `VOID_TYPE` `TARGET_EXPR_INITIAL`. The fix involves taking the cv_unqualified type of the `TARGET_EXPRs` before extracting their `TARGET_EXPR_INITIAL`, which recreates the `TARGET_EXPRs` with cv unqualified types.
