The GCC documentation has been updated to clarify that the Modula-2 runtime library plugin, `m2rte.so`, is dynamically linked to `libstdc++`. This information is crucial for users installing or configuring GCC, ensuring they understand all runtime dependencies. The change adds a relevant paragraph to the `GM2-prerequisite` section of `doc/install.texi`.
