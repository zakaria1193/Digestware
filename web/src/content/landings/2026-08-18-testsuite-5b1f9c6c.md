The test `pr119507.C` checks for specific COMDAT sections related to exception handling. Alpha emits a single `.gcc_except_table` section, whereas the test expects per-function COMDAT sections. This commit skips the test on Alpha, aligning with existing skips for ARM and SPARC, to prevent spurious failures.
