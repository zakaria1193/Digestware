The -mpc32, -mpc64, and -mpc80 options, which control x87 floating-point precision, are now functional on MinGW targets. Previously, these options were accepted but had no effect as the necessary control libraries were not linked. This change ensures that the appropriate CRT_fp*.o objects are linked, setting the x87 precision at link time. The behavior of these options in DLLs and new threads is also documented.
