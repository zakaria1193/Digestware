In accordance with LWG3869, GCC's libstdc++ has deprecated the `std::errc` constants `no_message_available`, `no_stream_resources`, `not_a_stream`, and `stream_timeout`. These constants are related to UNIX STREAMS functionality. The deprecation is marked with the `_GLIBCXX23_DEPRECATED` attribute.
