This commit optimizes string construction from `streambuf` iterators in libstdc++. By utilizing the `sgetn` member function, which efficiently reads character sequences, the construction process is up to three times faster for iterator pairs and ranges. This change includes new test cases to cover various construction scenarios, including those with sentinels and C++11 support.
