The internal iterator for `std::ranges::repeat_view` has been extracted into a separate class, `_Repeat_iterator`, in `bits/ranges_util.h`. This change allows the iterator to be used in other parts of libstdc++ without pulling in the entire `std/ranges` header. It also enables new overloads for container functions and improves how iterators are constructed and specialized, especially concerning sentinels and difference types.
