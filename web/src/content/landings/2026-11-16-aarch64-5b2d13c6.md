This commit introduces support for the `aarch64-pc-cygwin` target triplet, enabling the binutils toolchain (including BFD, GAS, and LD) to correctly handle compilation and linking for ARM 64-bit processors targeting the Cygwin environment on a PC. This expansion broadens the cross-compilation capabilities for the aarch64 architecture.
