The `get_scalar_to_descriptor_type` function, responsible for creating the type for scalar array descriptors, has been moved from `trans-expr.cc` to `trans-types.cc` and declared in `trans-types.h`. This change, addressing PR122521, makes the function publicly available for use in other parts of the Fortran front-end.
