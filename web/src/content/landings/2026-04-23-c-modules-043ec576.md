Fixes a bug in C++ modules where a pointer-to-member constant (`PTRMEM_CST`) might not be emitted during module import, leading to linker errors. The fix ensures that the member pointed to by the constant is marked as used during stream-in, guaranteeing its emission in the final binary. This resolves PR124981.
