GCC expands FP classification built-ins like isnan and isfinite into direct FP comparisons on alpha. These comparisons can trigger exceptions for subnormal operands, leading to program termination. This change replaces these direct comparisons with checks on the floating-point encoding in an integer register, preventing crashes with subnormal values. It also prevents unnecessary rewriting of these built-ins by the compiler.
