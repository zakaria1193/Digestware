This commit adds a new test case to reproduce a crash that occurred in the thread pass at optimization level -O2, stemming from an incorrect positional context used by the path solver. The issue arose when `match.pd` queried the solver with a statement from an arbitrary block, leading to segfaults due to incorrect edge lookups when handling PHI nodes out of their expected block context.
