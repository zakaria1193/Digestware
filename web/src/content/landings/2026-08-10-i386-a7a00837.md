This change refactors the i386 backend to separate MOVBE instruction alternatives from the `*bswaphi2_movbe` pattern. The MOVBE instruction does not affect the EFLAGS register, but the original pattern included a flags clobber that was only needed by other alternatives. By splitting the MOVBE cases into a new `*movbehi2` pattern, this unnecessary clobber is removed, leading to cleaner and potentially more efficient machine code.
