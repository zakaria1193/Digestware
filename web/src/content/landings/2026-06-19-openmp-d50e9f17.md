This update introduces new `GOMP_reduction_start` and `GOMP_reduction_end` builtins within GCC and libgomp. Previously, OpenMP reductions used the same `GOMP_atomic_start` and `GOMP_atomic_end` entry points as general `#pragma omp atomic` constructs, making it impossible for OMPT (OpenMP Tools) to distinguish between them. Compilers now emit calls to these new, dedicated functions for reduction clauses, which will improve the accuracy and detail of OpenMP profiling and debugging tools.
