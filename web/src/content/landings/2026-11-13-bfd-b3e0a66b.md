This commit adds a new helper function, `_bfd_elf_relax_delete_range_ok`, to the BFD ELF backend. This function validates deletion intervals during the relaxation process, ensuring that offsets and counts do not cause unsigned integer underflows. Such underflows could previously lead to heap buffer overflows or crashes. By introducing this check, the patch enhances the robustness of ELF relaxation handling.
