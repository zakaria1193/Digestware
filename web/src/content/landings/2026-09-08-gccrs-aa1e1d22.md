gccrs now includes a scan to track type-bound predicates within its type-checking context. This enables the compiler to correctly handle constraints on associated types in generic bounds, such as `P::Target: PartialOrd<Q::Target>`. The new mechanism adds a loop to capture associated predicates, improving the compiler's ability to resolve complex generic bounds, although performance is noted as an area for future optimization.
