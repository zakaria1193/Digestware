The ieee.exp testsuite was inheriting flags from DEFAULT_CFLAGS, which sometimes caused spurious failures due to the presence of ``-ansi`` and ``-pedantic-errors``. To fix this, the commit introduces a new variable, DEFAULT_IEEE_CFLAGS, that is independent of DEFAULT_CFLAGS.  Target-specific flags are now handled more carefully to avoid unwanted ``-ffloat-store`` flags when compiling for x86-64.
