gas now correctly handles the .cfi_def_cfa_offset directive when using a non-stack pointer (SP) or frame pointer (FP) as the Call Frame Analysis (CFA) base register. This change relaxes a previous restriction, enabling the use of .cfi_def_cfa_offset with flexible FDEs on targets that support this feature. The logic has also been simplified to prioritize error condition checks.
