This commit addresses a PR for the Modula-2 front-end by ensuring that the `makeSystem` script used in the libgm2 Makefiles is POSIX compliant. It replaces the use of `echo` with an equivalent `printf` command, avoiding potential compatibility issues across different shell environments. The change also ensures that `makeSystem` is invoked via `$(SHELL)` in the relevant Makefiles.
