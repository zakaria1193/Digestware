This commit resolves an internal compiler error (ICE) that occurred on the i386 architecture when floating-point arithmetic units were disabled. The issue arose from an incorrect option check in `i386-options.cc`, particularly when compiling with `-msoft-float` and `-mfpmath=387`. The fix ensures that SSE arithmetic is used when 387 is disabled but SSE is available, preventing the ICE.
