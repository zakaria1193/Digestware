The `_bfd_mul_overflow` function, responsible for checking multiplication overflows, has been moved from `libbfd.h` to `bfd.h`. This change promotes its availability across various Binutils components, including the assembler (gas) and linker (ld), which previously had their own `gas_mul_overflow` definition. Consolidating this utility in `bfd.h` streamlines code and reduces redundancy.
