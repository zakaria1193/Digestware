GCC can now simplify expressions of the form `(x % C1) % C2` when `C2` is a divisor of `C1`. This optimization, implemented in the `match.pd` system, replaces the nested remainder operations with a single remainder operation `x % C2`, effectively removing one division from the generated code. This is beneficial for performance, as division operations are typically more expensive than modulo operations.
