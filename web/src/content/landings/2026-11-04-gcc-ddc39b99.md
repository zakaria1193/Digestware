This commit modifies the `find_first_parameter_load` function within GCC's RTL (Register Transfer Language) analysis to accept a pointer to an `rtx_call_insn` object. This change is part of ongoing internal refactoring and improves type safety and clarity when analyzing function calls. Related functions like `sjlj_mark_call_sites` and `insert_insn_end_basic_block` are updated to cast their arguments appropriately.
