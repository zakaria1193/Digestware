This commit introduces new RISC-V Vector (RVV) intrinsics for the Zvbdota extension, which handles batched dot-product operations. It includes support for static source-format selection and accumulator VLEN requirements. The implementation limits the `ci` (count/index) by the physical VLEN when known and checks its encoding on scalable targets. Explicit rounding modes are also included for `vfbdota`, ensuring accurate floating-point calculations.
