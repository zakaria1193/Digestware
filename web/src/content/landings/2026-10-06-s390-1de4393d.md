A compiler internal error (ICE) on s390 occurred because the bswap:HI reg-reg splitter was incorrectly firing before register allocation. This led to invalid subregister operations that simplified_gen_subreg could not handle, resulting in NULL_RTX and a crash. The fix ensures the splitter runs only after register allocation, when it operates on hard registers and avoids paradoxical subreg issues, while also adding a safety check for NULL_RTX.
