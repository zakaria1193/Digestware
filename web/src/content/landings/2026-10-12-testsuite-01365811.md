This commit corrects the `vec_extractm` testcase to function correctly on big-endian PowerPC targets. The original test assumed little-endian ordering, causing failures due to differences in vector layouts for char and word extractions. The fix applies endian-dependent expected values, ensuring accurate comparisons and consistent use of result variables across all tests within the case.
