The x86/Intel assembler was mishandling equates that involved registers during instruction operand parsing. Specifically, registers were being converted to O_constant, which misled the assembler and resulted in local absolute symbols appearing in the symbol table instead of register symbols. This commit fixes this issue by cloning symbols/expressions as necessary before modifying them.
