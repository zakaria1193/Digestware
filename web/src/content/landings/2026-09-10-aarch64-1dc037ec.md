  instructions (`aarch64_<sra_op>rshr_n<mode>_insn` and `aarch64_<sra_op>rsra_n<mode>_insn`).

AArch64's rounding shift-right instructions were previously only reachable by their own specific expanders, preventing general code and the optimizer from utilizing them effectively. This change modifies these instructions to accept shift amounts as vectors, making them compatible with generic code and improving optimization opportunities, such as simplifying sequences involving addition and shifts.
