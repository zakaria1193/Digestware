This commit prevents the `get_scratch_register_on_entry` function in the i386 backend from clobbering the DRAP (Dynamic Re-Alignment Pointer) register during stack probing operations. While some safeguards were in place, not all cases were covered, leading to potential corruption of this register, particularly on 32-bit Windows targets where DRAP-based stack realignment is enabled. The fix ensures that the DRAP register, currently DI_REG, is never returned as a scratch register, preserving its integrity.
