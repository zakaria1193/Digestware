The LoongArch linker previously failed to correctly process local STT_GNU_IFUNC symbols if they were exclusively referenced from non-allocatable sections. This was because the relocation scan would skip these sections, preventing the IFUNC symbol from being found. This change modifies the linker's behavior to treat such local IFUNC symbols similarly to global ones, ensuring they are processed correctly even without a PLT entry, thus resolving bugs related to symbol resolution and debugging information.
