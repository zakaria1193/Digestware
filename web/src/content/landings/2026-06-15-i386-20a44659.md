This change prevents the GCC compiler from entering an internal compiler error (ICE) when encountering 16-bit modes for SSE registers in conjunction with SSE1 without SSE2. Previously, the register allocator would attempt to create invalid subregisters, leading to failure. The fix modifies `ix86_hard_regno_mode_ok` to reject these invalid modes, ensuring correct behavior and stability.
