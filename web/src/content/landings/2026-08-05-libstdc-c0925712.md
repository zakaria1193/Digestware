This commit enhances several internal bit manipulation functions within `libstdc++`, making them available as `constexpr` in C++11. Functions like `__countl_zero`, `__countr_zero`, `__popcount`, `__bit_width`, and `__bit_floor` now support C++11 compilation and can be evaluated at compile time. Specializations for `__rand_uint128` are also provided, and `__bit_floor` is optimized for specific types to avoid unnecessary dependencies.
