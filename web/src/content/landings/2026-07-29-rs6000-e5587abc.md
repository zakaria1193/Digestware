This commit resolves an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) when building GCC cross-compilers for Darwin. The issue stemmed from the `REGISTER_NAMES` array in `darwin.h` not including the Dense Math Registers. By adding these registers, the compiler can correctly process them, preventing the ICE and ensuring successful cross-compilation for Darwin targets, as reported in PR126438.
