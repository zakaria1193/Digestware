A C++ compiler ICE (Internal Compiler Error) occurred during name mangling for aggregate initializers containing `std::meta::info` types. This commit fixes two issues: it prevents `build_zero_cst` from being used incorrectly for skipped members of `std::meta::info` type, instead using `get_null_reflection()`, and ensures that aggregates with identical zero-initialized members are mangled identically, regardless of whether members are explicitly listed or skipped.
