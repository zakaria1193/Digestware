This commit adds the `exchange_malloc` language item to the GCC Rust compiler. This new language item is a fundamental requirement for the implementation of the `owned_box` language item and the proper functioning of Rust's box expressions. Its introduction registers it within the compiler's language item mapping, indicating its direct support and special handling by the front-end.
