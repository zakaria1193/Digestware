GCC has added support for the 'paddis' instruction, which is planned for a future revision of the PowerPC architecture. This includes new predicates, constraints, and instruction patterns to recognize and generate the instruction. The changes also ensure compatibility with existing instructions like paddi and addi, enabling more efficient arithmetic operations on future PowerPC hardware.
