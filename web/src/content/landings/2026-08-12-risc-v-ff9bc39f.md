GCC adds recognition for POSTINDEX_ST macro-fusion on RISC-V, combining a scalar integer or floating-point store with a subsequent in-place update of its base register. This optimization reuses existing helpers for indexed memory access. The fusion is disabled by default and its associated tests are XFAILed, awaiting explicit CPU enablement.
