GCC introduces POSTINDEX_LD macro-fusion recognition for RISC-V, enabling the compiler to combine a scalar integer or floating-point load with a subsequent in-place update of its base register. This optimization reuses shared indexed-memory helpers and supports various load-destination relationships. The fusion is disabled by default, and its positive dump checks are XFAILed, pending CPU enablement.
