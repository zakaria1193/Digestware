The minipool code, which handles the management of literal pools on ARM targets, has been extracted into its own dedicated compilation unit (`minipool.cc` and `minipool.h`) and placed within its own namespace. This refactoring improves modularity and organization, as the minipool logic has minimal direct dependencies on other parts of the ARM backend.
