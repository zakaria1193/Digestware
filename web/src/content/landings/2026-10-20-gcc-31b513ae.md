Several files across GCC's libraries and testsuite now define `WIN32_LEAN_AND_MEAN` before including the `<windows.h>` header. This change addresses potential macro conflicts, such as with the `abort()` function, and can also slightly speed up compilation by excluding less commonly used Windows APIs.
