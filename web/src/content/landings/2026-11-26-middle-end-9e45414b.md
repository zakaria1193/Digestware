This commit introduces new builtins, `__builtin_bitcompressg` and `__builtin_bitexpandg`, to optimize `std::bit_compress` and `std::bit_expand`. These builtins leverage target-specific instructions like x86 BMI2's pext/pdep and SVE2's bext/bdep for efficient bit manipulation. The implementation involves changes across various GCC components, including documentation, internal functions, and optimization tables.
