GCC's handling of special member function selection during overload resolution has been corrected to properly account for the 'explicit' specifier. The compiler previously assumed that non-'eligible' functions were not selectable, but this overlooked the nuances of 'explicit'. The change in `add_method` now ensures that less-explicit functions are not removed when a more-explicit candidate exists, fixing PR127627 and ensuring correct overload resolution for explicit special member functions.
