This commit adds new test cases to the RISC-V testsuite to ensure that the `vwabdau.vv` vector instruction does not cause register group overlaps. These tests specifically check that the source registers (`vs1`, `vs2`) do not fall within the destination register group (`vd`), which is crucial for maintaining correctness according to the RISC-V vector specification.
