This commit rectifies the costing for RISC-V segment store instructions and updates the costs for segment permute operations. Segment store costing was previously inaccurate due to incorrect scaling with group size. The segment permute costs are now aligned with benchmark numbers. These adjustments improve the compiler's ability to make optimal vectorization decisions, leading to better performance.
