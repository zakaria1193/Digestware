The cost model for x86-64 has been updated to assign a gain of one instruction cost to `pand`, `pior`, and `pxor` operations on `TImode` operands. This accurately reflects that these operations on 128-bit integers require a single memory load, unlike previous costings that might have implied two loads for DImode operations. This change should lead to more optimal instruction selection for bitwise operations on 128-bit data types.
