This patch enables the use of the SVE ASRD instruction for vector division by a constant power of two on aarch64 targets. Previously, such operations would use the general SVE SDIV instruction, but ASRD (Arithmetic Shift Right, rounds towards zero) is more efficient for this specific case, leading to improved performance.
