GCC has eliminated the `ptr_info_def` structure, consolidating its three constituent members directly into the `prange_storage` object. This change simplifies the data structures used for tracking pointer information within the SSA (Static Single Assignment) representation and is a step towards further refactoring of pointer-related data.
