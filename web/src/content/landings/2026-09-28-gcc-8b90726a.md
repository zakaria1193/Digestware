This commit corrects the order of comparison operands passed to `maybe_fold_and_comparisons` during the `ifcombine_ifandif` optimization. Incorrect operand order could lead to the loss of IEEE exception trapping for outer comparisons, which is essential for floating-point correctness. The change ensures that comparisons are processed in their original order, preserving trapping behavior, while noting that further refactoring might be needed for `and_comparisons_1` due to its reliance on SSA defs.
