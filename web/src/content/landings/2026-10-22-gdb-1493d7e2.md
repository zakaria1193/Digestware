GDB has removed redundant fallback definitions for O_NOCTTY from its source files top.c and inflow.c. These definitions are already provided by gnulib's fcntl.h, which includes a similar fallback to 0 for undefined macros. The commit also adds fcntl-h to the list of imported gnulib modules to explicitly note this dependency.
