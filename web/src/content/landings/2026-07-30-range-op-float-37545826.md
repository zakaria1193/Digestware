This change corrects how GCC handles infinity in reverse narrowing floating-point to floating-point casts. Previously, specific infinity range representations like [-inf, -inf] were not widened correctly, leading to miscompilations for certain conversions (e.g., double to float). The fix ensures these ranges are handled more precisely, especially when dealing with the limits of representable numbers.
