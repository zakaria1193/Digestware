The compiler's pattern matching system has been enhanced to recognize and fold two arithmetic idioms that are built from the negation of a value. These optimizations, which involve combinations of addition, bitwise AND, and negation, can lead to more efficient code generation, particularly for operations like rounding up to the nearest power of two. The changes also include updates to test cases and ensure that the optimizations do not interfere with overflow sanitization.
