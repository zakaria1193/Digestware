This commit ports the `vld1` and `vst1` families of NEON intrinsics on AArch64 to GCC's pragma-based framework. Obsolete intrinsic declarations and handling logic within the compiler are removed, and new assembly tests are added to verify the correctness of the migrated intrinsics. The changes consolidate the management of these fundamental NEON load/store operations.
