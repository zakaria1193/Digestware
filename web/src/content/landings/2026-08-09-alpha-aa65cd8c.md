This commit corrects a bug in Alpha's conversion from TFmode (quad-precision floating-point) to SFmode (single-precision floating-point). Due to the lack of a direct hardware instruction, the conversion passed through DFmode (double-precision) and encountered issues with tie-breaking during the second rounding step. The fix implements a round-to-odd strategy at the last representable fraction bit of DFmode, ensuring correct rounding and preventing underflow flag loss, which resolved issues in glibc's math test suite.
