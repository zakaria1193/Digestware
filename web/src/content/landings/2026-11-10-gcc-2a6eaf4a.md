This commit makes the SLP (Superword-Level Parallelism) reduction group discovery process fully recursive. This allows the analysis to discover and handle sub-groups that arise from analyzing previously found isomorphic or unknown groups. The improvement is demonstrated by a test case from `astcenc` which now passes SLP discovery on AArch64 with SVE, revealing eight four-lane SLP reduction groups.
