This commit refactors `basic_filebuf::overflow` in libstdc++ to eliminate the 'bump and rewind' mechanism for handling pending output. Previously, when writing pending output failed, the put area's pointer was advanced and then rewound. This change modifies `overflow` to not advance the pointer at all. Instead, it increments the size passed to `_M_convert_to_external` to account for the extra character. This relies on a related change in `_M_convert_to_external` and ensures the ABI remains compatible by introducing an overloaded `_M_convert_to_external` function.
