This patch introduces a new simplification rule in `match.pd` to optimize expressions of the form `((X >> C1) & C2) << C3`. These expressions, which extract bits, mask them, and then shift them, are folded into equivalent, more efficient forms. The optimization applies to unsigned types under specific conditions related to shift amounts and bit precision, potentially generating more compact and faster machine code, as demonstrated by an example on aarch64.
