This commit addresses an ICE (Internal Compiler Error) in the Fortran compiler that occurred after a previous change. The issue was traced to the order of deallocation operations for PDT components within the `gfc_trans_allocate` function. By moving the `gfc_replace_expr` call to occur after the deallocation, the compiler now handles these operations correctly, resolving the regression.
