The interprocedural analysis (IPA) pass, specifically within `tree-ssa-alias.cc`, had functions like `ptr_deref_may_alias_global_p` that could be called with SSA names representing non-pointer types. This could lead to incorrect behavior, such as accessing range information instead of pointer information. This commit adds guards to these functions to ensure they only operate on valid pointer SSA names, improving the robustness of alias analysis.
