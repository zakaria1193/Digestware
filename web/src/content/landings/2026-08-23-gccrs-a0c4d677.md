This commit makes GCC's target-specific built-in functions accessible to the gccrs frontend. By ensuring the `BuiltinsContext` is initialized and calling `targetm.init_builtins()`, gccrs can now leverage intrinsics like `__builtin_clz` or `__builtin_popcount` provided by the GCC target machine. This is a foundational step for supporting LLVM builtins and other low-level operations within gccrs.
