RISC-V support has been enhanced with the addition of TImode and OImode move expanders. This resolves an issue where sub-parts of vector registers, such as (subreg:TI (reg:V4SF R) 0), were valid but could not be split or moved. The changes define necessary move operations to enable these capabilities on the RISC-V architecture.
