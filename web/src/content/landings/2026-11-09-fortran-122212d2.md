This commit modifies the generated coarray accessor routines in Fortran to use `FL_VARIABLE` flavor for dummy arguments instead of `FL_PARAMETER`. This change ensures that these arguments are treated as modifiable variables, resolving issues where scalars were incorrectly processed by `gfc_build_qualified_array` and received unnecessary static tokens. The patterns in `coarray_stat_function.f90` are also updated to accommodate these changes.
