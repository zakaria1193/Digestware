This commit prevents floating-point unordered comparisons on i386 from incorrectly raising the `FE_INVALID` exception when encountering a quiet NaN. For AVX and AVX512, the compiler now selects the dedicated quiet predicate (`_UQ`). For older SSE2, where only signaling predicates were available for unordered comparisons, the compiler avoids these comparisons when trapping math is enabled and NaNs are honored, thus preventing unintended exceptions.
