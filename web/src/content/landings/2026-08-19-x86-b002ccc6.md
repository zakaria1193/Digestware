The test case `avx512bw-shiftqihi-constant-1.c` was failing due to the GFNI instruction set, enabled by default, generating `vgf2p8affineqb` instructions that conflicted with the expected AVX512BW instructions. This commit adds the `-mno-gfni` flag to the test's options, preventing the use of GFNI instructions and resolving the test failures.
