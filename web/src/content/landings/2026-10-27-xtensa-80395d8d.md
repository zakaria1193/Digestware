This commit introduces xtensa_rtl_combine_reached_p(), a target-specific predicate function, to refine instruction combination optimizations in the Xtensa architecture. It aims to improve optimization opportunities by differentiating between early-stage RTL passes that benefit from constant-value scratch registers and later instruction combination passes where such registers might hinder optimization. This addresses an issue where non-standard instruction patterns could lead to unintended optimization outcomes.
