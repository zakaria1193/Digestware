This commit simplifies the metadata export process in gccrs by removing the explicit re-export of macros. Macros are already captured and emitted within the AST dump, making a separate export pass unnecessary. This change streamlines the metadata generation by avoiding redundant information, leading to a cleaner and more efficient process.
