The i386 backend now more accurately costs scalar integer divisions and modulo operations when the divisor is a constant. Previously, these operations were always costed as a full divide instruction, even if they could be expanded to cheaper shifts or multiplications. This change prevents incorrect profitability estimates during vectorization, allowing for better optimization decisions.
