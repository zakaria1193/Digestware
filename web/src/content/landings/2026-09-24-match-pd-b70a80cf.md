A bug in the `match.pd` pattern matching for bit extraction and shift operations has been fixed. The issue occurred when constants `C1` and `C3` in the `((X >> C1) & C2) << C3` pattern had different data precisions, leading to an assertion failure. The fix involves converting these constants to `widest_int` instead of a common type, resolving the ICE reported in PR127552.
