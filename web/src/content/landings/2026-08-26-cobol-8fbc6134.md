This commit corrects the implementation of COBOL WORDS operands in the CDF according to ISO standards, changing them from keywords to quoted literals. It also adds support for GnuCOBOL's `$DISPLAY` directive as an alias for `>>DISPLAY` and reserves future boolean bit-operators like `B_AND`.
