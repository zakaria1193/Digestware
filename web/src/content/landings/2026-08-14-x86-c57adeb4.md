This commit modifies the x86 assembler to copy the `operand_types` from an instruction's template into a global `_i386_insn` structure. This is a preparatory step for a subsequent change that will remove `operand_types` from being directly embedded in templates. By copying them into `i.tm_types`, the assembler ensures that subsequent operations, like building VEX and EVEX prefixes, can access the correct operand information even after the original template is modified or no longer directly referenced.
