This commit resolves an ICE (Internal Compiler Error) related to C++ module partitions and anonymous namespaces. Previously, the compiler incorrectly included anonymous namespaces from other partitions, leading to duplicate definitions and compilation failures. The fix prevents anonymous namespaces, which are local to a translation unit, from being forced out into module partitions, thereby resolving conflicts and enabling correct compilation.
