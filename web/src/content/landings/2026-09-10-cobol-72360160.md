This update enhances the Cobol compiler's compatibility with GnuCOBOL by introducing several features. It now supports hexadecimal numeric notation (e.g., `h'41'` for 65) under the `-dialect gnu` option and recognizes COBOL-WORDS for intrinsic functions. The changes also include finer distinctions for dialect options and improvements to handling REDEFINES tables and potential undefined behavior in literal subscript out-of-bounds cases.
