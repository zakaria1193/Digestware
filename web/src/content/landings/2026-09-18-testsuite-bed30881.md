A test case, `gcc.dg/tree-ssa/predcom-10.c`, has been fixed to explicitly provide its expected output. Previously, the test derived its expected value from `argc`, but `argc` is not always initialized to 1 on embedded platforms. This change ensures the test's reliability across different environments.
