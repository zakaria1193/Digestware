This commit addresses a potential compiler warning on the PDP-11 backend related to uninitialized variables. In the `pdp11_rtx_costs` function, a variable `src` was used after being conditionally assigned based on `GET_RTX_LENGTH`. To resolve this, the assignment of `src` has been duplicated and moved directly into the two code blocks that use it, ensuring it is always initialized before use and silencing the warning. This change is necessary for successful builds with `config-list.mk`.
