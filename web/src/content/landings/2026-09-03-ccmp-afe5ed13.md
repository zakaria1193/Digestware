This commit improves the `ccmp` (conditional compare) expander by rejecting chains composed entirely of boolean values when no explicit comparison is present. Previously, the expander could misinterpret such chains, leading to incorrect instruction generation, as seen with `return x || y;` on AArch64. The change ensures that candidate recognition reports whether an explicit comparison was found, and rejects candidates without one, thus preventing faulty code emission and improving the robustness of logical operation expansion.
