The AdvSIMD search_line_neon implementation in libcpp has been significantly optimized for AArch64 processors. The new version focuses on quickly finding matches in short lines with reduced initialization overhead. This results in performance improvements of approximately 54% on Neoverse V2 and 42% on Neoverse N1.
