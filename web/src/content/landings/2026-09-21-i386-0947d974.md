The 'enabled' attribute for certain MMX instructions on the i386 architecture, specifically `negv2qi2` and `v2qi3` (plusminus), was incorrectly using a condition that could change during optimization (`optimize_function_for_size_p`). This commit modifies the attribute to use `optimize_size` instead, ensuring it remains a stable, pass-invariant property as required by RTL documentation, thereby fixing a bug related to optimization stability.
