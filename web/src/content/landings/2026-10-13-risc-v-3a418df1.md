This commit refines the RISC-V scheduler for the SpacemiT-X60 processor by accurately modeling that ALU instructions utilize only one of the two available Arithmetic Logic Units (ALU0 or ALU1). This change leads to modest performance improvements, particularly noticeable when vector instructions are removed from the `-march` parameter or when loop unrolling is enabled.
