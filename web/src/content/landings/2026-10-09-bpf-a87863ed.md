This commit improves error reporting for indirect calls in the BPF backend. Previously, errors related to indirect calls were reported at the end of the function, making debugging difficult. The issue was caused by diagnostics being issued too late in the compilation process, after location information was lost. The fix ensures that indirect calls are checked during expansion, providing accurate error messages and locations, and also addresses duplicate error reporting.
