GCC introduces a new intrinsic function, `__builtin_clmulg`, to support carry-less multiplication (clmul). This builtin is designed to align with C++23's `std::clmul` and `std::widening_clmul` proposals. It handles both standard and widening carry-less multiplications, with the latter being crucial for targets like x86 that have specialized instructions for larger-than-operand-size results. The builtin is type-generic and handles potential type-width limitations on different architectures.
