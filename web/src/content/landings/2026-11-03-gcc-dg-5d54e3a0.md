A test case in GCC's regression suite (`gcc.dg/20021029-1.c`) has been updated to accept read-only data entries in the `.rdata` section, in addition to `.rodata`. This change is primarily to ensure compatibility with Windows targets using the PE/COFF executable format, where `.rdata` is commonly used for read-only data.
