This commit refactors the bitint lowering process to correctly handle statements involving additions/subtractions (`plus_minus`) and left shifts (`lshift`). Previously, the code could incorrectly merge statements that were not mergeable, leading to issues. The changes introduce a more robust analysis of statement chains, ensuring that operations requiring specific states (like carry for addition or limb bits for shifts) are not merged with incompatible operations. This prevents potential errors and improves the correctness of bitint lowering.
