The `match.pd` file gains new optimization patterns that fold certain bitwise OR and AND operations involving `popcount`. Specifically, `(a != 0) | (popcount(a) == n)` is folded into `(a != 0) | (n == 0)`, and `(a == 0) & (popcount(a) != n)` into `(a == 0) & (n != 0)`. This simplifies expressions where the result is determined by the non-zero status of `a` and a popcount comparison.
