This commit fixes a bug where atomic builtins could incorrectly expand operands into memory locations, leading to potential data races. The `expand_builtin_atomic_fetch_op` and `expand_ifn_atomic_bit_test_and` functions now ensure that memory operands are first forced into a pseudo-register before the atomic operation is performed. This improves the reliability of atomic operations when operands might otherwise be subject to modification during expansion.
