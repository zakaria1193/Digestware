Libstdc++ now introduces the concept of 'segmented iterators' and a utility function `std::__for_each_segment` for traversing them. These iterators represent ranges composed of sub-ranges, and they must provide a special member function for segment traversal. This change implements this traversal mechanism for `std::deque`, `ranges::join_view`, and `ranges::concat_view`, laying the groundwork for optimizations like efficient distance calculations.
