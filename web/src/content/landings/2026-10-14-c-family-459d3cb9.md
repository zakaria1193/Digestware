This change introduces the predefined macro `__UNSAFE_MATH_OPTIMIZATIONS__` in GCC. This macro is defined when the `-funsafe-math-optimizations` compiler flag is enabled, allowing code, particularly in headers like glibc's vector math declarations, to detect and conditionally adapt to these potentially less accurate optimizations. The macro is also handled correctly for `#pragma GCC optimize` directives.
