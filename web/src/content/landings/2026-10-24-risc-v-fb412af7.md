This change addresses a SiFive RISC-V CPU erratum (MAL-9174/MAL-9191) where vector indexed loads can fail to restart correctly after a trap if the destination overlaps the index. New compiler options, -mfix-sifive-mal-9174 and its alias -mfix-sifive-mal-9191, are introduced to prevent this overlap. The workaround is enabled by default for the sifive-p870-d core. The compiler now uses a new `index_load_overlap` attribute to conditionally disable problematic instruction alternatives.
