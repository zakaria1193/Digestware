The GCC Rust frontend enhances its method resolution probe to accept a trait for filtering. This allows the compiler to pre-filter potential method candidates based on trait requirements, rather than scanning the entire crate. This optimization improves the efficiency of resolving method calls, especially in large codebases with many trait implementations.
