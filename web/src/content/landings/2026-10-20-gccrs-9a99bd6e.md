This commit improves error reporting for the `assert_zero_valid<T>` intrinsic in Rust-GCC. Currently, this intrinsic is not fully implemented and often acts as a no-op. The change ensures that the compiler always emits a warning indicating that the intrinsic is not fully functional, prompting developers to address the missing cases and paving the way for future implementation.
