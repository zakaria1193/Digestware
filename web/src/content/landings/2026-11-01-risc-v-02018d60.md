This commit enhances the RISC-V backend's ability to recognize 'UNSPEC' operations that can trigger floating-point exceptions, such as FE_INVALID. Previously, because these UNSPECs used integer modes, the compiler incorrectly assumed they could not trap, allowing the scheduler to move them too aggressively. The patch introduces a target-specific override (`riscv_unspec_may_trap_p`) to correctly flag these potentially trapping operations, preventing invalid speculation and ensuring correct program behavior.
