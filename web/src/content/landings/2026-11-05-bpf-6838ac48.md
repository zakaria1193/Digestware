A bug in the BPF backend's expansion of `memmove` caused an incorrect offset calculation in its backward copy loop. This issue specifically affected cases where larger chunks of data were being moved. The fix ensures the offset correctly accounts for the increment size, and new test cases validate the correction on x86_64.
