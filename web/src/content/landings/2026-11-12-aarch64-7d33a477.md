This commit ports NEON floating-point min/max reduction intrinsics (vminv, vmaxv) to the pragma-based framework for aarch64. While the AArch64 instructions differ semantically from GCC's reduction operations, this change ensures they are correctly handled by expanding them to UNSPEC_FMINV and UNSPEC_FMAXV. This refactoring unifies the handling of reduction intrinsics and removes older, direct expansion logic for these FP variants.
