This commit corrects a test case in the GCC testsuite (`gcc.dg/pr126464.c`) that was causing failures due to floating-point constants exceeding the representable range of `long double` on certain architectures. The fix adds preprocessor checks to ensure that such constants are only used when they are actually representable by the target's floating-point types, preventing spurious test failures.
