This commit migrates the `v{ld,st}{1,2,3,4}_lane` families of NEON intrinsics on AArch64 to GCC's pragma-based framework. The change cleans up the codebase by removing obsolete macros and functions associated with the previous intrinsic implementation. New assembly tests are introduced to validate the functionality of these ported intrinsics.
