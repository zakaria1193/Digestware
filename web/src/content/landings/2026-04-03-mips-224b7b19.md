An internal compiler error (ICE) occurred on mips64-elf during the build of libstdc++ due to an incorrect definition of `MAX_FIXED_MODE_SIZE` that didn't account for MIPS's TImode support. This commit removes the `MAX_FIXED_MODE_SIZE` override, which resolves the ICE.
