This change resolves issues related to parameterised derived types (PDTs) in Fortran when used with `intent(out)` dummy arguments. It enhances the `structure_alloc_comps` and `gfc_check_pdt_dummy` functions to correctly handle components with codimensions, pointers, or parameterised kinds, ensuring proper initialization and type checking. The changes also ensure that deferred initializations are handled correctly for these scenarios.
