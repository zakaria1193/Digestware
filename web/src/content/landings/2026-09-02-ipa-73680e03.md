This commit addresses an issue in the IPA pass where `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` was folding aggregate constants with differing type precisions. When a bit-field and its containing byte had the same `TYPE_SIZE` but different `TYPE_PRECISION`, the conversion could lead to incorrect values, especially on big-endian systems. The change requires matching precision between the aggregate constant and the condition's type, preventing misinterpretation and ensuring correct predicate evaluation.
