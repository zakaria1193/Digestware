This commit refines the vectorizer's handling of strided gather and scatter operations by accounting for loop peeling used for alignment. Previously, the offset bounds could be incorrectly lowered, leading to potential memory access errors, particularly on AArch64 with SVE. The update ensures that offset bounds are not prematurely reduced, preserving correctness.
