This commit enhances several functions in emit-rtl.cc and except.cc by using rtx_sequence to iterate over instruction sequences. This change improves the robustness of operations like resetting flags, verifying RTL sharing, and extracting exception handling information. The functions get_first_nonnote_insn and others now consistently use rtx_sequence for traversal.
