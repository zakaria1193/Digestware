This commit introduces the `WORDEN` operator for Algol 68 programs compiled with GCC. The operator is defined for `SIZETY int`, `SIZETY real`, and `SIZETY bits`, allowing conversions to their corresponding 'word' representations through truncation or extension. This functionality is added to the compiler's Algol 68 library without including documentation or tests, which are planned for separate commits.
