The Points-To Analysis (PTA) in GCC has been enhanced to track whether a points-to solution involves local automatic variables. This is achieved by introducing new functions and modifying existing ones to record the presence of such variables, improving the precision and accuracy of alias analysis, especially concerning stack-allocated data.
