This commit addresses an issue where the `packh` instruction was not being generated correctly when both the Zbb and Zbkb RISC-V extensions were enabled. It corrects the cost model for masked byte shifts to accurately reflect a single instruction cost for `packh` under Zbkb, and refines instruction selection to prevent the generic shift-reverse pattern from incorrectly taking precedence over the dedicated `packh` pattern.
