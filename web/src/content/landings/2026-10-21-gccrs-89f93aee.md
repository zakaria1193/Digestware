The gccrs compiler now has a basic implementation for the `breakpoint` intrinsic. This intrinsic is used for debugging purposes. Currently, on x86 architectures, it uses the `ud2` instruction to trap, as there isn't a direct equivalent to LLVM's `debugtrap` operation available in GCC's backend. A new test case is included.
