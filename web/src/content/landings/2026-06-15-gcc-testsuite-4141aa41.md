This commit updates the GCC testsuite to skip several Link-Time Optimization (LTO) tests for extended and simple assembly on 64-bit Solaris/SPARC systems. These tests consistently fail to link due to a `R_SPARC_32` relocation error, a problem recently introduced in the Solaris linker `ld` and previously present with `gld`. The tests are now conditionally skipped to prevent spurious failures during test runs on this specific architecture, as the underlying linker issue is external to GCC.
