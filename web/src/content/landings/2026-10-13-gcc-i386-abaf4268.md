This commit adjusts the cost model for double-word zero-extensions on Skylake and Ice Lake microarchitectures. The previous cost led to suboptimal optimization choices, such as preventing Store-to-Vector (STV) conversions by causing redundant zero-extensions. By using the 'add' cost, which accounts for the upper word clearing, the optimizer can now make more efficient decisions.
