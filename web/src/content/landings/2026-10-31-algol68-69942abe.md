The Algol68 compiler's type widening logic has been updated to correctly handle indirect widening paths. Specifically, 'long int' can now be indirectly widened to 'long compl', and 'word int' to 'word compl'. This ensures more accurate type coercion and prevents potential issues in mixed-type operations within the Algol68 subset of GCC.
