This commit fixes an issue in gccrs where `const` blocks appearing at the beginning of a statement were incorrectly parsed as constant items, leading to failures. The fix ensures these blocks are now parsed as expressions, similar to how `unsafe` blocks are handled, resolving Rust-GCC/gccrs#4997.
