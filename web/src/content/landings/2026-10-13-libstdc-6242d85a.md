This commit enables most of the C++ `<chrono>` library for freestanding environments. This was achieved by adjusting feature test macros and moving declarations outside of hosted-only groups. Functionality that relies on host-specific features like `now()` member functions, `file_clock`, and `tzdb` access has been excluded or adapted. Conversions to/from `std::time_t` now use `long long` in freestanding mode, and leap second information uses a hardcoded list.
