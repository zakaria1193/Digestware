This commit enhances the `cond_expr_convert_p` in `match.pd` to correctly handle operand precision. Previously, it only checked `TYPE_SIZE`, which could lead to an internal compiler error (ICE) during range analysis when integral operands of different precisions (e.g., `_BitInt(17)` vs `unsigned int`) were compared. The check now ensures that integral arms are convertible to each other, allowing for differences in sign.
