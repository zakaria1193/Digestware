This commit optimizes the formatting library in libstdc++ by adding a partial specialization of `_Iter_sink` for `std::ostreambuf_iterator`. This specialization leverages bulk `sputn` writes and zero-copy put-area operations, replacing per-character `sputc` calls. It ensures that all counting and truncation logic is handled within the specialization, allowing the base `_Streambuf_sink` to remain a pure I/O layer and enabling accurate calculation of total output length.
