GCC has added support for saturating subtract instructions that may be included in a future PowerPC architecture. Instead of relying on a specific built-in function, the compiler now automatically recognizes code patterns where a saturating subtract can be generated. This change enables more efficient handling of arithmetic operations that need to clamp results within a defined range.
