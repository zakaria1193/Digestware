This commit abstracts the `pt_solution` API for SSA names in GCC, ensuring that the `pt_solution` field is only accessed via a const pointer. Access is now managed through dedicated functions like `ssa_name_pt_solution()` and `set_ssa_name_pt_solution()`. This change removes references to the `ptr_info_def` structure, facilitating its eventual removal and making `pt_solution` a direct member of `prange_storage`.
