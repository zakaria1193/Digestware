GCC has added support for macro-fusion of SUB/SUBW instructions followed by SEQZ (set if equal to zero) or SNEZ (set if not equal to zero) on RISC-V architectures. This optimization occurs when the SUB/SUBW instruction has the necessary producer-consumer relationship with the SEQZ/SNEZ instruction and their destination registers align correctly. This new fusion is disabled by default and is validated with a new test case.
