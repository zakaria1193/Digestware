This commit fixes a bug in GCC's `match.pd` file related to folding `CTZ` (Count Trailing Zeros) comparisons. The fold incorrectly applied when the value at zero for `CTZ` was less than the comparison constant, leading to incorrect results and potential miscompilation on targets like GCN and certain RS6000 configurations. The fix ensures the fold only applies when the value at zero is greater than or equal to the comparison constant, resolving issues reported in PR95527.
