A new `get_bitmask` method has been added to `prange_storage` in GCC. This function allows for the efficient extraction of a bitmask directly from the storage, avoiding the overhead of constructing a full `prange` object. This optimization is utilized in `get_nonzero_bits_1` and `get_ptr_info_alignment` to improve performance.
