GCC's libgomp library has been updated to include overflow checks in its aligned memory allocation functions, `gomp_aligned_alloc` and `omp_aligned_alloc`. This addresses potential vulnerabilities, particularly on platforms like MinGW where `_aligned_alloc` might not perform its own overflow checking, ensuring safer memory management.
