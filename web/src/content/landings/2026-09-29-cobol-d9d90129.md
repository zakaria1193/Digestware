This commit enhances the processing of `CALL` statements in GCC's Cobol compiler, particularly when the `-fno-static-call` option is active. It ensures correct behavior for calls that are the final statement in a source module and properly manages the `ON EXCEPTION` clause, regardless of whether exception processing is enabled.
