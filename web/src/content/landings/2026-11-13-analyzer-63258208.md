This commit enhances the GCC Analyzer by adding new selftests and more member functions to the `complexity` class. These additions include a new constructor, `from_child_complexities` method, and overloaded `operator+` and `operator+=` for the `complexity` class. A dedicated selftest function, `analyzer_complexity_cc_tests`, is introduced and registered to verify these new capabilities, improving the testing and robustness of the analyzer's complexity analysis.
