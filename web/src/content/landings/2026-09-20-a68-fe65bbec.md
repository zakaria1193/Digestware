The Algol-68 compiler's prelude has been extended with new definitions for fixed-sized modes, including `shortmaxbits` and `shortshortmaxbits`. This commit also introduces new standard definitions for these modes and associated lowering functions for mathematical operations like sine, cosine, and tangent.
