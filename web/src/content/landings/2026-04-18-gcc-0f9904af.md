This commit fixes an internal compiler error (ICE) in `gimplify_var_or_parm_decl` in the D frontend. The assert() should always have the same result type as its inferred expression type. It also moves the pattern of calling `BUILT_IN_TRAP` to its own codegen function, `build_trap_call`, as it is repeated throughout the frontend.
