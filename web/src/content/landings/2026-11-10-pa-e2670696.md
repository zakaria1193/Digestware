The HOPa compiler component's `read_only_operand` predicate incorrectly assumed constant pools were always in the TEXT section for non-PIC code. This assumption was specific to 32-bit HP-UX. The predicate has been updated to explicitly check for `!TARGET_HPUX`, ensuring that constant pool logic is correctly applied to non-HP-UX PA-RISC targets like pa-linux.
