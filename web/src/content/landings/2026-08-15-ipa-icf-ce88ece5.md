This commit fixes a bug in GCC's Inter-Function Cloning (ICF) pass where it failed to consider the non-null contract promises of functions. Previously, ICF could merge identical function bodies even if they made different guarantees about their arguments' non-nullability. This could lead to a function with a strict non-null promise being unified with one that accepts nulls, subsequently causing a null pointer dereference when called via the latter symbol. The changes ensure that ICF correctly compares function type attributes and differentiates between METHOD_TYPE and FUNCTION_TYPE to prevent such dangerous merges.
