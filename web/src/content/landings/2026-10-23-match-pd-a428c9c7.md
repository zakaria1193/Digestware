The pattern matching rule in match.pd that folds ORDERED and NE comparisons to LTGT has been adjusted to correctly handle floating-point NaNs and trapping math. Previously, this rule could raise an invalid operation exception even with non-trapping math due to how it interacted with GENERIC code. The fix restricts the fold to cases where trapping math is disabled or NaNs are not honored, aligning with the behavior of related rules and ensuring correct comparison semantics.
