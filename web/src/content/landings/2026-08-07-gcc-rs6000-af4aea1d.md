The option `-mblock-ops-vector-pair` for the RS/6000 and PowerPC targets has been reverted to its undocumented status. This change removes the option from the GCC documentation (`invoke.texi`) and undoes its classification as a documented feature within the compiler's option table (`rs6000.opt`).
