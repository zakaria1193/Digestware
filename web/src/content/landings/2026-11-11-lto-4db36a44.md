This commit improves LTO's handling of DWARF debug information by preserving input DIE references before SCC unification. When an instance's origin tree is replaced due to SCC unification or prevailing-declaration, the original origin can be lost. This change saves these origins by reader-cache index and resolves them later for accurate DWARF generation, ensuring that the debugger can correctly trace entities back to their source inputs even after transformations.
