This commit introduces two missing instruction pattern splitters for the RISC-V zicond extension, specifically for combined (LT/LTU & EQ/NE) and (LE/LEU & EQ/NE) conditions. An LLVM review of x264 code on RISC-V revealed these omissions, where the operands for logical AND operations could be in either order, but not all splitters supported reversed operands. Adding these splitters resolves the oversight and is expected to yield a significant reduction in instruction count for x264, while maintaining compatibility.
