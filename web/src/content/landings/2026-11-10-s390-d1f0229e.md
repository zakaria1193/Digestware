This commit corrects inaccuracies in the s390 assembler's documentation for the RIS and RSI instruction formats. It clarifies that the second opcode field in the RIS format begins at bit 40, and adjusts the documented length of the RSI instruction to 4 bytes. These changes ensure the documentation accurately reflects the z/Architecture Principles of Operation.
