GCC's optimizer sometimes failed to recognize that the `_M_engaged` flag in `std::optional` is false after its destructor runs, especially if the destructor involved external calls. This could lead to uninitialized warnings. This commit adds a `__builtin_unreachable()` call after the destructor if `_M_engaged` is still true, explicitly informing the optimizer that `_M_engaged` must be false post-destruction. This helps prevent spurious warnings and improves optimization. This fixes PR127005.
