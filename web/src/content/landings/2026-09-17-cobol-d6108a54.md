The Cobol compiler's routine for formatting numeric strings according to PICTURE clauses has been refactored for performance. A new specific routine for ASCII strings eliminates costly encoding conversions. The underlying conversion logic has also been optimized, resulting in an approximately eight-fold speed improvement for this operation. Changes include modifications in `genapi.cc`, `move.cc`, `parse.y`, `symbols.cc`, and `util.cc`, along with updates to `libgcobol`.
