The x86 `cpuid.h` header has been updated to correctly pass the leaf argument to `__get_cpuid_max`. This change resolves regressions in the testsuite, specifically a vectorization test that failed due to incorrect CPUID information being used. The fix involves directly passing the leaf argument and applying a mask before invoking the CPUID instruction.
