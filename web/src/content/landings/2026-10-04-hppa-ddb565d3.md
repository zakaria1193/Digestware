This commit corrects the `canonicalize_funcptr_for_compare` expander on the HPPA architecture. Previously, it could fail when comparing a function pointer with a `SYMBOL_REF` that did not represent a function address (e.g., a data or text symbol). The fix ensures that such cases are handled directly, emitting a move instruction to bypass unnecessary libcall wrappers and preventing potential issues.
