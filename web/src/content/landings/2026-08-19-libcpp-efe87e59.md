The `search_line_sve2` function in `libcpp/lex.cc` has been optimized to improve performance and code simplicity. Similar to its AdvSIMD counterpart, it now loops until a match is found, even with unaligned input pointers or when near the end of the buffer. This relies on `CPP_BUFFER_PADDING` being at least 256 bytes (the maximum SVE vector length) and assumes a match exists within the buffer. Performance testing shows a 1-2% improvement on a trace of libcpp calls.
