This commit introduces a new optimization in `match.pd` that transforms a specific bitwise pattern into an unsigned range test. The pattern `((X + C1) | (C2 - X)) < 0` is equivalent to checking if `X < -C1` or `X > C2`. This is further simplified to an unsigned comparison `(X + C1) > C1 + C2`. The optimization is applied to various spellings of this pattern, including those with constants C1 or C2 equal to zero, and is restricted to GIMPLE intermediate representation to avoid issues with sanitizers or GENERIC code.
