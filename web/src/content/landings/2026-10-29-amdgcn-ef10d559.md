This patch improves performance on AMD GCN targets by over-aligning 'omp simd array' variables to 256 bytes, up to a maximum. These variables, often used for SIMD reductions, are accessed as vectors but declared as arrays, leading to suboptimal alignment. Benchmarks show significant speedups in dot product kernels, with double-precision bandwidth increasing from 565 GB/s to 630 GB/s. The patch also caps vector type alignment to 256 bytes, preventing stack space waste from excessive natural alignments.
