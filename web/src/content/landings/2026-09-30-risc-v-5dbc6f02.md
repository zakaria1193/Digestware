GCC now supports constructing full-width vectors on RISC-V architectures by concatenating two half-width vectors. This is achieved by implementing new `vec_concat` and `vec_init` expanders that utilize the `vslideup` instruction. The support has been extended to include half-precision floating-point formats (Float16 and BFloat16) and general vector half modes. This enhancement allows for more flexible vector initialization, particularly useful in autovectorization efforts.
