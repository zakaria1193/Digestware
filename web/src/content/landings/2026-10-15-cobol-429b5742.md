This commit modifies the behavior of `sequential_file_write` in `libgcobol/gfileio.cc` to ensure that `SEQUENTIAL` files always operate with `ADVANCING 0`. This prevents the addition of control characters that could occur with `ADVANCING 1` on such files, ensuring more predictable file handling.
