A new lint in gccrs warns about Rust enums marked with `#[repr(C)]` whose discriminants exceed the size of C's `int` or `unsigned int`. Such enums are non-portable because C standards (since C23) restrict enum sizes, and Rust's `isize` interpretation for `repr(C)` discriminants might not match the C ABI. The discriminant is evaluated at compile time for this check.
