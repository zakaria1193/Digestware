This commit modifies the DWARF output generation to safely handle cases where the call frame information vector (`cie_cfi_vec`) is empty. This can occur when the RTL front end starts processing at a later pass, or on platforms that do not utilize CFI (Common Frame Interface) instructions. The change prevents potential crashes or errors by checking if the vector is unset before attempting to iterate over it in `output_call_frame_info`.
