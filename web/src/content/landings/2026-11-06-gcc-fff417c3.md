This commit removes two alignment fields from the SSA name storage, as alignment information is now fully maintained within the bitmask. This change simplifies the internal data structures and renames associated functions from `ptr_info_alignment` to `ssa_name_alignment` for clarity. Several files across GCC's middle-end are updated to reflect these changes.
