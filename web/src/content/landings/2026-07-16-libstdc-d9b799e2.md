This commit introduces a new class, `_Streambuf_sink`, to libstdc++'s formatting library. This class enables direct writing to `std::basic_streambuf` using `sputn`, prioritizing zero-copy writes into the streambuf's put area. It also incorporates internal tracking of I/O errors to ensure that format-phase exceptions propagate correctly without setting `badbit` prematurely. The sink is specialized for `char_traits<_CharT>` to guarantee the presence of the `__safe_pbump` member.
