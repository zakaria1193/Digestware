This change enables 128-bit vector smaxmin and umaxmin operations for the x86 architecture using SSE4.1 instructions. These operations do not require AVX512VL or AVX512DQ extensions. The relevant test cases, vec-narrow-1.C and vec-narrow-minmax-2.C, are updated to compile with '-msse4' and specify the int128 requirement for x86. Previously, these 128-bit operations were not available for ia32 even with the '-mmmx' flag.
