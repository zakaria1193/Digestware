This commit enables the use of SVE2 PMULLB instructions for carry-less multiplication (clmul) in streaming mode on aarch64. Previously, clmul operations in streaming mode were limited and fell back to less efficient multiply-based sequences. The new PMULLB instruction, available in streaming mode, provides a more direct and performant way to compute clmul, especially for 8-bit and 32-bit inputs. This change significantly reduces instruction counts for clmul operations, as demonstrated by an example that shrinks a 44-instruction sequence to just 7.
