The RISC-V vector extension now allows register overlap for dual-widen vx instructions, including `vwadd[u].vx`, `vwsub[u].vx`, and `vwmul[u].vx`. This change leverages the 'Wvr' constraint in the vector configuration, potentially improving instruction scheduling and register utilization.
