GCC's template parsing has been improved to avoid performing access checks on members found within dependent scopes during dependent lookup. Previously, the `strip_using_decl` transformation could convert a `USING_DECL` to a `TYPE_DECL`, leading `lookup_member` to incorrectly attempt an access check on a dependent type. This commit modifies `lookup_member` to skip access checks when the lookup result originates from a dependent scope, aligning with template instantiation rules and fixing PR124983.
