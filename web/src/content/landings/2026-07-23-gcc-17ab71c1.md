This change optimizes the GCC vectorizer by preventing the allocation of `stmt_vec_info` structures for labels. This is a cleanup measure as these structures are not needed for labels. The modification affects `tree-vect-loop.cc`, `tree-vect-slp.cc`, and `tree-vect-data-refs.cc`, ensuring that only relevant statements are processed.
