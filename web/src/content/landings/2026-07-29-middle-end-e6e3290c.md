This commit addresses errors in GCC's middle-end optimization pass related to folding conditional fused multiply-subtract (`COND_FMS`) operations. Typos in the optimization patterns led to incorrect negation of values and missing masks, impacting the accuracy of FMA (Fused Multiply-Add) optimizations. The fix corrects these pattern errors and adds new test cases to ensure robustness, particularly for SVE (Scalable Vector Extension) on AArch64 architectures.
