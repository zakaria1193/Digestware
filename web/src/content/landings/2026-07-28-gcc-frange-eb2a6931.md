The `frange::intersect` operation has been rewritten to perform pairwise intersections of sub-ranges from two range sets. This provides a more accurate intersection result compared to the previous method, which treated the ranges as single intervals. As `MAX_PAIRS` is still 1, the cross-product in the intersection will yield at most one resulting piece, meaning no functional change for now, but this lays the groundwork for handling multiple sub-ranges more effectively in the future.
