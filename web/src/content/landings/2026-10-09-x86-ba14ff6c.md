Binutils now supports the ACE V1 block scale register instructions, including the `bsr0` register and instructions like `BSRINIT`, `BSRMOVF`, `BSRMOVH`, and `BSRMOVL`. This update adds assembler and disassembler capabilities for these new instructions and includes Intel syntax dumps for ACE instructions.
