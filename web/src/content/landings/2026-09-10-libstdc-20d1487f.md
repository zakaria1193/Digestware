The standard C++ library's inplace_vector has been updated to address a deprecation warning related to type traits. The change replaces `is_trivial_v` with the more explicit `is_trivially_default_constructible_v` and `is_trivially_copyable_v`, aligning with modern C++ best practices and ensuring future compatibility.
