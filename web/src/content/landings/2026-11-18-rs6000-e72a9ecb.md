POWER10's Table of Contents (TOC) patterns, used for large functions, were incorrectly sized. These patterns, which expand to two instructions, inherited a default length of four bytes instead of their actual eight. This underestimation caused the branch shortening logic to miscalculate distances to labels, leading to conditional branches being emitted as short branches. This resulted in assembler errors when branches fell outside the +/-32KB range, particularly with -mtune=power10. The fix sets the length of these four patterns to eight bytes, resolving the assembler errors.
