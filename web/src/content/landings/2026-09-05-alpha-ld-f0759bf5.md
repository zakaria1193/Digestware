This commit enhances the Alpha linker to diagnose and report invalid references to IFUNC symbols whose sections have been deleted during the linking process. Previously, such references could lead to an `R_ALPHA_NONE` relocation in `.rela.iplt`, causing a crash at program startup. The linker now identifies these inconsistencies, reporting the symbol, relocation, and section name, thus preventing runtime failures.
