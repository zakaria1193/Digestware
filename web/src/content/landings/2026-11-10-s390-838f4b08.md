This commit enhances the s390 assembler documentation by clarifying how register operands are handled. It specifies that register operands can reside in general, floating-point, vector, access, or control registers. The documentation now notes that vector registers require an additional bit in the RXB field for identification, in addition to the standard four-bit R field. Storage operand addressing methods are also more clearly described.
