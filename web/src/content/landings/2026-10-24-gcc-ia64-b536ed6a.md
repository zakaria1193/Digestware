A bug in the IA64 unwinder's `ia64_copy_rbs` function caused it to copy eight times the intended amount of data when handling alternate signal stacks. This was because the function expected a length in 8-byte words, but the kernel and Linux callers provided a length in bytes. This incorrect copying could lead to overwriting live stack data or causing faults. This commit modifies `ia64_copy_rbs` to accept its length in bytes, resolving the issue.
