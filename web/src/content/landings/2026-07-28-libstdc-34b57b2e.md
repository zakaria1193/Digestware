This change disables compiler warnings related to -Warray-bounds in specific parts of the C++ standard library's `shared_ptr` implementation. False positives were being triggered in the destructors and `_M_dispose` member functions of `_Sp_counted_deleter` and `_Sp_counted_ptr_inplace`. By suppressing these warnings, the compiler output remains cleaner and focuses on genuine programming errors, without impacting the correctness or behavior of `shared_ptr`.
