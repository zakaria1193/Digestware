This commit enhances the postreload pass to recover branch conditions even when they are not directly carried by the jump instruction itself. Previously, optimization was limited to targets where the comparison was embedded in the jump (like RISC-V or MIPS). The change uses `get_condition` to find the comparison in the cc register, enabling common subexpression elimination for more cases. This optimization is guarded to prevent performance regressions, ensuring it's only applied when beneficial.
