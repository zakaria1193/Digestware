This commit improves the efficiency of GCC's backward threader by adjusting the lifecycle of the path solver. Previously, the path solver was created when the threader was initialized and persisted until destruction, even if not actively used. Now, it's created at the beginning of a search and destroyed at its end. This change, along with making the solver creation a virtual method, prepares for future enhancements without altering current functionality.
