The GNU Assembler (GAS) now optimizes XOR instructions on the x86/APX architecture when the immediate operand is all ones (equivalent to -1 or 0xFF for byte operations). This operation is identical to a bitwise NOT. The assembler replaces these XOR forms with the `NOT` instruction, which can offer better performance and encoding. The change handles various operand sizes and includes optimizations for legacy encodings where applicable.
