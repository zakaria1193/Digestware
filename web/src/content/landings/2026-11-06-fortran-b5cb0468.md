This commit resolves a double-free vulnerability in the Fortran frontend related to parenthesized array constructors. The issue occurred because allocatable components within such constructors were being freed twice: once when the temporary expression was handled and again when the underlying variables were processed. The fix ensures that parenthesized array constructors receive a deep copy of their temporary expression, similar to regular constructors, and that the `expr_may_alias_variables` analysis correctly accounts for them, preventing the erroneous double-free.
