This commit enables the use of the F16C and AVX512VL extensions for converting `_Float16` (half-precision float) to `_Float64` (double-precision float) on i386 architectures. Previously, without the AVX512FP16 instruction set, GCC would emit a call to a `libgcc` helper function. Now, for processors supporting F16C or AVX512VL, GCC emits a sequence of `vcvtph2ps` (half-precision to single-precision) and `vcvtss2sd` (single-precision to double-precision) instructions. This provides a significant performance improvement by avoiding the overhead of a function call.
