This commit modifies libgm2's configuration process to align with libstdc++, allowing it to detect and use clock_gettime and nanosleep even on platforms that do not strictly claim POSIX compliance. Previously, the configuration tests were gated by POSIX compliance flags, preventing the discovery of these APIs on systems like Darwin where they have long been available.

By removing this strict gating, libgm2 can now leverage these modern time-keeping functions on a wider range of operating systems, improving its portability and reliability.
