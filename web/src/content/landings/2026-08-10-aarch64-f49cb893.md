This patch adds support for several new AArch64 SME2p3/SVE2p3 x2 narrowing right shift intrinsics. These include signed and unsigned variants for right shifts on 8-bit, 16-bit, and 32-bit data types, specifically `svqrshrn_s8_s16`, `svqrshrn_u8_u16`, `svqshrn_s8_s16`, `svqshrn_s16_s32`, `svqshrn_u8_u16`, `svqshrn_u16_u32`, and their unsigned variants. The addition of these intrinsics expands the compiler's capabilities for efficient low-level manipulation of vector data on compatible AArch64 hardware.
