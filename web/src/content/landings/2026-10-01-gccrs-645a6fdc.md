The GCC Rust compiler now supports a range of atomic binary operations, including add, subtract, AND, NAND, OR, and XOR. These intrinsics are essential for lock-free programming, allowing threads to atomically modify shared integer values. The implementation updates intrinsic definitions and handlers, and renames a utility function for better clarity in resolving atomic builtins.
