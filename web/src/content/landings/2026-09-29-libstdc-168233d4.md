This change ensures that assertions related to iterator validity in `std::ranges::repeat_view` are always enabled, regardless of the bound type. Previously, checks for a non-negative current iterator position were disabled when the bound was `unreachable_sentinel_t`, which was incorrect. Additionally, internal constructor assertions are removed as they are unreachable in normal use.
