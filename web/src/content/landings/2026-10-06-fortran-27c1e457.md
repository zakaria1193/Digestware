This commit corrects a bug in the Fortran compiler where the `STAT=` variable in `TEAM` statements was not being correctly marked as addressable. This led to optimization passes (like `-O2`) incorrectly optimizing away writes to the variable, causing its value to be lost. The fix uses `gfc_build_addr_expr` instead of `build_fold_addr_expr` to ensure the variable's address is properly handled.
