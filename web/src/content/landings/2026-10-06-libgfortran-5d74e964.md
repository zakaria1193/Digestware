This change corrects libgfortran's handling of the END TEAM statement in coarray Fortran. Previously, it incorrectly returned STAT=0 even when images within the team had stopped or failed, violating the F2023 standard. The fix ensures that END TEAM now correctly reports a non-zero STAT= for such cases by incorporating a team health check after deallocating coarrays.
