A new pipeline scheduling model has been added for the aarch64 hip12 core. This model was evaluated using SPEC 2017 benchmarks and resulted in minimal performance regressions, with a slight overall improvement of +0.17% for integer rates and +0.12% for floating-point rates. The next patch will address a specific issue found with the 507.cactuBSSN_r benchmark.
