The Local Register Allocator (LRA) has been updated to correctly handle subregisters within its `get_dependent_filter` mechanism. Previously, the code would traverse subregisters without properly adjusting the register number, leading to incorrect analysis. This patch ensures that the actual hard register number is used, resolving potential problems where dependent filters could not be correctly evaluated.
