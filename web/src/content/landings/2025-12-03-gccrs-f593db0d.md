This commit enhances gccrs's ability to differentiate between compiling the standard library's 'core' crate and other user crates. This distinction is crucial for generating correct canonical paths, particularly during macro expansion and trait derivation. For instance, when deriving traits in a regular crate, paths should refer to `core::marker::Copy`, but when compiling `core` itself, paths must correctly point to `crate::marker::Copy`.
