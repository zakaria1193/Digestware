This commit improves SPARC's handling of hardware errata by ensuring that instruction window traversals for workarounds properly skip over empty instructions. Previously, zero-length instructions (like frame blockages or empty `asm` statements) occupied space in the window, potentially disrupting the intended sequence of the workaround. The fix uses `next_active_non_empty_insn()` to ensure only valid instructions are considered, aligning with other existing workaround implementations.
