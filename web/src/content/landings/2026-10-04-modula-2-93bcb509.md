A latent bug in GNU Make 3.81 caused parallel builds involving the Modula-2 compiler to hang. This issue was triggered by marking the `m2.pod` file as `.INTERMEDIATE`. The fix removes this marking and explicitly deletes `m2.pod` during clean builds, allowing parallel compilation to proceed without interruption.
