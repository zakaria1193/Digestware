This change refines the placement of the vzeroupper instruction on the i386 architecture to reduce performance penalties when transitioning between AVX and SSE code. Previously, conservative restrictions unnecessarily inserted vzeroupper even when no SSE registers were live across a function call. The update uses inter-procedural register allocation (IPA-RA) information to determine if vzeroupper is truly needed, avoiding the penalty for integer-only callees and calls where no preserved SSE register remains live.
