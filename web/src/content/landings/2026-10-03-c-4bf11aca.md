GCC now implements C++29 paper P3950R1, which clarifies that the `return_value` and `return_void` promise members in C++ coroutines are not mutually exclusive. This change removes diagnostics that previously flagged errors when both `return_void` and `return_value` could be successfully looked up. The `__cpp_impl_coroutine` macro has been updated to reflect the C++29 standard version, and tests have been adjusted accordingly.
