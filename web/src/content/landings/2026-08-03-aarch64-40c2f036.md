Vectorization of saturating narrowing operations on AArch64 has been improved by correctly naming the relevant patterns. Previously, the backend lacked the necessary `sstrunc` or `ustrunc` optabs, causing saturating narrowing loops to be vectorized inefficiently using clamps and permutes. This commit adds the expander and ensures the patterns match correctly, reducing the instruction count for such operations.
