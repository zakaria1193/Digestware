This patch introduces a check to ensure that the `-fwrapv-pointer` flag setting matches between the caller and the callee when inter-procedural analysis (IPA) decides to inline a function. This consistency is crucial because the compiler's assumptions about pointer arithmetic wrapping behavior can differ based on this flag, and a mismatch could lead to incorrect optimizations or code generation.
