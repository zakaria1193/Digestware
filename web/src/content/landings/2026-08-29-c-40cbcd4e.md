A bug in C++'s constructor handling, specifically when unsharing `CONSTRUCTOR` trees for array elements, has been resolved. The issue stemmed from `RANGE_EXPR` indices remaining shared after a shallow copy. This commit ensures that `RANGE_EXPR` indices are also copied, preventing modifications in one constructor from affecting others and thus avoiding incorrect initialization omissions.
