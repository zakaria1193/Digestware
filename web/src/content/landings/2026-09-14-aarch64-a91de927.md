A missing `#undef REQUIRED_EXTENSIONS` directive has been added to the AArch64 SVE and SME built-ins definition file. This prevents a macro redefinition error when the `-Werror` flag is used, ensuring cleaner compilation for AArch64 targets that utilize these specific intrinsics.
