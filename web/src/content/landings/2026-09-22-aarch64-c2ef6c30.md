This commit ports the `vld{1,2,3,4}_dup` family of NEON broadcasting load intrinsics on AArch64 to GCC's pragma-based framework. This migration removes obsolete internal structures and macros related to the old intrinsic framework, leading to cleaner code. Corresponding assembly tests are added to ensure the correctness of the ported intrinsics.
