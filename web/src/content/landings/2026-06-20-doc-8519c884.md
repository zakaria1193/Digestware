GCC's documentation has been updated to standardize the spelling of "compile time." When used as a noun, the phrase will now consistently appear as two separate words, without a hyphen. This change affects multiple documentation files, including those detailing integer overflow builtins, warning options, tree SSA passes, and RTL conversions, ensuring grammatical consistency across the project.
