An oversight in GCC's built-in function handling caused an Internal Compiler Error (ICE) when dealing with trigonometric functions based on pi (e.g., `sinpi`, `cospi`). These functions were not included in the `SEQ_OF_CASE_MATHFN` macro, preventing the compiler from mapping them to appropriate type-specific declarations. This patch adds the missing functions to the macro and updates `builtin_with_linkage_p` to resolve the ICE and ensure proper handling.
