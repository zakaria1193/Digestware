This commit addresses an issue in the forward propagation pass related to the optimization of long multiplication operations. Specifically, it corrects two ladder forms where the high part was masked and added, but mismatched cross-product operands were allowed. The fix ensures that the cross-product operand used in the added term is consistent with the one being masked. This prevents double-counting or dropping parts of the high-half of intermediate products, thus guaranteeing correct results for these optimizations.
