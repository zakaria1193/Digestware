This commit optimizes RTL sequences for boolean logical operations, particularly for targets like RISC-V with limited conditional instruction sets. It leverages De Morgan's laws to rewrite sequences involving OR and XOR operations. Specifically, it transforms `(ior (xor A 1) (xor B 1))` into `(xor (and A B) 1)` when `A` and `B` are known to be 0/1 values. This optimization can significantly reduce the number of dynamic instructions, as demonstrated by a reduction in x264's performance.
