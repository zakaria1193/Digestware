The `widening_mul` optimization pass has been refactored by moving the logic for splitting each of the two operands into halves into a new helper function, `build_long_mul_partial_operand`. This change improves code organization and readability within `tree-ssa-math-opts.cc`. While no functional change is intended, the order of emitted partial products for the two operands may differ.
