This commit simplifies the definition of SVE type suffixes in the aarch64 backend. `DEF_SVE_TYPE_SUFFIX` is now defined in terms of `DEF_SVE_NEON_TYPE_SUFFIX`. This change requires callers to only define `DEF_SVE_NEON_TYPE_SUFFIX` when including `aarch64-sve-builtins.def`, reducing redundancy and consolidating definitions.
