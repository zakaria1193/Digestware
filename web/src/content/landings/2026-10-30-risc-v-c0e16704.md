This commit adds test cases to verify that the source registers (vs1 and vs2) for RISC-V floating-point widening ternary instructions do not overlap with the destination register (vd). This is crucial because the destination register is also read as a wider accumulator, and such overlap could lead to incorrect results. The tests cover various instructions including vfwmacc.vv, vfwmsac.vv, and others.
