The RL78 ELF relaxation function `rl78_elf_relax_section` incorrectly returned `true` in its error path for out-of-memory or I/O failures. This allowed the linker to proceed with corrupted state, potentially leading to further issues. This commit corrects the error path to return `false`, ensuring that such failures are properly reported and handled.
