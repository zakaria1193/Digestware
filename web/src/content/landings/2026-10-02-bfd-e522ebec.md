This commit fixes a "union phase confusion" in how GOT entries are handled for `DT_RELR` relocations on RISC-V targets. It adjusts the condition that checks for no allocated GOT entry, aligning it with changes in how the offset semantics are repurposed after `allocate_dynrelocs`.
