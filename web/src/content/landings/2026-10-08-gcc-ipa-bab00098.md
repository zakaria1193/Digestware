This commit fixes an issue in the ifcombine optimization pass where signed bitfields were not being correctly zero-extended. Previously, a bug in checking sign bits led to incorrect evaluation of equality comparisons with sign-extended constants, causing some optimizations to fail. The fix adjusts how bitfield masks are interpreted to properly distinguish between zero-extensions and sign-extensions, ensuring correct optimization behavior.
