This change qualifies the definitions of `std::get` for `std::pair`, `std::tuple`, and `std::array` within libstdc++. This ensures that the function signatures precisely match their forward declarations, preventing potential ambiguities and ensuring correct behavior. An overload for `std::get` with an out-of-bounds index for tuples has also been moved to a more appropriate header.
