This commit fixes an issue where the C frontend incorrectly re-wrapped the result of the logical NOT operator (`!`) in an `EXCESS_PRECISION_EXPR`, even though the result of `!` is always an `int`. This caused problems with types like `_Float16` or when x87 excess precision was enabled, leading to incorrect `sizeof` results, ICEs in `build_conditional_expr`, or ICEs at the expansion stage. The fix prevents re-wrapping the result of `TRUTH_NOT_EXPR` while still testing the operand at its excess precision.
