This commit adjusts the IPA function summary generation to correctly process internal callback edges. It guards against missing callgraph edges for IFN_ASSUME calls and special-cases summary generation to account for the fact that internal callback edges lack a callback-carrying edge. This ensures accurate size and time information (both 0) is recorded.
