This commit addresses an assertion failure in the Ada compiler related to the initialization of extension aggregates containing task components. The failure occurred because a necessary pointer to the type was not being passed correctly during the construction of the aggregate's code. The changes ensure that the correct type information is propagated through the expansion and allocation procedures, resolving the assertion and improving the compiler's robustness for complex Ada types.
