This change optimizes the __builtin_mul_overflow intrinsic when the result type's precision is smaller than its machine mode. Previously, it performed an overflow check at the mode's precision and a redundant fit check upon storing the result. Now, it uses the result type's precision for the overflow check and informs the store helper that overflow is already handled, eliminating the redundant check. This simplifies the expansion and ensures correctness for such cases.
