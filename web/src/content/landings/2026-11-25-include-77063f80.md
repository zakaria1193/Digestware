This commit replaces the assembly implementations of `count_leading_zeros` and `count_trailing_zeros` in `longlong.h` for IA-64 with corresponding built-in functions. This change leverages optimized expansions within GCC, leading to a significant reduction in instruction count and improved execution cycle counts for these operations.
