When using Dwarf's DRAP (DWARF Register Allocation Path) for stack unwinding, frame pointer relative expressions were used to locate saved registers. A change to skip emitting CFA_RESTORE notes for registers in the red zone broke unwinding for DRAP frames, as these expressions became stale after the frame pointer was restored. This commit ensures CFA_RESTORE notes are emitted even for red zone registers in DRAP frames to maintain correct debugging information.
