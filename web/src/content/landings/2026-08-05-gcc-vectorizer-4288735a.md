This commit refactors parts of the GCC vectorizer, specifically the `vectorizable_conversion` function. It elides requirements for scalar operands `op0` and `op1` when they are fed by SLP permutations, which cannot be computed in that context. A new overload for `vect_is_simple_use` is introduced to ease this transition, and an unused scalar operand argument is removed from `supportable_indirect_convert_operation`.
