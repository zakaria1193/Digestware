This patch fixes an issue in the Inter Procedural Analysis (IPA) pass where the `modified` flag in `build_new_function_type` was not initialized correctly. This incorrect initialization could lead to attributes not being dropped when they should have been, causing problems like those reported in PR128602. The fix ensures that both cases—parameters being removed and functions initially having no parameters—are handled properly, leading to more accurate attribute management.
