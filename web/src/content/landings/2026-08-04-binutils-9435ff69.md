This commit prevents errors in the `.pre-commit-config.yaml` file that were caused by tab characters, specifically when editing with Emacs. Tab indentation can lead to `InvalidConfigError` during pre-commit hook execution. The fix adds a 'Local Variables' section to the YAML file, instructing editors to use spaces instead of tabs, thereby ensuring consistent formatting and preventing configuration parsing issues.
