This commit rectifies an issue in `combine_comparisons` where signed vector boolean types were handled incorrectly, leading to faulty optimizations for less-than and greater-than comparisons. The fix introduces support for signed boolean types, ensuring that operations like LT, LE, GT, and GE produce accurate results by distinguishing them from their unsigned counterparts. This change required updates across multiple GIMPLE folding functions to pass the new type information.
