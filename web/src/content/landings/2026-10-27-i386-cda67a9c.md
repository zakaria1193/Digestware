This commit enables the expansion of 64-bit CRC-32C calculations on ia32 (32-bit x86) targets. It achieves this by chaining two `crc32l` instructions to process the low and high 32-bit halves of XORed operands, effectively avoiding a `DImode` XOR. The existing `crc32q` expansion on x86-64 is retained, and tests are updated to cover both ia32 and x86-64 targets.
