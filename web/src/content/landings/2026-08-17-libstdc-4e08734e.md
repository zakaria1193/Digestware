This patch resolves integer type mismatches in `stdx::simd` that affected ARM targets, particularly `arm-none-eabi`. Due to `int32_t` being defined as `long`, generic `int` vectors passed as `int32x2_t` were causing compilation errors. The fix uses `intN_t` aliases consistently and ensures correct bitcasting in NEON popcount implementation, enhancing type safety and avoiding unnecessary reinterpretations.
