This commit introduces test cases for RISC-V integer widening ternary instructions to ensure that source registers (vs1 and vs2) do not overlap with the destination register (vd). This is important because the destination register also serves as the accumulator for a wider element width, and overlap could lead to incorrect results. The tests cover various integer multiply-accumulate instructions.
