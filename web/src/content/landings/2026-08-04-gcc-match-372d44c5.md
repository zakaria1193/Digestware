This change simplifies expressions of the form `(A | C) == A` to `(A & C) != 0` when `C` is a power of two. This optimization avoids a bitwise OR and replaces it with a bitwise AND and a comparison against zero, which can be more efficient on some architectures. The inverse transformation for `(A | C) != A` to `(A & C) == 0` is also included. A new test case verifies the optimization.
