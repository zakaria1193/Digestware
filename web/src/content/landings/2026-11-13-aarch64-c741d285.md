The aarch64 backend previously underestimated the cost of integer vector reductions that use dot products. This led to insufficient unrolling on cores with high dot-product accumulator latency. This change introduces a per-core `reduc_dotprod_cost` to accurately measure this latency, significantly improving performance for reduction loops, with a 1.9x to 2.7x speedup observed for certain loops at -O3.
