This commit introduces a new suboption, `analyzer-graphs=yes|no`, for GCC's `-fdiagnostics-add-output=sarif` flag. When enabled, this option captures detailed internal graph structures of the static analyzer, specifically its supergraph and exploded graph, and embeds them within the SARIF output. This feature is intended as a debugging aid for developers working on the analyzer itself, allowing for more in-depth analysis of its internal state.
