The RISC-V toolchain now deprecates and warns about the selection of privileged spec versions. Since the removal of support for older versions (prior to 1.9.1) means no CSRs are dependent on the specific version, the selection mechanism no longer affects the generated code. Tools like the assembler, linker, and objdump will issue warnings for deprecated options and attributes, directing users to binutils 2.47 or earlier if a different spec version is strictly required.
