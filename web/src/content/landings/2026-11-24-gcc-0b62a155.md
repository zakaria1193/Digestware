Previously, GCC's Value Range Propagation (VRP) incorrectly folded CLZ (count leading zeros) and CTZ (count trailing zeros) intrinsic calls when executed unconditionally, because the result for an input of zero was considered undefined. This led to incorrect optimizations and testsuite failures at higher optimization levels. This commit extends internal rewrite mechanisms to define the behavior of one-argument CLZ/CTZ for zero inputs, ensuring correct results and preventing optimization-related issues.
