The reflect_hwi function in hwint.cc previously used a slow loop to emulate __builtin_bitreverse64. This commit replaces that loop with a direct call to the intrinsic function when supported, offering a significant performance improvement. It also introduces STAGE0_CXX_HAS_BUILTIN in system.h to check for specific builtins, ensuring broader compatibility and better bootstrapping for GCC.
