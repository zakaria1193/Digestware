A bug in the ifcombine pass incorrectly stripped narrowing conversions from variables used in bitwise AND operations. This led to the compiler widening the variable and ORing in unwanted bits, causing miscompilation. The fix modifies how bit tests are recognized, prioritizing direct operand matching before considering conversions, thus preventing the resurrection of truncated bits and ensuring correct program behavior.
