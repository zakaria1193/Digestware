An internal error in the SPARC backend's `sparc_type_code` routine occurred when encountering an `OPAQUE_TYPE`. This commit modifies the routine to accept `OPAQUE_TYPE` similarly to other special types, resolving a test case failure in `g++.dg/reflect/null7.C`.
