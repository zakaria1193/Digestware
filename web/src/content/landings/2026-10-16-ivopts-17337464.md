The `ivopts` pass has been updated to more accurately estimate register pressure by counting hard registers rather than just values. Previously, the register pressure calculation could significantly underestimate needs on targets where a single logical value, like a 64-bit pointer on a 32-bit architecture, required multiple physical registers. This led to incorrect decisions about spilling and loop optimizations. The change introduces weights based on `targetm.class_max_nregs` to account for multi-register values, ensuring more precise pressure estimates and better optimization choices, especially on architectures like GCN or 32-bit systems handling 64-bit data.
