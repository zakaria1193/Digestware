This commit enhances the GCC COBOL compiler's lexical analysis to correctly identify user-defined words that are composed entirely of digits but may contain a single period, distinguishing them from floating-point literals. Previously, such constructs could be misidentified, leading to parsing errors or incorrect tokenization. The change ensures that names like '2E0' are treated as valid user-defined words, not numeric literals, fixing RT 3727.
