This commit resolves an issue in GCC's Fortran frontend where the scope of an index-name within a `DO CONCURRENT` construct's header was not correctly handled. Previously, type-spec references within the loop body could incorrectly refer to variables outside the construct, leading to wrong code generation. The fix replaces hand-written expression walkers with a `gfc_traverse_expr` callback and extends statement walkers to cover all expression forms, ensuring that references within the construct always bind to the intended index-name.
