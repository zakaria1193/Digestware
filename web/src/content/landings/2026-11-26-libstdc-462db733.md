The C++17 standard introduced new non-parallel algorithms alongside parallel ones. These non-parallel algorithms, such as std::for_each_n and std::reduce, are available in freestanding environments, unlike their parallel counterparts. This commit adds comments to the relevant preprocessor directives in libstdc++ to clarify why they check the __cplusplus macro directly rather than using the __cpp_lib_parallel_algorithm feature test macro.
