The MIPS assembler's handling of opcode flags related to multiplication and division instructions has been refactored for better consistency. The peculiar INSN_MULT flag, which previously lifted scheduling restrictions, has been removed and replaced with the INSN_DIV flag, which applies to all integer division opcodes. This change aims to simplify interlock logic and enable optimizations for MTHI/MFLO instructions.
