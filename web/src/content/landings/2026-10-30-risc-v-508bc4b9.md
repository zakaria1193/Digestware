This commit addresses a RISC-V bootstrap issue by correcting the usage of `__NORMAL_OP_TA2` in the `shuffle_interleave_even_odd_patterns` function. Previously, using this flag directly with `BINARY_OP_P` caused a compiler warning. The fix involves casting `__NORMAL_OP_TA2` to an unsigned type, similar to its usage in `expand_const_vector_onestep`, resolving the warning and ensuring a clean bootstrap.
