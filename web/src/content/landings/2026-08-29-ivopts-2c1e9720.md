The `ivopts_estimate_reg_pressure` function was previously biased by the number of candidates in a set, making larger sets less likely to be chosen. This change removes that bias, allowing the optimizer to more fairly evaluate sets with varying numbers of loop-invariant candidates, improving the selection of optimal candidate sets.
