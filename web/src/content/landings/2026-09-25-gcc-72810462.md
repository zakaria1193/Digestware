This commit corrects a logic error in GIMPLE's range cache where inferred ranges were incorrectly applied even when abnormal outgoing edges existed in a block. The fix ensures that ranges are not exported from blocks with abnormal edges, preventing incorrect optimization assumptions. Additionally, a testcase was updated to correctly reflect the optimization flag requirement, ensuring accurate testing of this logic.
