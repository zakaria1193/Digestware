This commit corrects an error in the ND32 target's cost calculation functions. Previously, `nds32_rtx_costs_speed_prefer` incorrectly returned the cost value directly in two places, instead of storing it in the provided pointer and returning `true` to indicate completion. This fix ensures the functions adhere to their expected interface, resolving a build failure observed with `config-list.mk`.
