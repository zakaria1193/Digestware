This commit introduces a comprehensive suite of linker tests for the RISC-V Zcmt extension, focusing on table-jump relaxation. The tests cover various aspects including table generation, profitability analysis, relocation handling, symbol management, relaxation ordering, and unsupported link modes. They also exercise the option parser and global relaxation controls, ensuring that emitted relocations adhere to expected formats.
