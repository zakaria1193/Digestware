This commit corrects how registers are defined within the `register_outgoing_edges` function in the range-fold optimization pass. By moving the definitions of registers `r1` and `r2` into the specific branches where they are used, the compiler ensures that these registers' memory is freed correctly. This addresses a pre-existing issue that was exacerbated by a recent patch, improving the pass's robustness.
