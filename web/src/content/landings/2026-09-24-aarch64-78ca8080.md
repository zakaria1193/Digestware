The NEON SHA256 intrinsics, supporting the FEAT_SHA2 feature, have been ported to GCC's pragma-based framework. This change also introduces assembly tests for each intrinsic to ensure correct functionality. Old builtin functions and definitions have been removed in favor of the new framework.
