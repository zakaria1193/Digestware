The alignment of `std::meta::info` in C++ reflection has been corrected to match `alignof(void*)` instead of the previous incorrect value of 1. This change impacts the `alignof(decltype(^^::))` expression, ensuring consistent ABI behavior for reflection metadata, particularly for C++26 experimental features.
