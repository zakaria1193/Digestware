This commit enhances the `match.pd` patterns to merge consecutive `VEC_PERM_EXPR` operations when their combined inputs are limited to two sources, `a` and `b`. This optimization is particularly effective for scenarios where vectors are built element by element, reducing a chain of eleven instructions to a single `shufps` on x86-64. The change also resolves a spurious "variable used uninitialized" warning by correctly folding the permutation expressions.
