Jonathan Wakely discusses the policy on using LLM tools for bug fixes in GCC. He references the Linux kernel's policy and notes that the FSF and GCC steering committee are also working on policies. The draft GCC policy proposes *not* accepting AI-generated code, only allowing AI for assistance, idea generation, and testing, due to concerns about copyright and legal prerequisites for GCC contributions.
