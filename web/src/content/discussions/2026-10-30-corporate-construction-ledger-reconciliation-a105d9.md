- term: Corporate construction-ledger arrangement
  definition: A method of financing property development where construction costs
    are tracked and paid through a corporate accounting system rather than a traditional
    mortgage. This implies direct oversight and payment processing by a corporate
    entity and its accounting administrators.
- term: KPMG
  definition: A multinational professional services network, often involved in audit,
    tax, and advisory services. In this context, KPMG is requested to review and coordinate
    payments for construction invoices.
- term: Construction in Progress (CIP)
  definition: An accounting term representing the total cost of a construction project
    that has not yet been completed. It is a key component of the general ledger for
    tracking project expenditures.
- term: General Ledger Reconciliation
  definition: The process of comparing accounting records in the general ledger to
    supporting documents (e.g., invoices, bank statements) to ensure accuracy and
    identify discrepancies. This is crucial for financial reporting and control.

This discussion is a formal request for a review of accounting and project reconciliation for two residential properties under development in East Nashville. The request is directed to Lineage Construction, KPMG, and Baker Donelson, outlining a corporate construction-ledger arrangement instead of traditional mortgage financing. It details the accounting process and requests specific reconciliation categories and project reference information.
