Andrew Burgess proposes to remove the inclusion of `config.h` from `libctf/swap.h` to resolve duplicate definition warnings that occur during in-source builds of GDB. The issue arises because `libsframe/config.h` is included first, and then a subsequent build of `libctf` creates `libctf/config.h`, leading to conflicts. Removing the `libctf/swap.h` include eliminates these warnings.
