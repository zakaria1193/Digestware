The call for proposals is open for the AI Plumbers devroom at FOSDEM 2027, focusing on the essential plumbing of AI systems. Topics include AI compilers, edge AI, inference frameworks, and RISC-V based AI accelerators. GCC and GNU toolchain developers are encouraged to submit proposals by November 30th, 2026.
