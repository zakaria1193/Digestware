The GNU Compiler Collection version 13.5 has been released, serving as the final update for the GCC 13 branch. This release incorporates over 265 bug fixes, addressing regressions and critical issues found in GCC 13.4. Development and maintenance of GCC now continue on the GCC 14, 15, 16 branches, and the development trunk.
