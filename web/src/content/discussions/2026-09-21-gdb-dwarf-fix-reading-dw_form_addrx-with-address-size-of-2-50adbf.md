Simon Marchi submits a patch addressing a GDB crash encountered when processing DWARF debug information, specifically with `DW_FORM_addrx` entries and a 2-byte address size. The issue was found during an investigation of AVR binaries generated by clang. The crash occurred due to a heap-buffer-overflow in `bfd_getl64` when reading address indices, indicating an incorrect handling of address size when decoding this DWARF form.
