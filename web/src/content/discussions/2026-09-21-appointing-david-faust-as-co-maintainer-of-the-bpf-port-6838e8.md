Jose E. Marchesi is formally requesting the GCC Steering Committee to consider appointing David Faust as a co-maintainer for the BPF CPU port. Faust has already been actively involved as a reviewer and has contributed substantially to the port's development, making him a valuable asset for continued maintenance and growth.
