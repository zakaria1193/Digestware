Jose E. Marchesi has retired from the GCC Steering Committee, citing his age and diminishing knowledge of recent GCC developments. He expressed his gratitude to all contributors he has worked with over the years, calling it a great experience. His retirement was announced openly at the GNU Tools Cauldron, contrasting with the typical quiet departure of members.
