Florian Weimer responds to a suggestion by Paul Eggert to provide an example for the "Assisted-by" Git trailer in the glibc contribution guidelines when parts of a contribution have been auto-generated. The discussion centers around whether to mention specific LLMs like Claude Opus or stick to a more general reference to Autoconf. Florian suggests mentioning Autoconf to avoid dependence on specific, potentially non-open LLMs.
