Claudio Bantaloukas announces updates to the GCC forge, including the archival of closed pull requests from the forge to the gcc-patches mailing list.  New joiners no longer have to be /allowed. The organization's teams in forgejo have also been updated, defining roles such as Collaborators, Write.After.Approval, Reviewers, and Maintainers.
