This patch addresses a build issue where recompiling libsframe/sframe.c after the initial build results in duplicate definition warnings (e.g., 'PACKAGE' redefined). The problem occurs because sframe.c includes config.h twice: once from libsframe/config.h (which exists after the initial configure) and again via libctf/swap.h which includes libctf/config.h. By removing the direct include of config.h in libsframe/sframe.c, the patch resolves the warning without affecting functionality.
