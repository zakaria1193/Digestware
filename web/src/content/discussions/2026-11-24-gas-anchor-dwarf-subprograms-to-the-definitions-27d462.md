This patch series aims to improve DWARF debug information generated by GAS by anchoring subprogram DIEs (Debug Information Entries) to their actual definitions. This addresses issues where tools like `addr2line` might fail to find function names or source lines due to symbol resolution problems with weak or COMDAT functions, which can lose their original symbols after linking. The series includes specific fixes for ARM Thumb and MIPS architectures to correctly handle ISA modes.
