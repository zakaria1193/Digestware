Jan Hubicka announces the GNU Tools Cauldron 2026, which will be held in Prague from October 2nd to 4th. The Call for Proposals is open until August 31st, and registration is open as well.  A Toolchains Track will also be held at the Linux Plumbers Conference in Prague, from October 5th to 7th; the CFP for that track closes July 24th.
