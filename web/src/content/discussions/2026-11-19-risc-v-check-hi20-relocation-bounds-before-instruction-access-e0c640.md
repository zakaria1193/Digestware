This RFC proposes adding a bounds check for HI20 instruction accesses in the RISC-V ELF backend. The check ensures that the complete instruction fits within the section before relaxation or final access occurs. It also suggests validating the deletion window in the relaxation code. The author is seeking feedback and guidance on the best way to integrate this check, potentially as a follow-up to a related bug.
