Martin Jambor announces the five contributors selected to work on GCC as part of Google Summer of Code 2026. The projects include extending C++ support in the static analyzer, adding infrastructure to compile the Rust 'alloc' crate and handle the 'Drop' trait, implementing Libgomp optimizations for scheduler-guided OpenMP execution in Cloud VMs, and enhancing OpenACC support in GCC. The community bonding period lasts until May 24th.
