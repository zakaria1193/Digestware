The test case `pr121649.c` assumed that the `long` type was always 64-bit.  This assumption is incorrect on some `int128` targets.  This commit replaces `long` with `long long` to ensure the test functions correctly on all platforms, specifically those with 128-bit integers where `long` may not be 64-bit.
