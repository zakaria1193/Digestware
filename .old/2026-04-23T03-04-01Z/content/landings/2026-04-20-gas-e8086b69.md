The assembler was incorrectly issuing errors when encountering local register symbols, due to a missing check for local symbols. This commit adds the missing check, preventing these spurious errors. The testcases have been updated to reflect the correction. Also, redundant "sname" has been removed, because "name" already contains the same information.
