This commit makes minor improvements to the narrow-gp-writes pass for AArch64 in GCC. It removes redundant checks for `SUBREG` and `TRUNCATE`, prevents infinite recursion when narrowing `DImode` operands, and uses `HOST_WIDE_INT_PRINT_HEX` for printing masks. These changes enhance the optimization of narrow data type writes on AArch64 architectures.
