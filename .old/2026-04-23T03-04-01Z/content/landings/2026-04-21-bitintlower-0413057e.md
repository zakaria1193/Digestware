The `bitintlower` pass optimizes bit-precise integer operations. This commit addresses an issue where padding bits were not correctly cleared after signed multiplication and division operations when `-fwrapv` is used, which specifies that signed overflow should wrap around. The change extends clearing of padding bits to cover these cases, ensuring correct behavior for bit-precise integers when overflow wrapping is enabled.
