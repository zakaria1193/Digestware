The C++ parser was not correctly parsing `splice-type-specifier` within `mem-initializer-id`. This commit rectifies this by adding the necessary parsing logic in `cp_parser_mem_initializer_id`. The change also defers typename diagnostics and introduces new tests (`g++.dg/reflect/splice13.C` and `g++.dg/reflect/splice14.C`) to verify the fix.
