The compiler was incorrectly tracking the active member of the anonymous union within std::optional during constant evaluation. When the active member's lifetime ended via a reset, the compiler failed to recognize this, leading to potential use of out-of-lifetime values. The fix ensures the union is correctly treated as no longer having an active member after destruction.
