This commit fixes an internal compiler error (ICE) in the Fortran front end that occurred during assignment operations involving allocatable components within hidden types. The fix involves converting the right-hand side of the assignment to the type of the left-hand side using a `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` when their main variants differ. This ensures that the compiler handles the assignment correctly, resolving the ICE.
