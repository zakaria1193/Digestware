This commit introduces a new linkage kind, ``lk_module``, to explicitly represent entities with module linkage. Previously, module linkage was determined by checking ``lk_external && DECL_MODULE_ATTACH_P && !DECL_MODULE_EXPORT_P``. The addition of ``lk_module`` simplifies these checks and allows for further cleanup in the codebase. This change clarifies the representation of module linkage and paves the way for future improvements.
