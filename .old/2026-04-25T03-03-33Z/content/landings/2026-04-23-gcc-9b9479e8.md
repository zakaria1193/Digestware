The ieee.exp testsuite was inheriting flags from DEFAULT_CFLAGS, which sometimes caused spurious failures due to the ``-ansi -pedantic-errors`` flags it included. This commit introduces a new variable, DEFAULT_IEEE_CFLAGS, independent of DEFAULT_CFLAGS, to provide default flags specifically for IEEE floating-point tests. This ensures consistent and reliable test execution, as target-specific flags are now handled correctly without interference from DEFAULT_CFLAGS.
