An internal compiler error (ICE) occurred in the AArch64 backend when folding `svld1rq` (replicated quadword load) intrinsics with `-msve-vector-bits=128`. The compiler incorrectly handled the resulting identity permutation, leading to a failed assertion. This commit fixes the issue by emitting a `VIEW_CONVERT_EXPR` instead of a `VEC_PERM_EXPR` when the SVE vector length equals the quadword width, preventing the ICE.
