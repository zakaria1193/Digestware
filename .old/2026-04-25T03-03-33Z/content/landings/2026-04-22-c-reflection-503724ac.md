An access check was inadvertently skipped during the template substitution of reflection splices (``&[:R:]``), leading to compilation failures due to access control violations. This commit ensures that access checking is consistently turned off during the `build_offset_ref` call in `tsubst_splice_expr`, resolving the issue and allowing the correct compilation of reflection-based code.
