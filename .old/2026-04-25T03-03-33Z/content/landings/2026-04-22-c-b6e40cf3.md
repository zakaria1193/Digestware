When loading a function from a module, the compiler was unnecessarily re-evaluating constant expressions, leading to issues when the element constructor hadn't been cloned yet.  This change prevents the compiler from attempting to fold `TARGET_EXPR` too early, specifically before `cp_genericize_init_expr` has a chance to process it.  This resolves PR124973, PR120502, and PR120005.
