The `mdspan` deduction guide in libstdc++ now accepts the `data_handle_type` parameter by value instead of by reference, consistent with the corresponding constructor. This resolves LWG4511, ensuring that the deduction guide behaves as expected when deducing the template arguments of `mdspan`. A new test case was added to verify the corrected behavior.
