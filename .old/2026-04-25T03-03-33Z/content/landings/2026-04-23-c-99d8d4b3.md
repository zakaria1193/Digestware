A typo in the logic for checking `ff_genericize` in `cp_fold_r` was causing incorrect behavior in consteval, array, and module-related code. This commit fixes the typo, ensuring that the intended check (`ff_genericize` instead of `!ff_only_non_odr`) is performed. The fix is accompanied by the addition of `-O` to a test case to properly trigger the relevant optimization.
