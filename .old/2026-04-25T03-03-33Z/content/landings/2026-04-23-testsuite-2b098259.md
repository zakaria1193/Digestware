The testsuite's assembler options check in `gcc.misc-tests/options.exp` was failing when GCC was configured with an assembler whose name didn't end in 'as'. The fix modifies the test to use newline-sensitive matching and to check for the actual configured assembler name instead of assuming it ends in 'as'. This ensures that the testsuite functions correctly when a specific assembler is configured.
