The RISC-V backend now optimizes bit masking operations on shifted values.  When masking off bits on both the upper and lower parts of a register, the compiler uses a shift triplet (shift left, shift right, shift left) depending on the mask. If the input is already left-shifted and the shift count corresponds to the low mask bits, the compiler uses only two shifts.  This reduces the number of instructions, improving performance.
