This commit prevents the use of `views::iota` on an `iota_view`, resolving LWG4096. Applying `views::iota` to an existing `iota_view` would result in creating a copy of the `iota_view`, which is ill-formed because `iota_view` is not weakly incrementable. This change ensures that such usage is rejected at compile time, preventing unexpected behavior.
