The C compiler now correctly handles recursive struct/union redeclarations when qualifiers are involved. Previously, the compiler incorrectly rejected valid recursive redeclarations because the consistency check was performed before the variants were fully completed. The fix ensures that the consistency check is done after completing the variants, allowing the compiler to accept valid code.
