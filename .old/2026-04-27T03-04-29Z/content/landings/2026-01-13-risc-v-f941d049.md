This commit adds new tests for the RISC-V vector extension (RVV) to verify the combination of `vec_duplicate` and `vmsle.vv` instructions into `vmsle.vx`. The tests include assembly dump checks. The combination's effectiveness depends on the cost of transferring data from general-purpose registers to vector registers (GR2VR). The tests cover GR2VR costs of 0, 1, and 15.
