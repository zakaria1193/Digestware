This commit simplifies SLP pattern handling by ensuring that the related statement of new SLP pattern statements is the original statement of a possible pattern. It removes the setting of `STMT_VINFO_REDUC_DEF` on pattern statements, since these are only checked on original scalar statements. It fixes building of complex and addsub patterns in `tree-vect-slp-patterns.cc`.
