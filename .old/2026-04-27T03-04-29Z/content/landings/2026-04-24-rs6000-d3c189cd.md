This commit prevents premature folding of expressions during template parsing in C++ for the rs6000 architecture, addressing PR124133.  The issue arises when `targetm.resolve_overloaded_builtin` is called with non-dependent types, leading to potential ICEs due to uninstantiated trees with type mismatches. By disabling folding in the arguments during this phase and using `convert` and `build2` instead of `fold_convert` and `fold_build2`, the compiler avoids these ICEs and ensures correct template instantiation.
