This commit restores a check for the validity of `std::get` when using `elements_view`, resolving LWG3797. The check was unintentionally dropped during a previous update, leading to potential errors when instantiating `elements_view` with move-only iterators. This fix ensures that the constraints on `std::get` are enforced, preventing hard errors during iterator category computation.
