Thomas Koenig argues that concerns about LLM-generated code copying copyrighted material are less relevant for GCC patches. He reasons that non-trivial patches must integrate with GCC's existing data structures and algorithms, effectively becoming covered by GCC's GPL license. Florian Weimer agrees this argument has merit and extends his point to the practice of submitting code on behalf of others.
