The commit fixes the availability of `struct sock_fprog` in libsanitizer for non-glibc Linux systems.  The struct is provided by the linux headers (`#include <linux/filter.h>`). The fix adds the missing include, making `struct sock_fprog` available to all Linux systems, thus preventing build failures.
