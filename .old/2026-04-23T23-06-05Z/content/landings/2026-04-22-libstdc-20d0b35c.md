The `mdspan` deduction guide in the C++ standard library now accepts the `data_handle_type` by value, instead of by reference. This change aligns the deduction guide's behavior with the corresponding constructor, resolving LWG4511. A new test case, `testsuite/23_containers/mdspan/mdspan.cc`, has been added to verify the fix.
