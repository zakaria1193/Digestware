The libstdc++ now includes platform-specific implementations of atomic wait and wake functions for Darwin (macOS). This leverages kernel support available since macOS 10.12 (Sierra). The implementation supports both 32-bit and 64-bit quantities, with 64-bit support enabled when building for macOS 10.15 (Catalina) or later.
