The commit defines `__platform_wait_t` as `unsigned int` for OpenBSD and DragonFly. `std::semaphore` will then use `unsigned int` by default. The change anticipates the use of OpenBSD's `futex(2)` or DragonFly's `umtx(2)` syscalls for more efficient wait/notify operations.
