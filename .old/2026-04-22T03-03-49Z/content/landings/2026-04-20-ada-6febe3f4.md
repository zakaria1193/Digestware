This commit fixes a link failure that occurred when building GNAT (the GNU Ada compiler) tools on older Linux versions. The issue was caused by missing libraries in the linking process. The commit restores the necessary `TOOLS1_LIBS` to the Makefile, ensuring that `gnatmake` and `gnatlink` are linked correctly.
