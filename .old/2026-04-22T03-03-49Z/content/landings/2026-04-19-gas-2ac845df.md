The assembler's `operand` function in `expr.c` now checks for single quotes extending past the end of a line or statement. This prevents a buffer overrun in the `check_Scc_OszcOperations` function when processing specially crafted input. The patch also corrects a parsing error related to MRI mode hex floats.
