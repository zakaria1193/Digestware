The commit addresses an ICE that occurred when the compiler attempted to create a `VOID_TYPE TARGET_EXPR` during the processing of expansion statements. The fix involves taking the cv_unqualified type of the `TARGET_EXPR` before extracting its `TARGET_EXPR_INITIAL`, ensuring that the recreated `TARGET_EXPRs` have the correct cv-unqualified types. A new test case has been added to verify the fix.
