The `__cpp_impl_reflection` and `__cpp_lib_reflection` feature test macros are bumped from 202506L to 202603L to indicate the implementation of P3795R2, except for certain metafunctions.  The change updates the compiler's built-in definitions and the libstdc++ version definitions. Test cases are updated to reflect these changes.
