The libstdc++ codebase now includes `_GLIBCXX_RESOLVE_LIB_DEFECTS` comments to indicate where specific issues (identified by their LWG number) have been resolved in the implementation. This commit adds these comments for several pre-C++26 features that were resolved during the 2026 Croydon meeting.
