The `labs` function call in `ctf-link.c` within the libctf library was causing a compiler warning on 32-bit hosts due to a signed/unsigned comparison. The commit removes the `labs` call and casts the `inputs` variable instead.  A style/formatting fix was also included, reporting "too many inputs" using %lu.
