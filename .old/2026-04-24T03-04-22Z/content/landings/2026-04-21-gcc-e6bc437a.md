Typo corrections in the test cases ``gcc.dg/hoist-register-pressure-1.c``, ``gcc.dg/hoist-register-pressure-2.c``, and ``gcc.dg/hoist-register-pressure-3.c`` exposed failures for the cris-elf architecture.
