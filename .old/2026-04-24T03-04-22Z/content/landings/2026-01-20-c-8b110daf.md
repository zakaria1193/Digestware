The C++ frontend gains a new linkage kind, `lk_module`, to represent entities with module linkage. Previously, this was determined by checking `lk_external && DECL_MODULE_ATTACH_P && !DECL_MODULE_EXPORT_P`. The new `lk_module` enum simplifies these checks, and unifies the representation of module linkage.
