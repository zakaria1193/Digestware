A sanity check has been added to the `z80_elf_16_be_reloc` function in `elf32-z80.c` to ensure that the relocation offset is within the valid range. This prevents potential out-of-range writes, which could be detected by AddressSanitizer (ASan). This improves the robustness of the Z80 ELF handling in binutils.
