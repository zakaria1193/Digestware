This commit removes a redundant `CALL_P` check in the RISC-V backend's `vxrm_unknown_p` function. The check was made unnecessary by the use of `reg_set_p` to verify the VXRM definition. VXRM (Vector Rounding Mode) is marked as call-used in `riscv.h`, so `reg_set_p` always returns true when a call is encountered, rendering the `CALL_P` check redundant.
