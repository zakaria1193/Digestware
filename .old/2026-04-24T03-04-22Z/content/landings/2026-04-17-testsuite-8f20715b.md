The testsuite has been adjusted to accommodate targets that use short enums.  The `match-shift-cmp-3` test now produces different output on these targets because the fold optimization does not apply to sizes smaller than a word. The expected results have been updated to reflect this difference until the optimization can be expanded to support shorter input values.
