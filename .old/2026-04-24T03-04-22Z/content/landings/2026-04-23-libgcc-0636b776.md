This commit fixes an issue where `LDFLAGS_FOR_TARGET` was not being honored on Windows when building the shared `libgcc`. This inconsistency with other targets has been resolved by adding `$(LDFLAGS)` to `SHLIB_LINK` in `libgcc/config/i386/t-slibgcc-cygming`, ensuring that the specified linker flags are now used.
