Binutils proposes deprecating the 32-bit s390 target ('s390-*') and 'elf32-s390' format in the upcoming 2.47 release, with eventual removal in a future release. This change aligns with actions taken by the Linux kernel (which removed 32-bit s390 compatibility in 6.19), Glibc (deprecated in 2.43, to be removed in 2.44), and GCC (deprecating in 16). The proposal aims to streamline toolchain maintenance by removing support for an increasingly obsolete architecture, but may impact users still relying on 32-bit s390, emphasizing the need for a coordinated deprecation strategy across the toolchain.
