Nick Desaulniers announced the Call for Papers for the Toolchains Track at Linux Plumbers Conference 2026, which aims to address toolchain issues relevant to the Linux kernel. The conference will take place in Prague, Czechia, from October 5th to October 7th, 2026. Proposals should focus on discussions and problem-solving rather than research presentations. Submissions are open until July 24, 2026.
