GCC has added support for the Spacemit-A100 RISC-V processor core. This update enables developers to use `-mcpu=spacemit-a100` and `-mtune=spacemit-a100` for targeting this specific core. The A100 is an in-order, dual-issue core; its microarchitecture costs are currently aligned with the Spacemit X60. Support includes a new extension `xsmtvdotii`, a vector length of 1024 (`zvl1024b`), and notably, the exclusion of the H (hypervisor) extension, while being similar ISA-wise to the X100. A new test case verifies this core support.
