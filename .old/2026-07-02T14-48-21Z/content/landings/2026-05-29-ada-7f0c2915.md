This commit fixes an error where a duplicated external tag was reported when performing a structural generic instantiation. The issue arose because the compiler was registering tagged types during the analysis of the instantiation, even though the tag's name was unlikely to conflict with user-declared tags. The fix temporarily disables this registration in `Build_Structural_Instantiation`, preventing the spurious error and streamlining the instantiation process.
