The Fortran compiler now provides more detailed diagnostic messages for errors encountered during namelist reads. These enhanced messages include the exact line number, column position, filename, and the problematic portion of the input line, significantly improving the clarity and usefulness of error reporting for Fortran developers. This change directly addresses a long-standing issue (PR118793) to improve developer experience.
