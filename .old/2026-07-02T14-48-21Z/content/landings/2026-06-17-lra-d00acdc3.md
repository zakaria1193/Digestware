This commit resolves an ICE and a latent bug on aarch64 by improving how the Local Register Allocator (LRA) handles sub-register accesses. Previously, non-representable sub-registers of hard registers were not handled correctly, leading to incorrect memory access or allocation failures. The fix ensures that these accesses are reloaded through memory, mirroring how pseudos are handled, thereby preventing both compiler crashes and incorrect runtime behavior.
