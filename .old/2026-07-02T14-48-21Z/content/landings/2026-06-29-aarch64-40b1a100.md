The GCC compiler for AArch64 now optimizes widening signed multiplications by powers of two. Instead of performing a multiplication and then a sign extension, the compiler rewrites the operation to widen the multiplicand before the multiplication. This rewritten form can be directly translated to the AArch64 SBFIZ instruction, which performs a sign-extended widening of a multiply. This optimization is valid because overflow in the original signed multiplication is considered undefined behavior.
