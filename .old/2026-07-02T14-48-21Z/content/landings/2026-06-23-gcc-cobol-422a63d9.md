The GCC COBOL frontend now handles the IBM PROCESS directive with more flexibility, changing its behavior when used with the `-dialect ibm` flag. Previously, placing this directive before the `IDENTIFICATION DIVISION` resulted in an error, but it is now downgraded to a warning. This change, controlled by the new `-Wibm-cdf` option, aligns the compiler's behavior with common practices in certain IBM COBOL environments and fixes a reported bug.
