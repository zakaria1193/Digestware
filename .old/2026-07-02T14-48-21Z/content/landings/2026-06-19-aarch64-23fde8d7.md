The GCC AArch64 backend was updated to properly handle `vec_deconstruct` in its `vector_costs::count_ops` function. A recent change introduced `vec_deconstruct`, which was not accounted for in the AArch64 instruction cost model, leading to a bootstrap failure during compiler compilation. This update modifies `aarch64_vector_costs::count_ops` to treat `vec_deconstruct` similarly to `vec_construct`, resolving the issue.
