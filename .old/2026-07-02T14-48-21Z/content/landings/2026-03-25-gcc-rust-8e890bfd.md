This commit renames `ImmutableNameResolutionContext` to `FinalizedNameResolutionContext` in the Rust front-end, clarifying that this context is, in fact, mutable during later compilation stages. Concurrently, a new `map_usage` method has been introduced to enable mapping usages to their leaf-most definitions, which is essential for accurately resolving names throughout the compiler's backend and type-checkers.
