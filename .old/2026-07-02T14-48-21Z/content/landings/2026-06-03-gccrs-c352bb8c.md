This commit renames the `lookup_temp_var` function to `lookup_label_temp_var` within the GCC Rust compiler's expression compilation unit. The change clarifies the function's purpose, as it is exclusively used in the context of labels. This refactoring improves code readability and maintainability by making the function's specialized role explicit in its name.
