This commit refactors the resolution of Ada 2022 `declare` expressions to streamline the handling of transient scopes. Previously, a manual name resolution approach was used, which evolved to include local visibility and then scope management with a workaround for transient scopes. This change simplifies the process by correctly managing these transient scopes, which are not true scopes but placeholders that can interact with regular scopes. Additionally, it addresses a loophole in `Find_Type_Of_Object` uncovered during this analysis in the preanalysis case.
