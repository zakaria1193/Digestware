GCC has removed support for the AMX-TF32 instruction set extension. This change is due to the removal of AMX-TRANSPOSE from Intel's Instruction Set Extensions (ISE). The removal affects various parts of the compiler, including CPU feature detection, option handling, intrinsics, and documentation. It also includes the removal of corresponding test cases.
