The Advanced Matrix Extensions (AMX) TF32 instruction set is now disabled by default in binutils. AMX-TF32 was depublished and will not appear on any Intel or AMD hardware. While support is maintained for potential vendor utilization, it is not enabled for general use. Test cases have been updated to reflect this change.
