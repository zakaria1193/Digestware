This commit fixes a regression in GCC's Pointer Thread Analysis (PTA) where the DSE (Dead Store Elimination) pass incorrectly removed calls to `memcpy`. This occurred when the destination of `memcpy` was derived from a conditional dynamic allocation (like `malloc`) and the result was ultimately returned from the function. The fix ensures that the analysis correctly tracks escaped heap variables in such conditional cases.
