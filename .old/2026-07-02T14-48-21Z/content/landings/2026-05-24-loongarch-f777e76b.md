This commit introduces a new instruction pattern, `usdot_prod`, to the LoongArch architecture support in GCC. This pattern is designed to efficiently compute the dot product of vectors, specifically handling cases where one vector is sign-extended and the other is zero-extended. This optimization is crucial for performance-sensitive applications like `SPEC 2026 stockfish_r`, enabling the compiler to leverage specialized hardware instructions for significant speedups.
