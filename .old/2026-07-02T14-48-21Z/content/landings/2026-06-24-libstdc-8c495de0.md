This commit enables the `__gthread_yield` call within libstdc++-v3 for VxWorks targets. Previously, the `ac_has_sched_yield` check incorrectly prevented its usage, even though VxWorks environments support the underlying `gthread_yield` functionality, which is distinct from POSIX `sched_yield()`. This change improves the standard library's thread-concurrency mechanisms on VxWorks.
