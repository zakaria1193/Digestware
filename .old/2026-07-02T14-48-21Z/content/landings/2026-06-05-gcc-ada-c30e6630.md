A long-standing bug in GCC's Ada compiler regarding static accessibility checks for allocators has been resolved. The issue stemmed from the compiler failing to correctly account for subtype constraints when dealing with discriminants, which are special components of Ada types. This fix ensures that allocators are consistently checked against the precise constraints of the subtype indication, leading to more robust and correct compilation.
