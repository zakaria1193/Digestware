Previously, certain elaboration warning messages from `Check_Internal_Call_Continue` were always displayed, regardless of whether the `-gnatwl` flag was set. This fix adjusts the warning characters to ensure these messages are now properly conditional. Additionally, the `Output_Calls` function has been refactored to handle various warning characters consistently, leading to more predictable warning behavior for Ada developers.
