This commit refactors the GCC Rust front end's name resolution system by properly separating the APIs related to namespaces, name flattening, and resolved nodes. Functionality such as `find_leaf_definition_inner` and `NameResolutionContext::flatten` have been moved to more appropriate locations, ensuring a clearer distinction between responsibilities and improving file organization for better maintainability.
