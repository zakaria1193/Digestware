-gc-section support has been re-enabled for hppa*64*-*-hpux*.  The linker now allocates 16 bytes at the start of the .data section and remaps relocations with invalid offsets to this area. This resolves issues with the HP dynamic linker and allows unused sections to be garbage collected.
