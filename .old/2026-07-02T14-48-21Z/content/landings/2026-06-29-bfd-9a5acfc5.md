This commit removes outdated 32-bit-only configuration options for Solaris from both the BFD library and the linker. Following the removal of older Solaris versions (prior to Solaris 10/11) from the build system, these specific 32-bit configurations are no longer applicable and are therefore cleaned up to simplify the codebase.
