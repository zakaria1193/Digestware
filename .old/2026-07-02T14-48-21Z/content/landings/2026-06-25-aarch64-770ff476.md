This commit ports AArch64 NEON SIMD intrinsics for vector creation, including `vcreate`, `vcombine`, `vdup_n`, and `vmov_n`, to GCC's pragma-based framework. By consolidating these operations under the pragma system, the compiler's internal structure for handling NEON intrinsics becomes more streamlined and maintainable. Obsolete definitions and internal functions related to these intrinsics have been removed.
