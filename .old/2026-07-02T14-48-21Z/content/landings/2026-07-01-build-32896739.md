The HAVE_GNU_LD macro, which was primarily used to indicate linker support for GNU-style response files, has been removed. This commit replaces its single remaining use in `collect2.cc` and `gcc/ada/link.c` with a new configure test, `HAVE_LD_AT_FILE`. This simplifies the build system by removing a seldom-used macro.
