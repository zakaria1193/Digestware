A signed integer overflow vulnerability in the `d_compact_number` function within libiberty has been fixed. The issue occurred during arithmetic operations related to C++ name demangling. By changing the addition to use unsigned arithmetic (`+ 1u`), the overflow is prevented, ensuring the stability and security of the C++ demangling process.
