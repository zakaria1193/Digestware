This update to the COBOL frontend adjusts the lexer to give keywords higher priority than user-defined names, resolving a parsing ambiguity where keywords could be mistakenly interpreted as identifiers. Additionally, the compiler now enforces that `Program-ID` must be a unique user-defined name, preventing the use of COBOL keywords in this context. These changes fix a long-standing bug and improve the robustness and correctness of the COBOL parser.
