An assertion failure in GCC's Ada compiler has been resolved, which previously occurred when processing library procedures that had an empty declaration list. The compiler sometimes misused the declaration list of an ancestor subprogram body, even when the point of reference was within the statement list. This fix prevents compiler crashes and ensures proper hoisting of code, even when assertions are disabled.
