This patch refactors the AVR assembler's configuration files, `tc-avr.c` and `tc-avr.h`, to enforce GNU coding style by using tabs for indentation. Previously, these files contained a mix of tabs and spaces, which could lead to inconsistent formatting. The change is purely cosmetic and does not affect the functionality or behavior of the AVR assembler.
