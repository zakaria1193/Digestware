This commit enhances GCC's S390 backend to properly handle non-compare conditions within intermediate RTL (Register Transfer Language) expressions during the costing phase of constant propagation (cprop). Previously, such conditional expressions, like `r108={(0x1)?r113:r109}`, could cause issues in `s390_rtx_costs` because the costing logic did not anticipate them before they were fully folded. This fix ensures accurate cost calculation and improves the robustness of the S390 backend.
