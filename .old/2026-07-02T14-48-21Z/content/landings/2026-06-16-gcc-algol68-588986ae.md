A logic error in the Algol68 frontend of GCC, affecting the deduplication of "in-MOIF" modes, has been corrected. This fix addresses a subtle bug where the type-equivalencing mechanism incorrectly handled modes embedded within MOIF objects. The changes involve refactoring `a68_open_packet` to relocate extract replacement code and modifying `a68_replace_equivalent_mode` to correctly retrieve and check MOIF extracts, ensuring that equivalent modes are properly identified and deduplicated. Additionally, a new sanity check was added to `a68_lower_moids` to verify that all known modes have an associated C type.
