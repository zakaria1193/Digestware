This commit enhances the AVR backend's debugging capabilities by introducing a new format specifier, `%N`, to the `avr_xdump` family of functions. This format allows for brief and recursive printing of tree nodes, specifically `DECL_P` and `TYPE_P` nodes, without excessive verbosity. This feature simplifies the inspection of complex data structures within the compiler, such as nested pointer and array types, by providing a more concise and readable output, particularly useful when `-mlog=insert_attributes` is enabled.
