The GCC test suite now includes a new function, `remove_lto_sections`, specifically designed for Darwin platforms. This function identifies and skips sections prefixed with `.section __GNU_LTO` used by Darwin to signify Link-Time Optimization sections. This change ensures that the test suite accurately processes assembly output by ignoring LTO-specific sections, which is crucial for correct analysis during testing.
