This patch corrects an issue on the s390 architecture where certain 'extract-vector-zero' instructions were not generating the correct bitmasks. These instructions are intended to perform a left shift followed by masking out bits, with vacated bits being zeroed. However, the underlying RISBG instruction performs a left rotate, where bits shifted out are wrapped around. The incorrect bitmasks in the compiler's patterns led to the rotate behavior influencing the result, as detected by the `late_combine` optimization phase. The fix adjusts the bitmasks in the instruction patterns to accurately reflect the desired zeroing behavior.
