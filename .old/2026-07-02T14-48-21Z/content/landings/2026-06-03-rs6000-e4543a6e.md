This commit adds a new PowerPC-specific builtin, `__builtin_ppc_atomic_cas_local`, to GCC. This function is similar to the generic `__atomic_compare_exchange` but utilizes the `larx` (load-and-reserve) instruction with a specific hint (EH value of 1). This hint signals that a subsequent store operation is likely to occur, allowing the processor to optimize cache coherence for reduced lock contention and improved performance on PowerPC architectures.
