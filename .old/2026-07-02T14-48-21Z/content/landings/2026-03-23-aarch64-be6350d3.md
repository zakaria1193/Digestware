GCC's aarch64 backend renames files and namespaces related to Scalable Vector Extensions (SVE) built-ins to 'acle' (ARM C Language Extensions). This change broadens the scope, as these definitions will now also be used for NEON built-ins, avoiding confusion and better reflecting their general applicability to ARM's C language extensions rather than SVE exclusively. The rename affects configuration files and C++ source files, ensuring consistency across the compiler.
