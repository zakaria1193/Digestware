This commit fixes a long-standing bug in the `_mcount` function within libgcc, which has prevented proper profiling on 32-bit Solaris/x86 systems since 2016. The fix ensures that `_mcount` explicitly saves call-clobbered registers and correctly passes `selfpc` and `frompcindex` arguments to `internal_mcount` via the stack, as required by the Solaris ABI. This resolves previous profiling failures and allows binutils gprof tests to pass on the affected platform.
