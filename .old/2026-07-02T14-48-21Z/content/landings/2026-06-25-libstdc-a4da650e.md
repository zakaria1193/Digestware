Libstdc++ has updated numerous macros in its `acinclude.m4` file, replacing the deprecated `AC_LANG_SAVE`, `AC_LANG_C`, `AC_LANG_CPLUSPLUS`, and `AC_LANG_RESTORE` with the modern `AC_LANG_PUSH` and `AC_LANG_POP`. This change improves how the `libstdc++` build system manages language-specific configurations within Autoconf, ensuring better compatibility and adherence to current Autoconf practices. The refactoring affects many checks related to compiler features and system capabilities, streamlining the configuration process.
