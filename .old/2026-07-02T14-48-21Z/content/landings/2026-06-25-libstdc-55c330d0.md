This update enhances GCC's libstdc++ by making `bitset::reference` a full-fledged range proxy, aligning its behavior with `vector<bool>::reference` and other proxy types. The change involves backporting a const-assignment operator to C++23, which helps range concepts recognize proxy iterators, and backporting `swap` overloads to C++11 to ensure `ranges::swap` and `ranges::iter_swap` work as expected. This harmonization improves the consistency and usability of these proxy types within the C++ standard library.
