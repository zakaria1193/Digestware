`std::random_device` has been updated to utilize Hygon's RDRAND and RDSEED hardware random number generator instructions, bringing Hygon C86 platforms in line with Intel and AMD. This change adds the Hygon vendor signature to the allowlists for these instructions, ensuring that `std::random_device` defaults to using the faster RDRAND instruction on Hygon CPUs while retaining full access to RDSEED for higher-entropy seeding needs.
