The x86 backend of Binutils' GNU Assembler (GAS) now performs an optimization for `XCHG` (exchange) instructions where both source and destination operands are the same register. Under optimization, `xchg %rN, %rN` will be transformed into `mov %rN, %rN`. This change significantly reduces execution resource usage, as `MOV` is a much simpler operation than `XCHG`, leading to more efficient and potentially faster generated code.
