This commit modifies the GCC Rust front-end so that `extern crate self` correctly resolves to the crate currently being compiled. Previously, such declarations might have been misidentified or caused issues, but this change ensures that this specific `extern crate` syntax correctly references the local compilation unit. This also adds a `visited_crates` member to `DefaultResolver` for recursion protection, preventing infinite loops during resolution.
