The GCC testsuite has been updated to address issues with Mach-O ABI on Darwin, specifically for top-level assembly tests. Modern Darwin no longer supports non-Position Independent Executables (PIE), leading to linker warnings and test failures. This update skips such cases and corrects Mach-O specific assembly syntax in several tests, improving the reliability of the testsuite on Apple platforms.
