This commit resolves a C++ compiler bug (PR125408) related to lambda captures, specifically when dealing with folding and lvalueness checking. Previously, a `VAR_DECL` within an assert could incorrectly trigger a failure after `mark_rvalue_use` because the `rvalue_p` condition was misapplied. The fix adjusts the logic in `mark_use` to precisely identify cases where an rvalue needs to be converted to an lvalue, addressing the underlying issue of distinguishing between an expression's rvalue status and its need for lvalue adjustment.
