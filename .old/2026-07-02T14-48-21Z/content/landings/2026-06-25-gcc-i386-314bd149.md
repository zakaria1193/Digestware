This commit restricts AVX-VNNI-INT8 instructions, specifically `vpdpb{ss,su,uu}d[s]`, to their VEX encoding on i386 targets that support AVX-VNNI-INT8 but not AVX10.2. Previously, under register pressure, the compiler could erroneously generate the EVEX form of these instructions, which is only available with AVX10.2. By adding a VEX-only alternative and gating the EVEX alternative on AVX10.2, GCC prevents the generation of invalid instructions and ensures correctness on targets like `znver6`.
