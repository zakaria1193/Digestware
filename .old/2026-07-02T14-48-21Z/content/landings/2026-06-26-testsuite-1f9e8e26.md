The `atomic-builtins-1` test verifies that `__int128` OpenMP atomic operations fall back to GOMP functions when hardware support is absent. This commit correctly skips the test on targets that natively support 128-bit atomic operations, preventing false negatives. The `check_effective_target_sync_int_128` logic was updated to accurately reflect native support.
