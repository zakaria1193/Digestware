This update to GCC's instruction recognizer, known as 'recog,' enables it to properly handle dependent filters. These filters represent register constraints that depend on other operands within the same instruction. The system now evaluates these dynamic constraints during the instruction verification phase, specifically after static register filters have been checked, which improves the accuracy of instruction selection and correctness of generated code.
