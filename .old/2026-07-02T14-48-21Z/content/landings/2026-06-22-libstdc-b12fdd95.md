This change removes a dependency on `std::atomic<unsigned>` in `tzdb.cc`, which previously caused compilation issues on targets lacking atomic word operations, such as Cortex-M0. It replaces the atomic variable with a custom struct that conditionally uses `std::atomic_ref` or a mutex-protected access for leap second counts, ensuring broader target compatibility.
