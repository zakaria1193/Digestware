This commit resolves an internal compiler error (ICE) in the C++ reflection mechanism that occurred in `get_reflection` when processing `INDIRECT_REF` expressions. Previously, `INDIRECT_REF` was not always stripped, leading to an assertion failure within `outer_var_p`. The fix ensures `STRIP_REFERENCE_REF` is applied early in `get_reflection`, preventing the ICE and improving compiler stability for reflection-heavy code.
