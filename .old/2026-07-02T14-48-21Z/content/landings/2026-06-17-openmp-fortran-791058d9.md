The GCC Fortran frontend has enhanced its diagnostic reporting for OpenMP `declare reduction` directives. Previously, developers encountering issues with these directives might receive unspecific or cryptic error messages. This update refines the messages generated by `match_udr_expr`, `gfc_match_omp_declare_reduction`, and `gfc_resolve_omp_udr`, providing clearer and more precise feedback when a `declare reduction` is malformed or used incorrectly. This improvement directly benefits developers by reducing the time needed to identify and correct errors in their OpenMP parallelization efforts.
