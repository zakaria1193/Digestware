This change modifies the `Is_OK_Modifies_Context` function in `sem_util.adb` to permit `volatile` objects within SPARK's `Modifies` clauses without requiring additional guards. This update aligns the language's behavior with the expectation that volatile objects, which can change unexpectedly, should be allowed in such clauses to accurately reflect their potential for modification.
