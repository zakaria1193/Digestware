This commit fixes a bug in the Algol 68 front end where the sorting of union alternatives incorrectly relied on pointer comparisons for pack element names. This led to incorrect ordering when compiling certain Algol 68 code. The change ensures proper comparison of pack element names by their actual values, ensuring consistent and correct program behavior.
