This commit resolves a build issue on Darwin platforms for GCC's AArch64 backend by replacing `LONG_LONG_MAX` with `LLONG_MAX` in `aarch64-builtins.cc`. A recent change introduced the non-standard `LONG_LONG_MAX`, which is not guaranteed by `limits.h`, causing compilation failures on Darwin. Switching to `LLONG_MAX` ensures compliance with language standards and restores proper build functionality.
