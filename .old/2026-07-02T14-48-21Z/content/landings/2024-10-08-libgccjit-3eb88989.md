This update to libgccjit modifies `jit_langhook_type_for_mode` in the dummy frontend to correctly recognize and process new internal compiler types. Previously, `jit_langhook_type_for_mode` might fail to map these newer types to their corresponding JIT-compatible representations, potentially limiting the ability to compile certain constructs. Handling these missing types improves the robustness and feature coverage of libgccjit, allowing it to work with a broader range of GCC's internal type system.
