This commit addresses a code generation issue in the x86 backend by replacing `gen_rtx_SUBREG` with `gen_lowpart` when constructing `E_HImode` RTX. Specifically, this change applies to situations where a `(subreg:V2QI (reg:HI ...))` expression has a `CONST_INT` source. The use of `gen_lowpart` now correctly extracts the lower part of the register, ensuring proper handling of vector constants and resolving a bug that led to incorrect assembly output.
