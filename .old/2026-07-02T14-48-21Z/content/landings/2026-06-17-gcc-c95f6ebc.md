This commit resolves a bug where GCC, at optimization levels -O2 and higher, incorrectly optimized array manipulations when arrays were declared in an outer subprogram and accessed from a nested one. The issue arose because `DECL_NONADDRESSABLE_P` was sometimes incorrectly set for aggregate types that *could* have addressable subcomponents, misleading alias analysis. The fix ensures that `DECL_NONADDRESSABLE_P` is cleared for aggregate types in `lookup_field_for_decl`, preventing erroneous optimizations and maintaining correct aliasing behavior.
