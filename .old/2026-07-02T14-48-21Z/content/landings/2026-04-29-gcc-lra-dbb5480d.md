This change enhances the Local Register Allocator (LRA) to incorporate dependent filters. These filters represent register constraints that are dynamic, depending on the definition or usage of other operands within an instruction. The LRA now tracks and queries these filters during its pseudo phases and uses a hash-table cache to manage their potentially expensive evaluation, ensuring correctness and setting the stage for improved register allocation.
