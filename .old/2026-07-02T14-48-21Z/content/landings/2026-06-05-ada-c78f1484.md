This commit enhances the SPARK subset of Ada by allowing the `Modifies` contract clause to be specified as a pragma. Previously, `Modifies` primarily existed as an aspect. This change aligns its usage with other SPARK contract clauses like `Global` and `Depends`, which can already be expressed as pragmas, providing developers with more flexibility in defining program contracts.
