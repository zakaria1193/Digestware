The GCC C frontend was updated to properly manage the `.ACCESS_WITH_SIZE` wrapper when processing expressions involving unary operators (`!`, `+`, `-`) and pointers with the `__counted_by__` attribute. Previously, these combinations could trigger a compiler assertion, causing compilation to fail. GCC now unwraps the `.ACCESS_WITH_SIZE` call before further processing, resolving the issue without affecting bounds checking.
