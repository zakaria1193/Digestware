The i386 backend in GCC has updated its vector cost model to correctly handle the `vec_deconstruct` operation. Previously, the `vec_to_scalar` operation was expected in contexts such as emulated gather/scatter, elementwise accesses, and strided accesses. This change reflects a shift in how vector operations are represented internally, ensuring the cost model aligns with current internal compiler representations for better optimization decisions.
