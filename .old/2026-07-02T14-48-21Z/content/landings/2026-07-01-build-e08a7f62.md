This commit removes the `HAVE_GNU_AS` macro, simplifying GCC's assembler integration. It replaces specific uses of this macro with more general mechanisms like `ASM_V_SPEC`, which handles assembler command-line options. The changes affect various platforms, including Darwin, Solaris, AIX, and NVPTX, by adjusting their assembler-specific configurations and documentation.
