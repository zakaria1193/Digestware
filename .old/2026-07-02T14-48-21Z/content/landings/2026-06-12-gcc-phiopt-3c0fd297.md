A streamlined load factoring approach has been integrated into GCC's phi-optimization pass. This new mechanism focuses on factoring common loads within diamond-shaped control flow structures, ensuring that compiler optimizations are applied effectively without negatively impacting vectorization, which was a concern with prior, more aggressive factoring attempts. The change improves optimization in specific common code patterns.
