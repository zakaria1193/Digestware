This commit resolves a problem in C++ coroutines where guard variables for structured bindings were not persisting correctly across `co_await` operations. These guards were being destroyed prematurely because they were based on `TARGET_EXPR`, which has a limited scope. By changing to `get_temp_regvar`, which is more suitable for variables that need to live longer, the guards now correctly manage the lifetime of structured binding variables within coroutines. The guard variables are also explicitly declared to ensure visibility.
