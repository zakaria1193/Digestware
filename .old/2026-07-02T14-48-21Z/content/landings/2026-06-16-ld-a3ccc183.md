The Binutils linker test suite has been updated to mark the `pr29655` test as an expected failure (`xfail`) when running on SPARC platforms, including both Solaris and Linux. This change is a temporary measure in response to PR34151, which reports that the previously working `Build pr29655` test now fails on SPARC. Until the underlying issue is resolved, this adjustment prevents the test suite from reporting a regression by acknowledging the known failure.
