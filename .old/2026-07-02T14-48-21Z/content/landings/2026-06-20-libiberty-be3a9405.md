The `libiberty` subdirectory within Binutils has been synchronized with a recent commit from the GCC project. This update incorporates fixes for various typos, re-flows lines longer than 80 characters, regenerates some `autoconf` scripts, and addresses an exponential back-reference issue in the D language demangler. Importantly, a previous Binutils-specific patch related to `mmap` and sanitizer configuration checks was retained during this synchronization.
