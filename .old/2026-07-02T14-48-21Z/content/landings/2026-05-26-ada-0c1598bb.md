  links copies of these contracts back to their original definitions, guaranteeing
  that all necessary contextual information is available for correct and robust semantic
  analysis.

Previously, handling of class-wide pre- and postcondition expressions in GCC's Ada compiler could lead to a loss of original context. This meant that legality checks, particularly those involving the 'Old attribute, might fail to find the necessary contextual information. This change ensures that copies of these class-wide conditions are now attached to their corresponding pragmas, preserving the original context for all subsequent checks, leading to more robust contract validation.
