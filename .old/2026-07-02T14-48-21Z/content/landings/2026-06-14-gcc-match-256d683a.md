GCC's pattern matching in `match.pd` previously failed to account for truncation of the shifter operand in expressions like `(~x) >> (type)x`. This oversight could lead to miscompilations, particularly when the front-end introduced implicit casts. The compiler now explicitly rejects such patterns, ensuring that optimizations do not proceed with potentially undefined or incorrect behaviors. This change improves the robustness of GCC's optimization passes around bitwise operations and type conversions.
