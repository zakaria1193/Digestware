This commit updates Binutils to align how it handles conditional jump instructions (Jcc) in x86-64 mode with the existing handling for unconditional direct jumps (JMP) and calls (CALL). The change addresses architectural differences between AMD and Intel processors regarding instruction encoding and interpretation, improving ambiguity resolution outside of 64-bit mode. This ensures more consistent and correct assembly of conditional branches across different x86-64 platforms.
