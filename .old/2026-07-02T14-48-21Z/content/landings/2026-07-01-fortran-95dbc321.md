This commit resolves AddressSanitizer (ASan) problems encountered in Fortran's Parameterized Derived Type (PDT) testcases (PR121972). Changes include refining the logic in `expr.cc` to correctly identify PDT components and ensuring that `calloc` is used for types with parameterized components, similar to those with allocatable components. Additionally, the allocation of parameterized components for PDTs and class PDTs is consolidated, and allocatable components are nullified if necessary.
