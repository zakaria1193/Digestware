This commit fixes an issue in Libgomp where the `omp_default_device` environment variable was not correctly handled by the library's fallback code path. This oversight meant that on non-Linux systems, where the fallback mechanism is often used, programs might not correctly select the intended OpenMP device. The update ensures consistent and correct device selection across all supported platforms, particularly impacting systems that do not use Linux-specific device handling.
