  (like for character arrays in AVR-specific functions), it uses a safer method, preventing
  obscure crashes or unexpected behavior due to improper memory handling.

The GCC backend for AVR microcontrollers has been updated to replace direct `alloca` calls with `XALLOCAVEC` from the `libiberty` library. This change affects functions such as `avr_init_builtins`, `avr_cpu_cpp_builtins`, and `avr_log_set_avr_log`, which previously allocated temporary `char` arrays on the stack. Utilizing `XALLOCAVEC` ensures robust, checked allocation for temporary buffers, improving safety and maintaining consistency with other parts of GCC.
