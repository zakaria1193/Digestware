  estimate the cost of the original scalar loop and the vectorized loop to determine
  if vectorization is beneficial. This commit fixes an accounting issue where the
  cost of scalar statements was incorrectly attributed to the loop prologue instead
  of the loop body, which could skew the cost analysis.

GCC's vectorizer now records the cost of scalar statements as part of the ``vect_body``, rather than the ``vect_prologue``. This ensures that ``scalar_costs->body_cost ()`` returns a non-zero value, leading to more accurate cost calculations during vectorization.
