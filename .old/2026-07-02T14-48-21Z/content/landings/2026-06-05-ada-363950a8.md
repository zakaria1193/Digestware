Diagnostic IDs have been added to error messages for specific Ada restrictions, including `No_Specification_Of_Aspect`, `No_Use_Of_Attribute`, and `No_Use_Of_Pragma`. This enhancement makes it easier for developers to identify and understand the nature of restriction violations reported by the compiler.
