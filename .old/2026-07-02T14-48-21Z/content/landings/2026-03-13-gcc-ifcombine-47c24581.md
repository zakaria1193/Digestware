This commit makes two key functions in the ifcombine optimization pass, `recognize_if_then_else` and `tree_ssa_ifcombine_bb`, publicly accessible. Previously, these were marked as static, limiting their use to within `tree-ssa-ifcombine.cc`. By removing the static keyword and adding a new header file, these functions can now be invoked by other GCC passes that might benefit from their logic for combining conditional statements.
