A safety check has been added to the `vect_set_loop_condition_normal` function within GCC's tree-vectorization component. This ensures that the `loop_vinfo` structure is not null before its members are accessed. Dereferencing a null pointer could lead to a crash or undefined behavior. This fix prevents such issues by adding a conditional check.
