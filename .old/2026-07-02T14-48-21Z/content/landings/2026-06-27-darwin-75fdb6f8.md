This commit updates the Darwin backend to better handle NeXT-style metadata in object files, addressing PR124260. It allows metadata sections to reside within `const_data`, making them more flexible. Additionally, `ClassList` symbols are now explicitly made linker-visible, ensuring they are properly recognized. Expanded comments clarify the meaning of flags within `ImageInfo` metadata.
