Libstdc++ introduces a new `iconv_input` concept within `src/c++20/format.cc` to address the legacy `const char**` signature for `iconv` used in older systems and SUSv2. This concept dynamically determines whether to cast input pointers to `char**` or `const char**` when calling `iconv`, extending compatibility for the C++20 formatting library. This change allows `libstdc++` to correctly interact with different `iconv` implementations without breaking on systems with the older signature.
