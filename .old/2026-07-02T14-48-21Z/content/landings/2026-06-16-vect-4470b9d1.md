This commit corrects a flaw in GCC's vectorizer where it failed to appropriately track the `supported_offset_vectype` during gather and scatter discovery. The vectorizer would identify a larger offset type for necessary scaling but not communicate this information back, leading to missed vectorization opportunities. The fix ensures that `supported_offset_vectype` is properly set, and also optimizes the costing of scatter and gather instructions by skipping redundant conversions, thereby enhancing the efficiency of vectorized code, particularly on architectures like RISC-V.
