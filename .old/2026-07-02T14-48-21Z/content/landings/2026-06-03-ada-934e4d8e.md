The `Build_And_Insert_Type_Attr_Subp` procedure in GCC's Ada front end, responsible for inserting type attribute subprograms, has undergone a refactoring. A new helper function, `Find_Insertion_Point_For_Ancestor`, extracts part of its statement sequence, making the code easier to understand and reason about object mutations. This internal change improves the compiler's maintainability without altering user-visible behavior.
