This commit enhances the compiler's optimization capabilities by adding new simplification rules for common bitwise and logical operations. It specifically targets patterns that combine a sign bit check with a magnitude check. The compiler can now simplify expressions like `((signed)A >= 0) & ((A & INT_MAX) != 0)` directly to `(signed)A > 0`, reducing redundant operations and improving code efficiency.
