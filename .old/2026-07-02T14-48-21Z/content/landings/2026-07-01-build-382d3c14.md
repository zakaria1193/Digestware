Following the removal of `HAVE_GNU_AS` and the `--with-gnu-as` option, this commit removes the `$gas` variable from GCC's build system. Several configurations in `config.gcc` have been updated to use `as_flavor` or have unconditional settings for the assembler. Checks in `configure.ac` that previously required gas can now be performed unconditionally.
