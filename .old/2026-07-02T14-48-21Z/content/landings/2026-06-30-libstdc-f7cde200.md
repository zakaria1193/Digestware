This commit fixes an issue in libstdc++'s timezone database parsing where accumulated wall-time savings were not properly considered when determining rule transitions. Previously, each rule was evaluated in isolation, leading to incorrect offsets and time calculations, particularly for zones like 'Europe/Paris' around 1945. The updated algorithm now collects three critical rule transitions around a given time (`curr_tran`, `prev_tran`, `next_tran`) and adjusts their start times based on preceding rules' saves. This ensures that the correct active rule is identified, matching the behavior of standard tools like `zic` and `libc`.
