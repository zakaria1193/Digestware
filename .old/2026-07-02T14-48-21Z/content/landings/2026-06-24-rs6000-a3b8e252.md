This commit adds preliminary support for future VSX (Vector Scalar eXtension) vector integer arithmetic instructions for PowerPC processors within the `rs6000` backend. New VSX patterns for vector add, subtract, multiply, and multiply-high operations are introduced, guarded by the `TARGET_FUTURE` flag. A new builtin enablement stanza, `future-vsx`, ensures these builtins are only active when both `-mcpu=future` and `-mvsx` are specified, anticipating new architectural features and allowing for future compiler optimizations and performance gains.
