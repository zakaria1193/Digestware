This commit introduces a new linkage kind, `lk_module`, to explicitly represent the linkage of entities within C++ modules. Previously, this was determined by checking multiple flags. The new linkage kind aims to simplify linkage checks and pave the way for further cleanups in the C++ front end, particularly related to reflection.
