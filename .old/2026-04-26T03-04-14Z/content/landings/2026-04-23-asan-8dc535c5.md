A range check has been added to the z80_elf_16_be_reloc function in bfd/elf32-z80.c. This check ensures that the relocation offset is within the valid range, preventing potential out-of-bounds writes when AddressSanitizer (ASan) is enabled. This prevents crashes during dynamic linking.
