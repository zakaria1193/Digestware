The pipeline checker in the RISC-V backend was incorrectly reporting missing reservations for "ghost" instructions, leading to confusion. This commit filters out default reservations, specifically "ghost", from the checker's output. This change improves the accuracy and clarity of the pipeline checker, which is used for performance analysis and scheduling.
