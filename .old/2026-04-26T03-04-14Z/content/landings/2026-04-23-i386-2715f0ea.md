This commit increases the `STACK_CHECK_PROTECT` value in `config/i386/cygming.h` for 64-bit Windows. This adjustment allows `-fstack-check` to properly recover from stack overflow conditions on specific Windows Server 2025 configurations, preventing potential crashes or undefined behavior.
