A bug in GCC's C++ module implementation could cause member emission failures for pointer-to-members. Specifically, if a specialization was instantiated in a module, the member pointed to might not be marked as used in the importing translation unit, leading to a linker error.  This commit ensures that the member is marked as used during stream-in, resolving the linking issue.
