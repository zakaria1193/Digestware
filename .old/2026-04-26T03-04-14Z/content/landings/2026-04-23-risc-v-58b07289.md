The expansion for signed and unsigned min/max operations on si3 (32-bit integers) in the RISC-V backend was missing the `SUBREG_PROMOTED` annotation.  This caused GCC to emit a redundant sign-extension instruction.  Adding the annotation prevents the unnecessary `sext.w` instruction, improving code generation for these operations.
