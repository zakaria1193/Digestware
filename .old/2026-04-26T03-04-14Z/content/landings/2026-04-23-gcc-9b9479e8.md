The `ieee.exp` testsuite now uses a dedicated `DEFAULT_IEEE_CFLAGS` variable, independent of `DEFAULT_CFLAGS`. This prevents unintended flag inheritance from `DEFAULT_CFLAGS`, which could cause spurious test failures. A default of `-w -fno-inline` is included in the new variable. Target-specific flags are handled separately to accommodate variations like the inclusion of `-ffloat-store` depending on the architecture.
